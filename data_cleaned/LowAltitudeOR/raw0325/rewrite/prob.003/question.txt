The municipal law enforcement agency is looking to establish a UAV-based aerial surveillance system for routine monitoring of critical zones across the city.
Two potential sites have been identified for constructing drone hangars (Depots), and there are 4 designated observation points (Targets) requiring mandatory flyover inspection.
**Operational Guidelines:**
1. **Facility Siting Decision:** Determine which depot locations to activate. Opening each depot entails a one-time fixed infrastructure cost.
2. **Flight Path Planning:** Deploy UAVs from the activated depots. Each drone departs from its assigned depot, conducts sequential visits to a subset of target locations, and **must return to its originating depot** upon completing its circuit.
3. **Restrictions:**
   - Each target location must be inspected by exactly one UAV visit.
   - The cumulative flight distance along any single UAV route must remain within the drone's maximum battery endurance ($L$).

**Parameters:**
- **UAV Max Range ($L$):** 30.0 km
- **Travel Cost:** $1.0 per km

**Locations (Coordinates in km):**
*Candidate Depots:*
- Depot A (ID: 1): (0, 0)   | Fixed Cost: 100
- Depot B (ID: 2): (10, 0)  | Fixed Cost: 120 (More expensive land)

*Target Points:*
- T1 (ID: 3): (0, 5)
- T2 (ID: 4): (2, 5)
- T3 (ID: 5): (8, 5)
- T4 (ID: 6): (10, 5)

**Goal:**
Minimize the Total Cost = (Sum of Fixed Costs of Open Depots) + (Total Flight Distance Cost).
