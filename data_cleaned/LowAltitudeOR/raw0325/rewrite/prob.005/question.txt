The Regional Disaster Management Bureau is tasked with architecting a UAV-enabled humanitarian supply chain network in anticipation of future seismic events.
The planning methodology consists of a two-stage stochastic framework:
   - **Stage 1 (Pre-disaster Preparedness):** Make strategic decisions on the placement of relief distribution centers, the volume of supplies to stockpile at each facility, the allocation of available drones to specific facilities, and the pre-planned visitation routes from facilities to potential impact zones.
   - **Stage 2 (Post-disaster Operations):** Once the earthquake materializes and actual damage levels become known, determine the precise quantities of emergency supplies (such as blood products and pharmaceuticals) to dispatch to each affected area.

**Uncertainty:**
The demand at disaster sites is uncertain. We consider two potential scenarios:
- **Scenario 1 (Moderate):** Limited damage, lower demand.
- **Scenario 2 (Severe):** Widespread damage, higher demand.
The goal is to minimize the total cost (Fixed Opening + Inventory + Path Management + Expected Post-disaster Costs including Penalties).

**Parameters:**
- **Drone Capacity ($L_{MAX}$):** 15 units per trip.
- **Drone Battery Range ($B_{MAX}$):** 50 km.
- **Facility Inventory Capacity ($W_l$):** 100 units.
- **Drone Fleet ($D$):** 2 Drones (D1, D2).

**Locations & Coordinates:**
*Candidate Facilities ($K$):*
- Site A (ID: 1): (0, 0) | Opening Cost: 500
- Site B (ID: 2): (10, 0) | Opening Cost: 600

*Disaster Demand Sites ($I$):*
- Zone 1 (ID: 1): (0, 5)

**Scenario Data (Demand $D_{i}^{\xi}$):**

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

**Costs:**
- Inventory Prepositioning ($c_{INV}$): $2 / unit
- Path Management ($c_{MANA}$): $10 / link

- Unused Inventory Penalty ($c_{UNU}$): $5 / unit
