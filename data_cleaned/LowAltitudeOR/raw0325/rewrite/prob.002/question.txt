An emergency response organization, AeroRelief Emergency Logistics, intends to set up a network of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) dispatch centers across a region that is frequently affected by natural calamities.
The decision-making framework is structured in two distinct phases to account for the inherent unpredictability of disaster events:
- **Phase 1 (Strategic Planning):** Determine which hub sites to construct prior to any disaster occurrence. Establishing each hub carries a predetermined fixed construction expense.
- **Phase 2 (Tactical Response):** Following the onset of a specific disaster and the revelation of actual supply requirements, UAVs are dispatched from the pre-built hubs to fulfill demand at affected locations. This generates variable operational expenditures proportional to both the shipping volume and the travel distance involved.

The overarching objective is to identify an optimal subset of candidate hubs for construction such that the combined total of **Fixed Construction Expenditure** and the **Worst-Case Operational Expenditure** over all plausible disaster scenarios is minimized (Min-Max Robust Optimization).

**Candidate Hubs**
There are 3 candidate locations for hubs. Each has a maximum service radius of **10 km**.
The unit transportation cost is **$1.0 per unit per km**.

| Hub ID | Construction Cost ($) | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| H1 | 5,000 | Highland, moderate cost |
| H2 | 4,000 | Plains, low cost |
| H3 | 7,000 | City center, high cost |

**Demand Points and Scenarios**
There are 4 demand points (C1-C4). The demand volume depends on the disaster scenario.
Two scenarios are considered:
1.  **Scenario A (Normal Season):** Low, scattered demand.
2.  **Scenario B (Severe Flood):** High demand, concentrated in specific areas.

**Constraints & Requirements**
1.  **Mandatory Coverage:** In any realized scenario, every demand point must be assigned to an **active** (built) hub that is within the 10km service range.
2.  **Objective:** Minimize [Total Construction Cost + Max(Scenario A Op Cost, Scenario B Op Cost)].
    * *Operational Cost = Sum of (Demand * Distance * 1.0)* for the assigned links in that scenario.
