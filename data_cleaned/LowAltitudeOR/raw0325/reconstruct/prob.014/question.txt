MetroAir Authority is designing a Skyport network for a square metropolitan region (10km x 10km).
**Goal:** Select Skyport locations to minimize the Total System Cost, which includes:
   1. **Travel Time Cost:** Value of passengers' time (Air is faster than Ground).
   2. **Facility Cost:** Fixed cost to open and operate Skyports.
   3. **Collision Risk Cost:** A penalty for midair traffic congestion, specifically where air routes intersect.

**The Congestion Problem:**
- Air routes are direct lines between Skyports.
- If Route A (e.g., Bottom-Left to Top-Right) and Route B (e.g., Top-Left to Bottom-Right) are both active, they intersect at the center.
- **Risk Cost** = $C_{risk} \times (\text{Flow on Route A}) \times (\text{Flow on Route B})$.
- This quadratic penalty discourages "crossing the streams" unless the time savings are massive.

**Data - Candidate Nodes (Corners):**
- **0 (SW):** (0, 0)
- **1 (NE):** (10, 10)
- **2 (NW):** (0, 10)
- **3 (SE):** (10, 0)
- *Note:* All nodes are candidates. Fixed Cost = $500 per port.

**Data - Transport Demand (Pax/hr):**
- **D1 (Diagonal 1):** 0 $\to$ 1. Vol: 100.
- **D2 (Diagonal 2):** 2 $\to$ 3. Vol: 100. (Intersects D1!)
- **D3 (Vertical):** 0 $\to$ 2. Vol: 50. (No intersection risk).

**Parameters:**
- **Speeds:** Ground = 0.5 km/min, Air = 2.0 km/min.
- **Value of Time:** $1.0 / min.
- **Risk Unit Cost:** $0.1 per (pax $\times$ pax).
- **Access/Egress:** Passengers always use the nearest open Skyport.
