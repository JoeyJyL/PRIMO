### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if Skyport $ i $ is open, for all $ i \in F $.
- $ x_{mn} \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if demand pair $ (m, n) $ uses Air Taxi, for all $ (m, n) \in D $.

The original ground-mode variable $ z_{mn} $ is **eliminated** via substitution: since $ x_{mn} + z_{mn} = 1 $, we have $ z_{mn} = 1 - x_{mn} $ and substitute directly into the objective.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize total system cost: facility cost + travel time cost + collision risk cost.

**With variable elimination ($ z_{mn} = 1 - x_{mn} $):**

$$
\min Z = \underbrace{\sum_{i \in F} c_f \, y_i}_{\text{Facility}} + \underbrace{c_t \sum_{(m,n) \in D} \left[ \tau^a_{mn} \, x_{mn} + \tau^g_{mn} \, (1 - x_{mn}) \right]}_{\text{Travel Time}} + \underbrace{C_{risk} \cdot V_1 \cdot V_2 \cdot w_{D1,D2}}_{\text{Risk}}
$$

The travel time term simplifies to a constant plus a linear term in $ x $:

$$
c_t \sum_{(m,n)} \tau^g_{mn} + c_t \sum_{(m,n)} (\tau^a_{mn} - \tau^g_{mn}) \, x_{mn}
$$

**Product Linearization for Risk Term:**

The risk term involves the product $ x_{D1} \cdot x_{D2} $ (both diagonal routes active). We introduce an auxiliary binary variable $ w_{D1,D2} $ and linearize using **McCormick envelope**:

$$
w_{D1,D2} \le x_{D1}
$$
$$
w_{D1,D2} \le x_{D2}
$$
$$
w_{D1,D2} \ge x_{D1} + x_{D2} - 1
$$
$$
w_{D1,D2} \ge 0
$$

The risk cost becomes linear: $ C_{risk} \cdot V_1 \cdot V_2 \cdot w_{D1,D2} $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Skyport Availability Constraint:**

Air Taxi can only be selected for demand $ (m, n) $ if the nearest Skyports for both origin and destination are open.

For each $ (m, n) \in D $:

$$
x_{mn} \le y_{closest(m)}
$$

$$
x_{mn} \le y_{closest(n)}
$$

2. **McCormick Linearization Constraints (for risk product):**

$$
w_{D1,D2} \le x_{D1}, \quad w_{D1,D2} \le x_{D2}
$$
$$
w_{D1,D2} \ge x_{D1} + x_{D2} - 1
$$

3. **Binary and Domain Constraints:**

$$
y_i, x_{mn}, w_{D1,D2} \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in F $.
- $ x_{mn} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ (m, n) \in D $.
- $ w_{D1,D2} \in \{0, 1\} $: linearization variable for $ x_{D1} \cdot x_{D2} $.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min Z = \sum_{i} c_f \, y_i + c_t \sum_{(m,n)} \tau^g_{mn} + c_t \sum_{(m,n)} (\tau^a_{mn} - \tau^g_{mn}) \, x_{mn} + C_{risk} \cdot V_1 \cdot V_2 \cdot w_{D1,D2}
$$

**Constraints:**

$$
x_{mn} \le y_{closest(m)}, \quad x_{mn} \le y_{closest(n)} \quad \forall\, (m,n) \in D
$$

$$
w_{D1,D2} \le x_{D1}, \quad w_{D1,D2} \le x_{D2}, \quad w_{D1,D2} \ge x_{D1} + x_{D2} - 1
$$

$$
y_i, x_{mn}, w_{D1,D2} \in \{0, 1\}
$$
