### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

- $ y_j \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if vertiport $ j $ is open, for all $ j \in J $.
- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if zone $ i $ is assigned to vertiport $ j $, for all $ i \in I, j \in J $.

The original explicit load variable $ L_j $ is **eliminated** via direct substitution: since $ L_j = \sum_{i \in I} D_i x_{ij} $, we substitute this expression directly into the quadratic congestion term of the objective.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize total cost: fixed costs, access costs, and quadratic congestion costs.

With $ L_j $ eliminated, the objective becomes:

$$
\min Z = \sum_{j \in J} F_j \, y_j + \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} D_i \, C_{ij} \, x_{ij} + \sum_{j \in J} \alpha \left( \sum_{i \in I} D_i \, x_{ij} \right)^2
$$

Where:

- $ F_j $ is the fixed cost of vertiport $ j $.
- $ D_i $ is the demand of zone $ i $, $ C_{ij} $ is the per-passenger access cost.
- $ \alpha = 0.05 $ is the congestion coefficient.

The quadratic term expands to:

$$
\alpha \sum_{j \in J} \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{k \in I} D_i \, D_k \, x_{ij} \, x_{kj}
$$

This is a **convex quadratic** objective (sum of squares of linear expressions), solvable natively by MIQP solvers.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Single Assignment Constraint:**

Each zone must be assigned to exactly one vertiport.

For each $ i \in I $:

$$
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} = 1
$$

2. **Aggregated Capacity Linking (replaces individual $x_{ij} \le y_j$):**

Instead of one constraint per (zone, vertiport) pair, we use a **single aggregate constraint** per vertiport. The total number of zones assigned to vertiport $ j $ cannot exceed $ |I| \cdot y_j $:

For each $ j \in J $:

$$
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \le |I| \cdot y_j
$$

When $ y_j = 0 $, all $ x_{ij} = 0 $. When $ y_j = 1 $, all assignments are feasible.

3. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad y_j \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**

- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in I, j \in J $.
- $ y_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in J $.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min Z = \sum_{j \in J} F_j \, y_j + \sum_{i,j} D_i \, C_{ij} \, x_{ij} + \alpha \sum_{j \in J} \left( \sum_{i \in I} D_i \, x_{ij} \right)^2
$$

**Constraints:**

$$
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall\, i \in I
$$

$$
\sum_{i \in I} x_{ij} \le |I| \cdot y_j \quad \forall\, j \in J
$$

$$
x_{ij}, y_j \in \{0, 1\}
$$
