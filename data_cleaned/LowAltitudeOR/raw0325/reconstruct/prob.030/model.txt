### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

**Sets:**
- $\mathcal{M}$: Set of aircraft, indexed by $m$.
- $\mathcal{N}$: Set of base stations, indexed by $n$.
- $\mathcal{T} = \{0, \ldots, T-1\}$: Set of time slots.
- $\mathcal{K} = \{0, \ldots, 7\}$: Set of 8 compass directions.
- $\mathcal{L}$: Set of discrete bandwidth levels.

**Parameters:**
- $(\Delta x_k, \Delta y_k)$: Step displacement for direction $k$.
- $\beta_l$: Bandwidth fraction for level $l \in \mathcal{L}$.
- $\text{canReset}(m,t,n,l)$: Precomputed binary indicator; equals 1 iff assigning aircraft $m$ at time $t$ to BS $n$ with bandwidth level $l$ achieves data rate $\geq \alpha_{\max}$.

**Decision Variables:**

- $d_{m,t,k} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable; equals 1 if aircraft $m$ at time $t$ chooses direction $k$.
- $x_m(t), y_m(t) \in [0, 500]$: Position of aircraft $m$ at time $t$.
- $\text{AoI}_m(t) \in [0, \text{AoI}_{\max}]$: Age of Information of aircraft $m$ at time $t$.
- $r_m(t) \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable; equals 1 if AoI resets at time $t$.

**Auxiliary Variables (Combined Assignment):**

- $a_{m,t,n,l} \in \{0, 1\}$: Combined assignment variable; equals 1 if aircraft $m$ at time $t$ is served by BS $n$ with bandwidth level $l$. This replaces the separate BS selection variable $s_{m,t,n}$, bandwidth level variable $b_{m,t,l}$, and their product linearization auxiliary $z_{m,t,n,l}$ from the original formulation.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

Maximize the combined remaining power and AoI freshness:

$$
\max \sum_{m \in \mathcal{M}} \left[ \frac{P^{\text{remain}}_m(T)}{P_{\max}} + \frac{\text{AoI}_{\max} - \overline{\text{AoI}}_m}{\text{AoI}_{\max}} \right]
$$

where $P^{\text{remain}}_m(T) = P^{\text{init}} - T \cdot P_{\text{slot}}$ and $\overline{\text{AoI}}_m = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=1}^{T} \text{AoI}_m(t)$.

Equivalently, minimize average AoI (since remaining power is constant):

$$
\min \sum_{m \in \mathcal{M}} \sum_{t=1}^{T} \text{AoI}_m(t)
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Direction Selection:**

$$
\sum_{k \in \mathcal{K}} d_{m,t,k} = 1 \quad \forall m, t
$$

2. **Position Dynamics:**

$$
x_m(t+1) = x_m(t) + \sum_{k} \Delta x_k \cdot d_{m,t,k}
$$
$$
y_m(t+1) = y_m(t) + \sum_{k} \Delta y_k \cdot d_{m,t,k}
$$

3. **Combined Assignment (Auxiliary Variable Constraint):**

Each aircraft at each time slot is assigned to exactly one (BS, bandwidth level) pair:

$$
\sum_{n \in \mathcal{N}} \sum_{l \in \mathcal{L}} a_{m,t,n,l} = 1 \quad \forall m, t
$$

4. **AoI Reset via Combined Assignment:**

The reset indicator is directly computed from the combined assignment variable without needing product linearization:

$$
r_m(t) = \sum_{n \in \mathcal{N}} \sum_{l \in \mathcal{L}} \text{canReset}(m,t,n,l) \cdot a_{m,t,n,l} \quad \forall m, t
$$

5. **AoI Dynamics (Linearized):**

$$
\text{AoI}_m(t+1) \leq \text{AoI}_{\max} \cdot (1 - r_m(t))
$$
$$
\text{AoI}_m(t+1) \geq \text{AoI}_m(t) + 1 - (\text{AoI}_{\max} + 1) \cdot r_m(t)
$$

6. **AoI Threshold:**

$$
\sum_{m} \text{AoI}_m(t) \leq \text{AoI}_{\max} \quad \forall t
$$

7. **Collision Avoidance (Big-M linearized):**

For each time $t$ and aircraft pair $m \neq \mu$, at least one of $|x_m - x_\mu| \geq D_{\text{col}}$ or $|y_m - y_\mu| \geq D_{\text{col}}$ must hold.

8. **Boundary Constraints:**

$$
0 \leq x_m(t) \leq 500, \quad 0 \leq y_m(t) \leq 500
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $d_{m,t,k}$: direction selection.
- $x_m(t), y_m(t)$: aircraft positions.
- $\text{AoI}_m(t)$: age of information.
- $r_m(t)$: AoI reset indicator.

**Auxiliary Variables:**
- $a_{m,t,n,l}$: combined (BS, bandwidth level) assignment. Replaces separate $s_{m,t,n}$, $b_{m,t,l}$, and their product linearization $z_{m,t,n,l}$.

**Objective:**
$$
\min \sum_{m,t} \text{AoI}_m(t)
$$

**Constraints:**
1. One direction per aircraft per slot.
2. Position dynamics.
3. Combined assignment: $\sum_{n,l} a_{m,t,n,l} = 1$.
4. AoI reset: $r_m(t) = \sum_{n,l} \text{canReset} \cdot a_{m,t,n,l}$.
5. AoI dynamics (linearized).
6. AoI threshold.
7. Collision avoidance.
8. Boundary constraints.

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Program)
2. Problem Scale: Medium (M=2 aircraft, N=4 BSs, T=10 slots, L=5 levels, ~400 binary + 22 continuous variables)
3. Solution Method: Exact MILP solver (Gurobi) with precomputed rate feasibility
