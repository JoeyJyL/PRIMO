## Sets and Indices

- $N = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$: all nodes ($0$ = start depot, $6$ = end depot, $1$-$5$ = customers)
- $C = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: all customers
- $C' = \{1, 2, 3, 4\}$: drone-eligible customers (customer 5 is truck-only)
- $N_0 = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: departure nodes
- $N^+ = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6\}$: arrival nodes
- $A = \{(i,j) : i \in N_0, j \in N^+, i \neq j\}$: truck arcs
- $P = \{(i,j,k) : i \in N_0, j \in C', k \in C, i \neq j, j \neq k, i \neq k, \sigma_{ij}+\sigma_{jk} \leq e\}$: feasible drone sorties

## Parameters

- $s_{ij}$: truck travel time from $i$ to $j$ (Manhattan distance / truck speed)
- $\sigma_{ij}$: drone travel time from $i$ to $j$ (Euclidean distance / drone speed)
- $e = 0.5$ h: drone flight endurance
- $s_L = 1/60$ h: drone launch time
- $s_R = 1/60$ h: drone recovery time
- $c = |C| = 5$: number of customers
- $M$: sufficiently large constant
- $\epsilon$: small positive constant for strict time ordering

## Decision Variables

- $x_{ij} \in \{0,1\}$: 1 if truck traverses arc $(i,j)$
- $y_{ijk} \in \{0,1\}$: 1 if drone launches from $i$, delivers to $j$, retrieves at $k$
- $t_j \geq 0$: truck arrival time at node $j$
- $t'_j \geq 0$: drone arrival time at node $j$
- $p_{ij} \in \{0,1\}$: 1 if truck visits customer $i$ before customer $j$ (ordering variable)
- $f_{ij} \geq 0$: single-commodity flow on truck arc $(i,j)$ (replaces MTZ position variables $u_i$)

## Objective

$$\min \; t_6$$

## Constraints

### (C1) Customer Service

$$\sum_{\substack{i \in N_0 \\ i \neq j}} x_{ij} + \sum_{(i,j,k) \in P} y_{ijk} = 1, \quad \forall j \in C$$

### (C2)–(C3) Truck Depot Departure and Return

$$\sum_{j \in N^+} x_{0j} = 1, \qquad \sum_{i \in N_0} x_{i,6} = 1$$

### (C4) Truck Flow Conservation

$$\sum_{\substack{i \in N_0 \\ i \neq j}} x_{ij} = \sum_{\substack{k \in N^+ \\ k \neq j}} x_{jk}, \quad \forall j \in C$$

### (C5) Single-Commodity Flow Subtour Elimination (replaces MTZ)

The MTZ position variables $u_i$ and constraints $u_i - u_j + 1 \leq (c+2)(1 - x_{ij})$ are replaced by continuous flow variables $f_{ij}$.

**Depot outflow equals number of truck-visited customers:**
$$\sum_{j \in N^+} f_{0j} = \sum_{j \in C} \sum_{\substack{i \in N_0 \\ i \neq j}} x_{ij}$$

**Each truck-visited customer absorbs one unit of flow:**
$$\sum_{\substack{i \in N_0 \\ i \neq j}} f_{ij} - \sum_{\substack{k \in N^+ \\ k \neq j}} f_{jk} = \sum_{\substack{i \in N_0 \\ i \neq j}} x_{ij}, \quad \forall j \in C$$

**Flow capacity linked to truck arcs:**
$$f_{ij} \leq c \cdot x_{ij}, \quad \forall (i,j) \in A$$

### (C6) Drone Launch Limit

$$\sum_{\substack{j \in C' \\ j \neq i}} \sum_{k} y_{ijk} \leq 1, \quad \forall i \in N_0$$

### (C7) Drone Retrieval Limit

$$\sum_{i} \sum_{j \in C'} y_{ijk} \leq 1, \quad \forall k \in C$$

### (C8) Truck Must Visit Launch and Retrieval Nodes

$$y_{ijk} \leq \sum_{\substack{l \in N_0 \\ l \neq k}} x_{lk}, \quad \forall (i,j,k) \in P$$

$$y_{ijk} \leq \sum_{\substack{h \in N_0 \\ h \neq i}} x_{hi}, \quad \forall (i,j,k) \in P,\; i \in C$$

### (C9) Time-Based Drone Sortie Ordering (replaces MTZ position-based ordering)

$$t_k \geq t_i + \epsilon - M(1 - y_{ijk}), \quad \forall (i,j,k) \in P,\; i \in C,\; k \in C$$

### (C10) Time Synchronization at Launch Node

$$t'_i \geq t_i - M\!\left(1 - \sum_{(i,j,k) \in P} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall i \in C$$
$$t'_i \leq t_i + M\!\left(1 - \sum_{(i,j,k) \in P} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall i \in C$$

### (C11) Time Synchronization at Retrieval Node

$$t'_k \geq t_k - M\!\left(1 - \sum_{(i,j,k) \in P} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall k \in C$$
$$t'_k \leq t_k + M\!\left(1 - \sum_{(i,j,k) \in P} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall k \in C$$

### (C12) Truck Travel Time Propagation

$$t_k \geq t_h + s_{hk} + s_L \cdot [\text{launch at } k] + s_R \cdot [\text{retrieval at } k] - M(1 - x_{hk}), \quad \forall (h,k) \in A$$

### (C13) Drone Travel: Launch Node to Customer

$$t'_j \geq t'_i + \sigma_{ij} - M(1 - y_{ijk}), \quad \forall (i,j,k) \in P$$

### (C14) Drone Travel: Customer to Retrieval Node

$$t'_k \geq t'_j + \sigma_{jk} + s_R - M(1 - y_{ijk}), \quad \forall (i,j,k) \in P$$

### (C15) Drone Endurance

$$\sigma_{ij} + \sigma_{jk} \leq e + M(1 - y_{ijk}), \quad \forall (i,j,k) \in P$$

### (C16) Non-Overlapping Sorties (consecutive sortie ordering)

The ordering variable $p_{ij}$ is retained for enforcing sortie precedence. When two sorties are active, the earlier-launching sortie must complete before the later one begins:

**Depot-first case ($i = 0$):**
$$t'_l \geq t'_k - M(2 - y_{ijk} - y_{lmn}), \quad \forall (i,j,k) \in P,\; (l,m,n) \in P,\; i=0,\; l \in C$$

**Customer-customer case:**
$$t'_l \geq t'_k - M(3 - y_{ijk} - y_{lmn} - p_{il}), \quad \forall (i,j,k),(l,m,n) \in P,\; i,l \in C,\; i \neq l$$

### (C17) Time-Based Ordering Variable Coupling (replaces MTZ position-based coupling)

Instead of linking $p_{ij}$ to MTZ position variables $u_i$ via $u_i - u_j \geq 1 - (c+2)p_{ij}$, we couple $p_{ij}$ to the truck arrival time variables $t$:

$$t_j \geq t_i + \epsilon - M(1 - p_{ij}), \quad \forall i \in C,\; j \in C,\; i \neq j$$

**Ordering symmetry:**
$$p_{ij} + p_{ji} = 1, \quad \forall i, j \in C,\; i < j$$

### (C18) Initial Conditions

$$t_0 = 0, \quad t'_0 = 0$$

## Key Modeling Difference

This formulation replaces the **Miller-Tucker-Zemlin (MTZ) subtour elimination** with **single-commodity flow (SCF) subtour elimination**, using continuous flow variables $f_{ij}$ instead of position variables $u_i$. The ordering variables $p_{ij}$ are retained for consecutive sortie ordering (C16) but are now coupled to truck arrival times $t$ rather than MTZ positions $u$ (C17).

2. Problem Size: Small
