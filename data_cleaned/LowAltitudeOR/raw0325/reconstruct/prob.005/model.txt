### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the two-stage stochastic drone relief network.

Let's denote:

- $ x_{k} $: a binary variable indicating whether facility $ k $ is opened (1) or not (0).
- $ y_{dk} $: a binary variable indicating whether drone $ d $ is assigned to facility $ k $ (1) or not (0).
- $ z_{idk} $: a binary variable indicating whether drone $ d $ (assigned to $ k $) is scheduled to visit site $ i $ (1) or not (0).
- $ v_{k} $: the inventory quantity prepositioned at facility $ k $.
- $ q_{idk}^{\xi} $: the items delivered by drone $ d $ from facility $ k $ to site $ i $ in scenario $ \xi $.
- $ u_{i}^{\xi} $: the unsatisfied demand at site $ i $ in scenario $ \xi $.
- $ r_{k}^{\xi} $: the unused inventory at facility $ k $ in scenario $ \xi $.

Note: The intermediate flow variable $ w_{ik}^{\xi} $ in the original formulation has been **eliminated by substitution**. All references to $ w_{ik}^{\xi} $ are replaced by $ \sum_{d \in D} q_{idk}^{\xi} $.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_{k}, y_{dk}, z_{idk} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in K, d \in D, i \in I $.
- $ v_{k} \geq 0 $ for all $ k \in K $.
- $ q_{idk}^{\xi}, u_{i}^{\xi}, r_{k}^{\xi} \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in I, k \in K, d \in D, \xi \in \Xi $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which includes the first-stage costs and the expected second-stage recourse costs.

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{k \in K} (C_{OPEN} x_k + C_{INV} v_k) + \sum_{i \in I}\sum_{d \in D}\sum_{k \in K} C_{MANA} z_{idk} + \sum_{\xi \in \Xi} p_{\xi} \left( \sum_{i,d,k} C_{DELI} q_{idk}^{\xi} + \sum_i C_{UNS} u_i^{\xi} + \sum_k C_{UNU} r_k^{\xi} \right)
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Assignment Logic Constraints (with Big-M Aggregation):**

A drone must be assigned to an open facility, each drone can be assigned to at most one facility.

For all $ d \in D, k \in K $:

$$
y_{dk} \leq x_k
$$

$$
\sum_{k \in K} y_{dk} \leq 1
$$

Instead of individual linking $ z_{idk} \leq y_{dk} $ for each site, we use an **aggregate Big-M constraint** per drone-facility pair:

For all $ d \in D, k \in K $:

$$
\sum_{i \in I} z_{idk} \leq |I| \cdot y_{dk}
$$

Where $ |I| $ is the number of demand sites (the Big-M value).

2. **Coverage Constraint:**

Each demand site $ i $ must be visited by at least one drone.

For each site $ i \in I $:

$$
\sum_{d \in D}\sum_{k \in K} z_{idk} \geq 1
$$

3. **Range Constraint:**

The total distance traveled by drone $ d $ from facility $ k $ must not exceed its maximum range.

For all $ d \in D, k \in K $:

$$
\sum_{i \in I} dist(k, i) \cdot z_{idk} \leq B_{MAX} \cdot y_{dk}
$$

4. **Inventory Capacity Constraint:**

For each facility $ k \in K $:

$$
v_k \leq W_{cap} \cdot x_k
$$

5. **Demand Balance Constraint (with $ w $ eliminated by substitution):**

For all $ i \in I, \xi \in \Xi $:

$$
\sum_{k \in K} \sum_{d \in D} q_{idk}^{\xi} + u_{i}^{\xi} = Demand_{i}^{\xi}
$$

6. **Supply Balance Constraint (with $ w $ eliminated by substitution):**

For all $ k \in K, \xi \in \Xi $:

$$
\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{d \in D} q_{idk}^{\xi} + r_{k}^{\xi} = v_k
$$

7. **Payload Limit Constraint:**

For all $ i \in I, d \in D, k \in K, \xi \in \Xi $:

$$
q_{idk}^{\xi} \leq L_{MAX} \cdot z_{idk}
$$
