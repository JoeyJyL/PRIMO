
RuralExpress Logistics is optimizing its last-mile delivery network in a rural region.
**System Architecture:**
- **Central Distribution Center (CDC):** The starting point for all parcels.
- **Trucks:** Heavy vehicles that can visit customers directly OR transport parcels to Micro-Depots.
- **Drones:** Light aerial vehicles that operate from *opened* Micro-Depots (MD) to serve nearby customers.
- **Micro-Depots (MD):** Potential sites where drones are stationed. Opening an MD incurs a fixed cost.

**Operational Rules:**
1. **Assignment:** Each customer must be served either by a **Truck** (direct from CDC routing) or by a **Drone** (from an open MD).
2. **Truck Route:** One truck is available. It departs CDC, visits all "Truck-assigned Customers" and all "Open MDs" (to drop off drone parcels), then returns to CDC.
3. **Drone Service:** Drones perform round-trips (Star-shaped) from their MD to the customer.
4. **Constraints:**
   - Drones have a max service range ($R_{max}$). Customers beyond this range from an MD cannot be served by that MD.
   - Truck capacity is assumed sufficient for this pilot.

**Parameters:**
- **Truck Cost:** $1.0 per km.
- **Drone Cost:** $0.1 per km (Very cheap per unit distance).
- **Drone Max Range ($R_{max}$):** 10 km (Radius).

**Locations & Data:**
*Central Depot (CDC):* (0, 0)

*Candidate Micro-Depots (MD):*
- MD1 (ID: 1): (10, 10) | Fixed Cost: 100
- MD2 (ID: 2): (20, 0)  | Fixed Cost: 250 (Expensive)

*Customers (C):*
- C1 (ID: 3): (12, 12) | Demand: 5  | Near MD1
- C2 (ID: 4): (8, 8)   | Demand: 5  | Near MD1
- C3 (ID: 5): (22, 2)  | Demand: 5  | Near MD2
- C4 (ID: 6): (0, 15)  | Demand: 5  | Far from all MDs (Must be Truck)

**Goal:**
Minimize Total Cost = (Fixed Cost of Open MDs) + (Truck Routing Cost) + (Drone Delivery Cost).
Determine which MDs to open, which customers are served by drones vs trucks, and the truck's route.
