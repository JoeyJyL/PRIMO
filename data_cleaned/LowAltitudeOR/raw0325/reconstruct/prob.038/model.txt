### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

We use the **Big-M method** to decompose the trip coverage into explicit pickup and drop-off coverage indicator variables, linked to station decisions via Big-M constraints:

- $ y_s \in \{0, 1\} $: whether station site $ s $ is opened.
- $ p_e \in \{0, 1\} $: whether the **pickup** of trip $ e $ is covered by at least one open station.
- $ d_e \in \{0, 1\} $: whether the **drop-off** of trip $ e $ is covered by at least one open station.
- $ z_e \in \{0, 1\} $: whether trip $ e $ is fully covered (both pickup and drop-off).

### Step 2: Pre-processing

Before building the model, we pre-compute coverage relationships using the Haversine distance formula:

$$
\text{dist}(i,j) = r \times \text{hav}^{-1}\left(\text{hav}(\gamma_j - \gamma_i) + \cos(\gamma_i) \cos(\gamma_j) \text{hav}(\lambda_j - \lambda_i)\right)
$$

where $ r = 3958.8 $ miles (Earth's radius).

We define:

- $ P_{es} = 1 $ if the pickup location of trip $ e $ is within $ R $ miles of station $ s $, 0 otherwise.
- $ Q_{es} = 1 $ if the drop-off location of trip $ e $ is within $ R $ miles of station $ s $, 0 otherwise.

### Step 3: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total fixed cost of opening stations:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{s \in S} F_s \cdot y_s
$$

### Step 4: Constraint Construction

1. **Pickup Coverage — Forward Linking:**

The pickup coverage indicator can be 1 only if at least one open station covers the pickup:

$$
p_e \leq \sum_{s \in S} P_{es} \cdot y_s \quad \forall e \in E
$$

2. **Pickup Coverage — Backward Linking (Big-M):**

If any open station covers the pickup, the indicator must be 1. Using Big-M constant $ M = |S| $:

$$
M \cdot p_e \geq \sum_{s \in S} P_{es} \cdot y_s \quad \forall e \in E
$$

3. **Drop-off Coverage — Forward Linking:**

$$
d_e \leq \sum_{s \in S} Q_{es} \cdot y_s \quad \forall e \in E
$$

4. **Drop-off Coverage — Backward Linking (Big-M):**

$$
M \cdot d_e \geq \sum_{s \in S} Q_{es} \cdot y_s \quad \forall e \in E
$$

5. **Trip Coverage — Logical AND Linearization:**

Trip $ e $ is covered if and only if both pickup and drop-off are covered:

$$
z_e \leq p_e \quad \forall e \in E
$$

$$
z_e \leq d_e \quad \forall e \in E
$$

$$
z_e \geq p_e + d_e - 1 \quad \forall e \in E
$$

6. **Minimum Demand Fulfillment:**

$$
\sum_{e \in E} z_e \geq \lceil CS \times |E| \rceil
$$

7. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
y_s \in \{0, 1\}, \quad p_e, d_e, z_e \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall s \in S, e \in E
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Sets:**

- $ S = \{S1, S2, \ldots, S10\} $: candidate station sites.
- $ E = \{T1, T2, \ldots, T20\} $: estimated air taxi trips.

**Parameters:**

- $ F_s $: fixed cost of opening station $ s $ (\$K/year).
- $ R = 1.0 $ mile: maximum on-road travel distance.
- $ CS = 0.70 $: minimum demand fulfillment rate.
- $ M = |S| = 10 $: Big-M constant.
- $ P_{es} \in \{0,1\} $: pickup coverage indicator (pre-computed).
- $ Q_{es} \in \{0,1\} $: drop-off coverage indicator (pre-computed).

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_s \in \{0, 1\} $: whether to open station $ s $.
- $ p_e \in \{0, 1\} $: whether pickup of trip $ e $ is covered.
- $ d_e \in \{0, 1\} $: whether drop-off of trip $ e $ is covered.
- $ z_e \in \{0, 1\} $: whether trip $ e $ is fully covered.

**Objective Function:**

Minimize:

$$
Z = \sum_{s \in S} F_s \cdot y_s
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Pickup forward linking:**
$$
p_e \leq \sum_{s \in S} P_{es} \cdot y_s \quad \forall e \in E
$$

2. **Pickup backward linking (Big-M):**
$$
M \cdot p_e \geq \sum_{s \in S} P_{es} \cdot y_s \quad \forall e \in E
$$

3. **Drop-off forward linking:**
$$
d_e \leq \sum_{s \in S} Q_{es} \cdot y_s \quad \forall e \in E
$$

4. **Drop-off backward linking (Big-M):**
$$
M \cdot d_e \geq \sum_{s \in S} Q_{es} \cdot y_s \quad \forall e \in E
$$

5. **Trip coverage AND linearization:**
$$
z_e \leq p_e, \quad z_e \leq d_e, \quad z_e \geq p_e + d_e - 1 \quad \forall e \in E
$$

6. **Minimum coverage rate:**
$$
\sum_{e \in E} z_e \geq \lceil CS \times |E| \rceil
$$

7. **Binary constraints:**
$$
y_s, p_e, d_e, z_e \in \{0, 1\}
$$

1. Optimization Problem Classification: ILP
2. Problem Scale: Small
