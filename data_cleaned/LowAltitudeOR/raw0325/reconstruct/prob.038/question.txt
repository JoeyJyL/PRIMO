SkyLift Aviation is planning to launch an air taxi (VTOL) service in a metropolitan area and needs to decide where to build vertistop/vertiport stations.
**Goal:** Select the **minimum number of station locations** from a set of candidate sites to **cover at least a target percentage of estimated air taxi demand**, subject to a maximum on-road travel distance constraint between passengers and their nearest station.

**Background:**
- Air taxi passengers need on-road first/last mile connections to reach a vertistop (pickup station) and from a vertistop (drop-off station).
- Each passenger trip has a pickup location and a drop-off location. A trip is "covered" only if **both** the pickup and drop-off are within the on-road travel limit of some open station.
- The company wants to minimize infrastructure investment by opening as few stations as possible while ensuring a minimum demand fulfillment (coverage) rate.
- Candidate station sites are pre-screened based on feasibility (open space, zoning, multimodal transport hubs, landmarks, airports).

**Operational Parameters:**
- Maximum on-road travel distance to nearest station: $R = 1.0$ mile
- Minimum demand fulfillment rate: $CS = 70\%$ (at least 70% of eligible trips must be covered)
- Air taxi speed: 170 mph (used for demand estimation, not for location model)
