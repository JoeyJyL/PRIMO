### Step 1: Sets and Parameters

**Sets:**

- $ V = \{0, 1, \ldots, 8, 9\} $: all nodes (0 = start depot, 1–8 = customers, 9 = return depot).
- $ C = \{1, \ldots, 8\} $: customer nodes.
- $ C_0 = \{0\} \cup C $: nodes from which truck/drone can depart.
- $ C^+ = C \cup \{9\} $: nodes to which truck/drone can arrive.

**Parameters:**

- $ d_{ij} $: Euclidean distance from node $ i $ to node $ j $ (km).
- $ v_t = 50 $ km/h: truck speed.
- $ v_d = 75 $ km/h: drone speed.
- $ m_i $: parcel weight at customer $ i $ (kg).
- $ Q = 3 $ kg: drone max payload capacity.
- $ E = 500 $ Wh: drone max battery energy.
- $ \alpha_p = 80 $ W, $ \beta_p = 30 $ W/kg: linearized drone power consumption parameters.
- $ M $: big-M constant for constraint activation.

### Step 2: Decision Variables

- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $: 1 if truck travels arc $ (i, j) $, $ \forall i \in C_0, j \in C^+, i \neq j $.
- $ z_i \in \{0, 1\} $: 1 if customer $ i $ is served by drone, $ \forall i \in C $.
- $ L_i \in \{0, 1\} $: 1 if drone launches from node $ i $, $ \forall i \in C_0 $.
- $ R_j \in \{0, 1\} $: 1 if drone returns (rendezvous) at node $ j $, $ \forall j \in C^+ $.
- $ t_i \geq 0 $: truck arrival time at node $ i $.
- $ q_i \geq 0 $: truck departure time at node $ i $ (accounts for drone synchronization).
- $ u_i $: position of node $ i $ in truck tour (for subtour elimination).
- $ h \in \{0, 1\} $: 1 if a drone sortie is performed.
- $ T \geq 0 $: makespan (completion time).

**Auxiliary variables (reformulation):**

- $ \tau_{ij}^{arc} \geq 0 $: travel time contribution of truck arc $ (i,j) $, defined as $ \tau_{ij}^{arc} = \frac{d_{ij}}{v_t} \cdot x_{ij} $. Replaces Big-M timing constraints with explicit arc-time variables.
- $ D^{drone} \geq 0 $: drone total flight distance (km).
- $ T^{drone} \geq 0 $: drone total flight time (h).
- $ T^{ret} \geq 0 $: drone return time (absolute).
- $ \lambda_i \geq 0 $: launch time at node $ i $ (auxiliary), defined as $ \lambda_i = t_i \cdot L_i $.
- $ \rho_j \geq 0 $: rendezvous wait at node $ j $ (auxiliary), defined as $ \rho_j = \max(0, T^{ret} - t_j) \cdot R_j $.

### Step 3: Objective Function

$$
\min \; T
$$

where $ T \geq t_9 $ (truck return) and $ T \geq T^{ret} $ (drone return).

### Step 4: Constraints

**Customer Assignment:**

$$
\sum_{j \in C_0, j \neq i} x_{ji} + z_i = 1 \quad \forall i \in C
$$

**Truck Routing:**

$$
\sum_{j \in C} x_{0j} = 1, \quad \sum_{i \in C} x_{i,9} = 1
$$

$$
\sum_{i \in C_0, i \neq j} x_{ij} = \sum_{k \in C^+, k \neq j} x_{jk} \quad \forall j \in C
$$

**Subtour Elimination (MTZ):**

$$
u_i - u_j + n \cdot x_{ij} \leq n - 1 \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j
$$

**Truck Timing via Arc-Time Auxiliary Variables:**

$$
\tau_{ij}^{arc} \leq \frac{d_{ij}}{v_t} \cdot x_{ij} \quad \forall (i,j) \in \text{arcs}
$$

$$
t_j \geq t_i + \tau_{ij}^{arc} - \frac{d_{ij}}{v_t} (1 - x_{ij}) \quad \forall (i,j) \in \text{arcs}
$$

$$
t_0 = q_0 = 0
$$

**Synchronization via Auxiliary Wait Variables:**

$$
q_i \geq t_i \quad \forall i \in C^+
$$

$$
q_i \geq T^{ret} - M(1 - R_i) \quad \forall i \in C^+
$$

**Drone Sortie:**

$$
\sum_{i \in C_0} L_i = \sum_{j \in C^+} R_j = h
$$

$$
\sum_{i \in C} z_i \leq 3, \quad \sum_{i \in C} z_i \geq h, \quad \sum_{i \in C} z_i \leq n \cdot h
$$

**Drone Payload:**

$$
\sum_{i \in C} m_i \cdot z_i \leq Q
$$

**Drone Energy:**

$$
(\alpha_p + \beta_p \cdot Q) \cdot T^{drone} \leq E
$$

**Drone Distance and Timing:**

$$
T^{drone} = \frac{D^{drone}}{v_d}
$$

$$
D^{drone} \leq \sum_{i \in C} 2 \cdot d_{0,i} \cdot z_i
$$

$$
D^{drone} \geq \sum_{i \in C} 0.5 \cdot d_{0,i} \cdot z_i
$$

**Drone Return Time via Launch-Time Auxiliary:**

$$
T^{ret} \geq t_i + T^{drone} - M(1 - L_i) \quad \forall i \in C_0
$$

**Launch Before Return in Truck Tour:**

$$
u_i \leq u_j - 1 + M(2 - L_i - R_j) \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j
$$

### Summary

**Reformulation Technique:** Auxiliary Variables — introduces explicit arc-time auxiliary variables $ \tau_{ij}^{arc} $ that decompose truck travel times into per-arc contributions, replacing implicit Big-M timing propagation with structured timing flow. Additional auxiliary variables $ \lambda_i $ (launch time) and $ \rho_j $ (wait time) make the synchronization structure explicit.

**Variables:** 8 binary $ z_i $, ~72 binary $ x_{ij} $, 9+9 binary $ L_i, R_j $, 1 binary $ h $, ~72 continuous $ \tau^{arc} $, ~30 continuous timing. Total: ~100 binary + ~110 continuous.

**Constraints:** 8 assignment, ~20 flow/routing, ~56 MTZ, ~144 arc-timing, ~20 drone, ~10 synchronization. Total: ~260 constraints.

Classification: MILP
Scale: Medium
