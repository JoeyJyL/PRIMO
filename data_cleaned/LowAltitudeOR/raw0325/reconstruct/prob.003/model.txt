### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ y_k $: a binary variable indicating whether depot $ k $ is open (1) or not (0).
- $ x_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating if a UAV traverses the arc from node $ i $ to node $ j $ (1) or not (0).
- $ f_{ij} $: a continuous variable representing the single-commodity flow on arc $ (i, j) $, used for subtour elimination.
- $ d_i $: a continuous variable representing the cumulative travel distance upon arriving at node $ i $, used for range checking.

Thus, we have:

- $ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all candidate depots $ k \in D $.
- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all arcs $ (i, j) \in A $.
- $ f_{ij} \ge 0 $ for all arcs $ (i, j) \in A $.
- $ d_i \ge 0 $ for all nodes $ i \in N $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which includes the fixed opening costs for depots and the variable routing costs (distance).

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{k \in D} F_k y_k + \sum_{(i,j) \in A} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ F_k $ is the fixed opening cost for depot $ k $.
- $ c_{ij} $ is the distance/cost between node $ i $ and node $ j $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Target Coverage Constraint:**

Each target point must be entered and left exactly once.

For each Target $ j \in T $:

$$
\sum_{i \in N} x_{ij} = 1
$$

For each Target $ i \in T $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N} x_{ij} = 1
$$

2. **Depot Logic & Flow Conservation Constraint:**

Routes must start and end at the same open depot. If a route leaves depot $ k $, then depot $ k $ must be open.

For each Depot $ k \in D $:

$$
\sum_{j \in T} x_{kj} = \sum_{j \in T} x_{jk}
$$

$$
\sum_{j \in T} x_{kj} \le M y_k
$$

Where $ M = |T| $ is the number of targets.

3. **Single-Commodity Flow Subtour Elimination (replacing MTZ):**

Flow capacity is limited by routing decisions:

$$
f_{ij} \le |T| \cdot x_{ij} \quad \forall (i, j) \in A
$$

Each target consumes exactly 1 unit of flow:

For each Target $ j \in T $:

$$
\sum_{i \in N} f_{ij} - \sum_{k \in N} f_{jk} = 1
$$

Flow can only originate from open depots:

For each Depot $ k \in D $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N} f_{kj} \le |T| \cdot y_k
$$

4. **Cumulative Distance Tracking (for Range Checking only):**

Distance is initialized at depots:

$$
d_k = 0 \quad \forall k \in D
$$

Distance propagation on active arcs:

For each arc $ (i, j) $ where $ j \in T $:

$$
d_j \ge d_i + c_{ij} - M'(1 - x_{ij})
$$

Where $ M' $ is a sufficiently large constant.

5. **Range Constraint:**

The cumulative distance at any target must not exceed the battery range:

$$
d_j \le L \quad \forall j \in T
$$

6. **Return Range Constraint:**

If a target $ i $ returns to depot $ k $, the total round-trip distance must be within range:

$$
d_i + c_{ik} \le L + M'(1 - x_{ik}) \quad \forall i \in T, k \in D
$$

7. **No Depot-to-Depot Arcs:**

$$
\sum_{k_1 \in D} \sum_{k_2 \in D, k_2 \neq k_1} x_{k_1 k_2} = 0
$$
