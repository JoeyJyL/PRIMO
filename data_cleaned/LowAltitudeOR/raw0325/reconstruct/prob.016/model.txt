### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating whether Skyport $ i $ is opened, for $ i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $.
- $ p_r \in \{0, 1\} $: binary product-indicator variable representing whether Route $ r $ is active, for $ r \in \{1, 2, 3\} $.

**Reformulation note (Variable Elimination + MIN General Constraint):** The original formulation uses separate route-activation variables $ x_r $ with individual linking constraints $ x_r \le y_{o_r} $ and $ x_r \le y_{d_r} $. In this reformulation, we **eliminate** the original route variables $ x_r $ and instead introduce product-indicator variables $ p_r $, defined via a **MIN general constraint**: $ p_r = \min(y_{o_r}, y_{d_r}) $. For binary variables, $ \min(y_{o_r}, y_{d_r}) = y_{o_r} \cdot y_{d_r} $, so $ p_r = 1 $ if and only if both endpoints are open. This replaces the two individual linking constraints per route with a single general constraint, reducing the constraint count and eliminating the need for explicit route-activation linking.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The Logit probability is pre-calculated for each route:

* **Route 1 (S1 $ \to $ S3):**
    * $ V_{ground} = -0.5(60) - 0.1(10) + 0 = -31 $
    * $ V_{uam} = -0.5(15) - 0.1(50) + 0 = -12.5 $
    * $ P(UAM)_1 = \frac{e^{-12.5}}{e^{-12.5} + e^{-31}} \approx 1.0 $
    * $ D_1^{UAM} \approx 2000 $

* **Route 2 (S2 $ \to $ S3):**
    * $ V_{ground} = -0.5(50) - 0.1(8) + 0 = -25.8 $
    * $ V_{uam} = -0.5(12) - 0.1(45) + 0 = -10.5 $
    * $ P(UAM)_2 = \frac{e^{-10.5}}{e^{-10.5} + e^{-25.8}} \approx 1.0 $
    * $ D_2^{UAM} \approx 1500 $

* **Route 3 (S1 $ \to $ S4):**
    * $ V_{ground} = -0.5(40) - 0.1(12) + 0 = -21.2 $
    * $ V_{uam} = -0.5(20) - 0.1(40) + 2 = -12 $
    * $ P(UAM)_3 = \frac{e^{-12}}{e^{-12} + e^{-21.2}} \approx 1.0 $
    * $ D_3^{UAM} \approx 500 $

The objective function is:

$$
\max \sum_{r=1}^{3} D_r^{UAM} \cdot p_r = 2000 \, p_1 + 1500 \, p_2 + 500 \, p_3
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Budget (Skyport Count) Constraint:**

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} y_i = 2
$$

2. **MIN General Constraint (replaces individual linking):**

For each route $ r $ with origin $ o_r $ and destination $ d_r $:

$$
p_r = \min(y_{o_r}, \; y_{d_r})
$$

Specifically:
* Route 1: $ p_1 = \min(y_1, y_3) $
* Route 2: $ p_2 = \min(y_2, y_3) $
* Route 3: $ p_3 = \min(y_1, y_4) $

This single constraint per route replaces the two individual linking constraints $ x_r \le y_{o_r} $ and $ x_r \le y_{d_r} $, plus the implicit maximization-driven activation.

3. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
y_i \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall \, i \in \{1,2,3,4\}
$$
$$
p_r \in \{0, 1\}, \quad \forall \, r \in \{1,2,3\}
$$
