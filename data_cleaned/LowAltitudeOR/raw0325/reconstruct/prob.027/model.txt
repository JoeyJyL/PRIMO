### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

**Sets:**
- $M$: Set of candidate vertiports, indexed by $i, k, m$.
- $TR$: Set of OD pairs, indexed by $tr$.

**Parameters:**
- $f_i$: Fixed cost of building vertiport $i$.
- $d^{tr}$: Demand (passengers/day) for OD pair $tr$.
- $R^{tr}$: Revenue per passenger for OD pair $tr$.
- $c_{km}$: Operating cost per passenger for routing through vertiports $k$ and $m$.
- $p$: Number of vertiports to build.

**Decision Variables:**

- $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if candidate vertiport $i$ is selected to be built; 0 otherwise.
- $y_{km}^{tr} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if the demand for OD pair $tr$ is routed via origin vertiport $k$ and destination vertiport $m$; 0 otherwise.

**Auxiliary Variables (Linearized Product):**

- $h_{km} \in [0, 1]$: Continuous auxiliary variable representing the linearized product $x_k \cdot x_m$, i.e., both vertiports $k$ and $m$ are built. Defined for all $k, m \in M, k \neq m$.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the total profit:

$$
\max Z = \sum_{tr \in TR} \sum_{k \in M} \sum_{m \in M, m \neq k} d^{tr} (R^{tr} - c_{km}) \, y_{km}^{tr} - \sum_{i \in M} f_i \, x_i
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Vertiport Siting Constraint:**

Exactly $p$ vertiports must be selected from the candidate set $M$:

$$
\sum_{i \in M} x_i = p
$$

2. **Linearized Product Constraints (Joint Opening):**

The auxiliary variable $h_{km}$ represents $x_k \cdot x_m$ via standard McCormick envelope inequalities. For each pair $k, m \in M, k \neq m$:

$$
h_{km} \leq x_k
$$
$$
h_{km} \leq x_m
$$
$$
h_{km} \geq x_k + x_m - 1
$$
$$
h_{km} \geq 0
$$

3. **Arc Activation via Linearized Product:**

A routing $y_{km}^{tr}$ can only be active if both its origin vertiport $k$ and destination vertiport $m$ are built. This replaces separate origin and destination validity constraints with a single product-based linkage:

$$
y_{km}^{tr} \leq h_{km} \quad \forall tr \in TR, \forall k, m \in M, k \neq m
$$

4. **Single Routing Constraint:**

Each OD pair can be served at most once:

$$
\sum_{k \in M} \sum_{m \in M, m \neq k} y_{km}^{tr} \leq 1 \quad \forall tr \in TR
$$

5. **Binary and Domain Constraints:**

$$
x_i \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in M
$$
$$
y_{km}^{tr} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall tr \in TR, k, m \in M, k \neq m
$$
$$
h_{km} \in [0, 1] \quad \forall k, m \in M, k \neq m
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$: vertiport building indicator.
- $y_{km}^{tr} \in \{0, 1\}$: routing indicator.
- $h_{km} \in [0, 1]$: auxiliary variable for linearized product $x_k \cdot x_m$.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\max Z = \sum_{tr \in TR} \sum_{k \in M} \sum_{m \in M, m \neq k} d^{tr} (R^{tr} - c_{km}) \, y_{km}^{tr} - \sum_{i \in M} f_i \, x_i
$$

**Constraints:**

1. Vertiport siting: $\sum_i x_i = p$.
2. Linearized product: $h_{km} \leq x_k$, $h_{km} \leq x_m$, $h_{km} \geq x_k + x_m - 1$.
3. Arc activation: $y_{km}^{tr} \leq h_{km}$.
4. Single routing: $\sum_{k,m} y_{km}^{tr} \leq 1$.
5. Binary and domain constraints.
