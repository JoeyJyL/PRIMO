SkyNet Logistics is designing a drone-based last-mile delivery network with intermediate charging stations.
**Goal:** Determine the optimal number and locations of charging stations to connect all delivery points to the hub, balancing **infrastructure investment** (number of stations) and **service efficiency** (total path lengths).

**Problem Setting:**
- Drones depart from a central hub to deliver parcels to customers.
- Due to limited battery range, drones may need to stop at intermediate charging stations to recharge before reaching distant customers.
- Each charging station can cover delivery points within radius R (one-way range).
- The drone's round-trip operating range is 2R (can fly distance 2R on a full charge).
- An edge between two locations is feasible only if the distance is ≤ 2R.

**Network Design Decisions:**
1. Which candidate locations should be activated as charging stations?
2. Which stations serve as terminal stations (directly covering delivery points)?
3. What paths connect the hub to each terminal station?

**Bi-Objective Optimization:**
- Minimize total path lengths (service cost proxy)
- Minimize number of active charging stations (infrastructure cost)
- Combined objective: $Z = \theta \cdot f_1 + (1-\theta) \cdot f_2$ where $\theta \in [0,1]$

**Parameters:**
- Drone one-way coverage radius R = 15 units
- Drone round-trip range 2R = 30 units
- Weight parameter θ = 0.5 (balanced objective)

**Coverage Rule:**
- A delivery point is covered if it is within distance R from an active terminal station.
- All delivery points must be covered by at least one terminal station.
