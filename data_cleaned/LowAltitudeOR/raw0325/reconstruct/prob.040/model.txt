### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

We reformulate the UAM vertiport network design problem using the **Big-M Method**. The original model enforces network connectivity through a virtual sink flow network. We replace this with a **Big-M based Miller-Tucker-Zemlin (MTZ) arborescence** formulation that uses directed tree arcs, a root indicator, and ordering variables with Big-M constraints.

**Original variables (unchanged):**

- $ X_j \in \{0, 1\} $: whether a vertiport is constructed at candidate location $ j $.
- $ Y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $: whether demand point $ i $ is assigned to vertiport $ j $.

**Big-M connectivity variables (replacing virtual flow):**

- $ a_{jk} \in \{0, 1\} $: whether directed arc $ (j, k) $ is included in the spanning arborescence connecting selected vertiports (only for pairs with $ d_{jk} \leq D_{max} $).
- $ r_j \in \{0, 1\} $: whether vertiport $ j $ is the root of the arborescence (exactly one selected vertiport is root).
- $ \text{ord}_j \geq 0 $: MTZ ordering variable for vertiport $ j $, representing its depth level in the tree.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The objective is unchanged:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \alpha \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J'} h_i d_{ij} Y_{ij} + \sum_{j \in J'} f_j X_j
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

**Constraints 1–3 (unchanged):**

1. **Facility selection:** $ \sum_{j \in J'} X_j = P $

2. **Demand assignment:** $ \sum_{j \in J'} Y_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i \in I $

3. **Assignment feasibility:** $ Y_{ij} \leq X_j \quad \forall i \in I, j \in J' $

**Big-M Connectivity Constraints (replacing virtual flow):**

4. **Total arcs in arborescence:**

The spanning arborescence of $ P $ selected vertiports has exactly $ P - 1 $ directed arcs:

$$
\sum_{(j,k) \in A} a_{jk} = P - 1
$$

where $ A = \{(j,k) : j,k \in J', j \neq k, d_{jk} \leq D_{max}\} $.

5. **Arc endpoint selection:**

Arcs can only exist between selected vertiports:

$$
a_{jk} \leq X_j \quad \forall (j,k) \in A
$$
$$
a_{jk} \leq X_k \quad \forall (j,k) \in A
$$

6. **Incoming arc balance:**

Each selected non-root vertiport has exactly one incoming arc; the root has zero:

$$
\sum_{j : (j,k) \in A} a_{jk} = X_k - r_k \quad \forall k \in J'
$$

7. **Root selection:**

Exactly one selected vertiport is the root:

$$
\sum_{j \in J'} r_j = 1
$$

8. **Root must be selected:**

$$
r_j \leq X_j \quad \forall j \in J'
$$

9. **MTZ ordering (Big-M):**

Using Big-M constant $ M_{ord} = P $:

$$
\text{ord}_k \geq \text{ord}_j + 1 - P \cdot (1 - a_{jk}) \quad \forall (j,k) \in A
$$

When $ a_{jk} = 1 $: forces $ \text{ord}_k \geq \text{ord}_j + 1 $ (tree ordering).
When $ a_{jk} = 0 $: constraint becomes $ \text{ord}_k \geq \text{ord}_j + 1 - P $, which is non-binding.

10. **Ordering bounds:**

$$
0 \leq \text{ord}_j \leq (P - 1) \cdot X_j \quad \forall j \in J'
$$

Unselected vertiports have $ \text{ord}_j = 0 $.

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Sets:**

- $ I $: demand points.
- $ J' $: candidate vertiport locations.
- $ A = \{(j,k) : j,k \in J', j \neq k, d_{jk} \leq D_{max}\} $: feasible directed arcs.

**Parameters:**

- $ P = 3 $, $ D_{max} = 15 $ km, $ \alpha = 3.5 $ \$/person/km.
- $ h_i $: demand, $ d_{ij} $: distance, $ f_j $: construction cost.
- $ M_{ord} = P $: Big-M constant for MTZ ordering.

**Decision Variables:**

- $ X_j \in \{0,1\} $, $ Y_{ij} \in \{0,1\} $: facility selection and assignment.
- $ a_{jk} \in \{0,1\} $: directed arborescence arcs.
- $ r_j \in \{0,1\} $: root indicator.
- $ \text{ord}_j \in [0, P-1] $: MTZ ordering.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min \alpha \sum_{i,j} h_i d_{ij} Y_{ij} + \sum_j f_j X_j
$$

**Constraints:**

1. $ \sum_j X_j = P $
2. $ \sum_j Y_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall i $
3. $ Y_{ij} \leq X_j \quad \forall i, j $
4. $ \sum_{(j,k) \in A} a_{jk} = P - 1 $
5. $ a_{jk} \leq X_j, \quad a_{jk} \leq X_k \quad \forall (j,k) \in A $
6. $ \sum_j a_{jk} = X_k - r_k \quad \forall k $
7. $ \sum_j r_j = 1 $
8. $ r_j \leq X_j \quad \forall j $
9. $ \text{ord}_k \geq \text{ord}_j + 1 - P(1 - a_{jk}) \quad \forall (j,k) \in A $ (Big-M MTZ)
10. $ 0 \leq \text{ord}_j \leq (P-1) X_j \quad \forall j $

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP
2. Problem Scale: Small
