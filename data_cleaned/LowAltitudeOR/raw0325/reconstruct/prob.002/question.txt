AeroRelief Emergency Logistics is planning to establish a drone hub network in a disaster-prone region.
The planning process involves two stages to handle potential disaster uncertainties:
- **Stage 1 (Strategic):** Decide where to build drone hubs before any disaster occurs. Building a hub incurs a fixed construction cost.
- **Stage 2 (Operational):** Once a disaster hits and demand is realized, drones deliver supplies from built hubs to demand points. This incurs operational costs based on distance and transport volume.

The goal is to select a subset of candidate hubs to build such that the sum of **Construction Cost** and the **Worst-Case Operational Cost** across possible scenarios is minimized (Min-Max Robust Optimization).

**Candidate Hubs**
There are 3 candidate locations for hubs. Each has a maximum service radius of **10 km**.
The unit transportation cost is **$1.0 per unit per km**.

| Hub ID | Construction Cost ($) | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| H1 | 5,000 | Highland, moderate cost |
| H2 | 4,000 | Plains, low cost |
| H3 | 7,000 | City center, high cost |

**Demand Points and Scenarios**
There are 4 demand points (C1-C4). The demand volume depends on the disaster scenario.
Two scenarios are considered:
1.  **Scenario A (Normal Season):** Low, scattered demand.
2.  **Scenario B (Severe Flood):** High demand, concentrated in specific areas.

**Constraints & Requirements**
1.  **Mandatory Coverage:** In any realized scenario, every demand point must be assigned to an **active** (built) hub that is within the 10km service range.
2.  **Objective:** Minimize [Total Construction Cost + Max(Scenario A Op Cost, Scenario B Op Cost)].
    * *Operational Cost = Sum of (Demand * Distance * 1.0)* for the assigned links in that scenario.
