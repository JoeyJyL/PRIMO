### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ x_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating if a UAV travels from node $ i $ to node $ j $ (1) or not (0).
- $ y_k $: a binary variable indicating if Fixed Automated Station (FAS) $ k $ is open (1) or not (0).
- $ u_i $: a continuous variable representing the cumulative distance or energy consumed upon arriving at target node $ i $.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all valid arcs $ (i, j) \in A $.
- $ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all candidate locations $ k \in S $.
- $ 0 \le u_i \le L $ for all target nodes $ i \in T $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which consists of the fixed costs for opening FAS locations and the travel costs.

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{k \in S} f_k y_k + \sum_{(i,j) \in A} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ f_k $ is the fixed cost of opening FAS $ k $.
- $ c_{ij} $ is the travel cost (distance) from node $ i $ to node $ j $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Target Coverage Constraint:**

Each target node must be visited exactly once (in-degree and out-degree = 1).

For each target $ j \in T $:

$$
\sum_{i \in N} x_{ij} = 1
$$

For each target $ i \in T $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N} x_{ij} = 1
$$

2. **Start/End at FAS Constraint:**

Exactly one route starts from a FAS and exactly one route ends at a FAS (single drone path).

$$
\sum_{k \in S} \sum_{j \in T} x_{kj} = 1
$$

$$
\sum_{i \in T} \sum_{k \in S} x_{ik} = 1
$$

3. **FAS Opening Logic Constraint:**

If any flow starts at or ends at FAS $ k $, it must be open.

For each FAS $ k \in S $:

$$
\sum_{j \in T} x_{kj} \le y_k
$$

$$
\sum_{i \in T} x_{ik} \le y_k
$$

4. **Cumulative Distance Propagation via Indicator Constraints (replacing Big-M):**

Instead of traditional Big-M constraints $ u_j \ge u_i + c_{ij} - M(1 - x_{ij}) $, we use **indicator constraints** that are activated only when the corresponding arc is selected, avoiding the numerical issues and weak LP relaxations associated with Big-M.

**Initialization (FAS to Target):**

For each FAS $ k \in S $ and target $ t \in T $:

$$
x_{kt} = 1 \implies u_t \ge c_{kt}
$$

**Propagation (Target to Target):**

For each pair of targets $ i, j \in T $ where $ i \neq j $:

$$
x_{ij} = 1 \implies u_j \ge u_i + c_{ij}
$$

5. **Return Range Check via Indicator Constraint:**

If a target $ t $ connects back to FAS $ k $, the total cumulative distance plus the return leg must be within the battery range.

For each target $ t \in T $ and FAS $ k \in S $:

$$
x_{tk} = 1 \implies u_t + c_{tk} \le L
$$

6. **Binary and Bound Constraints:**

$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad y_k \in \{0, 1\}, \quad 0 \le u_i \le L
$$
