The City Transport Authority plans to deploy an automated UAV patrol system to monitor traffic flow on key urban road segments.
A set of candidate locations for building Fixed Automated Stations (FAS) has been identified. These stations can launch and recover drones, as well as swap batteries.
**Crucially, the system supports flexible routing:** A drone can take off from one FAS, visit a sequence of required monitoring points (Targets), and land at a *different* (or the same) open FAS. This allows for one-way trips that extend the effective patrol range.

**Goal:**
Determine:
1. Which FAS candidates to open.
2. The flight paths for drones to cover all Target Points.
Objective: Minimize the sum of **FAS Construction Costs** and **UAV Travel Costs**.

**Network & Parameters:**
- **UAV Max Range ($L$):** 22 km.
- **FAS Construction Cost:** $100 per station.
- **Travel Cost:** $1.0 per km.

**Locations (Coordinates in km):**
*Candidate FAS Sites ($S$):*
- FAS_1: (0, 0)
- FAS_2: (20, 0)
- FAS_3: (10, 10)

*Target Points to Monitor ($T$):*
- T_A: (5, 0)
- T_B: (10, 0)
- T_C: (15, 0)

**Scenario Analysis:**
- The targets T_A, T_B, T_C form a line between FAS_1 and FAS_2.
- Distances (Euclidean):
  - FAS_1 -> T_A: 5 km
  - T_A -> T_B: 5 km
  - T_B -> T_C: 5 km
  - T_C -> FAS_2: 5 km
  - T_C -> FAS_1: 15 km (Return trip is long!)
  - FAS_3 is located off-axis at (10, 10).

- **Constraint:** Each target must be visited exactly once. The route must start at an OPEN FAS and end at an OPEN FAS.
