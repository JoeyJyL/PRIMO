### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

**Sets:**
- $N$: Set of all nodes (Origin $O$, Destination $D$, and candidate charging stations).
- $A$: Set of feasible arcs, where arc $(i,j)$ exists if the Euclidean distance $d_{ij} \leq R$ (drone maximum range).

**Parameters:**
- $F_i$: Fixed cost of opening node $i$ ($F_O = F_D = 0$).
- $c_{ij} = d_{ij} \times r$: Travel cost on arc $(i,j)$, where $r$ is the cost per km.
- $R$: Drone maximum range (15 km).

**Decision Variables:**

- $y_i \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable indicating if node $i$ is opened/used.
- $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable indicating if arc $(i,j)$ is traversed.

**Auxiliary Variables (Linearized Product):**

- $w_{ij} \in [0, 1]$: Continuous auxiliary variable representing the linearized product $y_i \cdot y_j$, i.e., both endpoints of arc $(i,j)$ are open.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, consisting of fixed costs for opening nodes and travel costs for traversing arcs.

$$
\min Z = \sum_{i \in N} F_i \, y_i + \sum_{(i,j) \in A} c_{ij} \, x_{ij}
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Flow Balance Constraint (Shortest Path):**

For each node $i \in N$:

$$
\sum_{j:(i,j) \in A} x_{ij} - \sum_{j:(j,i) \in A} x_{ji} = 
\begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if } i = O \\
-1 & \text{if } i = D \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

2. **Linearized Product Constraints:**

The auxiliary variable $w_{ij}$ represents $y_i \cdot y_j$ via standard McCormick envelope inequalities. For each arc $(i,j) \in A$:

$$
w_{ij} \leq y_i
$$
$$
w_{ij} \leq y_j
$$
$$
w_{ij} \geq y_i + y_j - 1
$$
$$
w_{ij} \geq 0
$$

3. **Arc Activation via Linearized Product:**

An arc $(i,j)$ can only be traversed if both its endpoints are open. This replaces the traditional node activation constraint with a product-based linkage:

$$
x_{ij} \leq w_{ij}, \quad \forall (i,j) \in A
$$

4. **Source and Destination Fixing:**

The origin and destination are always open:

$$
y_O = 1, \quad y_D = 1
$$

5. **Binary and Domain Constraints:**

$$
y_i \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in N
$$
$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall (i,j) \in A
$$
$$
w_{ij} \in [0, 1] \quad \forall (i,j) \in A
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $y_i \in \{0, 1\}$: node opening indicator.
- $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: arc traversal indicator.
- $w_{ij} \in [0, 1]$: auxiliary variable for linearized product $y_i \cdot y_j$.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min Z = \sum_{i \in N} F_i \, y_i + \sum_{(i,j) \in A} c_{ij} \, x_{ij}
$$

**Constraints:**

1. Flow balance (path from $O$ to $D$).
2. Linearized product: $w_{ij} \leq y_i$, $w_{ij} \leq y_j$, $w_{ij} \geq y_i + y_j - 1$.
3. Arc activation: $x_{ij} \leq w_{ij}$.
4. Fix origin/destination: $y_O = 1$, $y_D = 1$.
5. Binary and domain constraints.

1. Optimization Problem Classification: ILP
2. Problem Scale: Medium
