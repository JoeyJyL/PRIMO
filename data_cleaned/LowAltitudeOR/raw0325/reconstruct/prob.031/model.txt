### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

This model reformulates the TD-MTDRP Set-Partitioning Model by introducing explicit timing auxiliary variables that decompose the recursive ready-time computation into modular components.

**Sets:**
- $V = \{0\} \cup N \cup \{n+1\}$: all nodes (depot 0, customers $N$, ending depot $n+1$).
- $\Omega$: set of all feasible truck-drone combination routes.
- $K$: set of homogeneous trucks.
- For each route $r \in \Omega$: truck stop sequence $(v_0, v_1, \ldots, v_{|S_r|})$ with drone sets $H_i$ at each stop $v_i$.

**Parameters:**
- $b_i$: demand of customer $i$.
- $s_i^t, s_i^d$: truck/drone service time at node $i$.
- $g_i$: parcel weight at node $i$.
- $[e_i, l_i]$: time window at node $i$.
- $Q$: truck capacity; $m$: max drones per truck; $B$: drone battery; $D$: drone payload limit.
- $\tau_{ij}$: truck travel time on edge $(i,j)$.
- $t_{ij}$: drone travel time on arc $\langle i,j \rangle$.

**Decision Variables:**

- $\theta_r \in \{0, 1\}$: equals 1 if route $r \in \Omega$ is selected.

**Auxiliary Variables (Timing Decomposition):**

For each route $r \in \Omega$ and each truck stop $v_i$ in route $r$:

- $\sigma_{r,i} \geq 0$: Truck arrival time at stop $v_i$ in route $r$.
- $\tau^{svc}_{r,i} \geq 0$: Truck service completion time at stop $v_i$ = $\sigma_{r,i} + s_{v_i}^t$.
- $\phi_{r,i,d} \geq 0$: Drone round-trip return time for drone launched from stop $v_i$ to serve customer $d \in H_i$.

$$
\phi_{r,i,d} = \sigma_{r,i} + t_{v_i, d} + s_d^d + t_{d, v_i}
$$

- $\delta_{r,i} \geq 0$: Truck departure time from stop $v_i$ (truck waits for all drones to return):

$$
\delta_{r,i} = \max\left(\tau^{svc}_{r,i},\ \max_{d \in H_i} \phi_{r,i,d}\right)
$$

- $\psi_r \geq 0$: Total duration of route $r$ = $\delta_{r,|S_r|} + \tau_{v_{|S_r|}, 0}$ (final departure + return to depot).

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

Minimize total duration across all selected routes:

$$
\min Z = \sum_{r \in \Omega} \psi_r \cdot \theta_r
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Customer Coverage (Set-Partitioning):**

Each customer is served exactly once (by truck or drone) across all selected routes:

$$
\sum_{r \in \Omega} (\alpha_{ir} + \beta_{ir}) \theta_r = 1, \quad \forall i \in N
$$

2. **Fleet Size Constraint:**

$$
\sum_{r \in \Omega} \theta_r \leq |K|
$$

3. **Auxiliary Timing Definitions:**

For each route $r$ and stop index $i$:

$$
\sigma_{r,0} = 0 \quad \text{(depart depot at time 0)}
$$
$$
\sigma_{r,i} = \delta_{r,i-1} + \tau_{v_{i-1}, v_i} \quad \forall i \geq 1
$$
$$
\tau^{svc}_{r,i} = \sigma_{r,i} + s_{v_i}^t
$$
$$
\phi_{r,i,d} = \sigma_{r,i} + t_{v_i, d} + s_d^d + t_{d, v_i} \quad \forall d \in H_i
$$

4. **Departure Time (Linearized Max):**

$$
\delta_{r,i} \geq \tau^{svc}_{r,i}
$$
$$
\delta_{r,i} \geq \phi_{r,i,d} \quad \forall d \in H_i
$$

At optimality (minimization), $\delta_{r,i}$ equals the maximum.

5. **Route Duration Definition:**

$$
\psi_r = \delta_{r,|S_r|} + \tau_{v_{|S_r|}, 0}
$$

6. **Route Feasibility Conditions:**

- Capacity: $\sum_{i \in \text{truck}} b_i + \sum_{d \in \text{drone}} b_d \leq Q$
- Drone battery: $2 \cdot t_{v_i, d} \leq B$ for each drone customer $d \in H_i$
- Drone payload: $g_d \leq D$ for each drone customer $d$
- Time windows: $\sigma_{r,i} \leq l_{v_i}$ for all stops
- Max drones: $|H_i| \leq m$ at each truck stop

7. **Binary Constraint:**

$$
\theta_r \in \{0, 1\}, \quad r \in \Omega
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $\theta_r \in \{0, 1\}$: route selection.

**Auxiliary Variables (Timing Decomposition):**
- $\sigma_{r,i}$: truck arrival time at stop $i$ in route $r$.
- $\tau^{svc}_{r,i}$: truck service completion time.
- $\phi_{r,i,d}$: drone round-trip return time.
- $\delta_{r,i}$: truck departure time (linearized max of service and drone returns).
- $\psi_r$: total route duration.

**Objective:**
$$
\min Z = \sum_{r \in \Omega} \psi_r \cdot \theta_r
$$

**Constraints:**
1. Set-partitioning coverage: $\sum_r (\alpha_{ir} + \beta_{ir}) \theta_r = 1$.
2. Fleet size: $\sum_r \theta_r \leq |K|$.
3. Timing definitions: $\sigma$, $\tau^{svc}$, $\phi$ linking.
4. Departure: $\delta_{r,i} \geq \max(\tau^{svc}_{r,i}, \max_d \phi_{r,i,d})$.
5. Duration: $\psi_r = \delta_{r,|S_r|} + \tau_{v_{|S_r|}, 0}$.
6. Feasibility: capacity, battery, payload, time windows.
7. Binary: $\theta_r \in \{0, 1\}$.

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP / Set-Partitioning Model with auxiliary timing decomposition
2. Problem Size: Medium (6 customers, 2 trucks, 2 drones/truck)
3. Solution Method: Exhaustive route enumeration with auxiliary timing computation
