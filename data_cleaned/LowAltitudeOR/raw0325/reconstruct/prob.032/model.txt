### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

This model reformulates the Air Taxi Skyport Location Problem using the Big-M Method. Instead of placing revenue coefficients directly in the objective, we introduce explicit revenue flow variables bounded by Big-M coefficients that enforce the linkage between allocation decisions and skyport opening status.

**Sets:**
- $\mathcal{L} = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$: origin zones (also candidate skyport locations).
- $\mathcal{J} = \{A, B\}$: destination airports.
- $p = 2$: number of skyports to open.

**Parameters (Pre-computed):**
- $D_{ij}$: monthly demand from origin $i$ to airport $j$.
- $\theta_{ikj}$: logit choice probability of air taxi for origin $i$ via skyport $k$ to airport $j$.
- $R_{ikj} = (f_{ik} + f_{kj}) \cdot \theta_{ikj} \cdot D_{ij}$: pre-computed maximum revenue coefficient for the $(i,k,j)$ combination.

**Decision Variables:**

- $y_k \in \{0, 1\}$: equals 1 if skyport $k$ is opened.
- $x_{ikj} \in \{0, 1\}$: equals 1 if origin $i$ is assigned to skyport $k$ for airport $j$.
- $f_{ikj} \geq 0$: Revenue flow variable — the actual revenue generated from origin $i$ routed via skyport $k$ to airport $j$.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

Maximize total revenue via the flow variables:

$$
\max Z = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{L}} \sum_{k \in \mathcal{L}} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{J}} f_{ikj}
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Single Allocation Constraint:**

Each origin-airport pair is assigned to exactly one skyport:

$$
\sum_{k \in \mathcal{L}} x_{ikj} = 1 \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{L}, j \in \mathcal{J}
$$

2. **Big-M Revenue Bound (Skyport Opening):**

Revenue can only flow through an open skyport. The Big-M coefficient $M_{ikj} = R_{ikj}$ is the maximum possible revenue for this combination:

$$
f_{ikj} \leq R_{ikj} \cdot y_k \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{L}, k \in \mathcal{L}, j \in \mathcal{J}
$$

When $y_k = 0$: $f_{ikj} \leq 0$, forcing zero revenue from closed skyports.
When $y_k = 1$: $f_{ikj} \leq R_{ikj}$, allowing up to full revenue.

3. **Revenue Activation Constraint:**

Revenue can only be generated if the OD pair is allocated to this skyport:

$$
f_{ikj} \leq R_{ikj} \cdot x_{ikj} \quad \forall i, k, j
$$

4. **Revenue Completeness Constraint:**

When an OD pair is allocated to an open skyport, the full revenue is captured:

$$
f_{ikj} \geq R_{ikj} \cdot (x_{ikj} + y_k - 1) \quad \forall i, k, j
$$

This ensures $f_{ikj} = R_{ikj}$ when both $x_{ikj} = 1$ and $y_k = 1$.

5. **Budget Constraint:**

$$
\sum_{k \in \mathcal{L}} y_k = p
$$

6. **Variable Bounds:**

$$
y_k \in \{0, 1\}, \quad x_{ikj} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad f_{ikj} \geq 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $y_k \in \{0, 1\}$: skyport opening indicator.
- $x_{ikj} \in \{0, 1\}$: allocation indicator.
- $f_{ikj} \geq 0$: revenue flow (Big-M bounded).

**Objective:**
$$
\max Z = \sum_{i,k,j} f_{ikj}
$$

**Constraints:**
1. Single allocation: $\sum_k x_{ikj} = 1$.
2. Big-M revenue bound: $f_{ikj} \leq R_{ikj} \cdot y_k$.
3. Revenue activation: $f_{ikj} \leq R_{ikj} \cdot x_{ikj}$.
4. Revenue completeness: $f_{ikj} \geq R_{ikj} \cdot (x_{ikj} + y_k - 1)$.
5. Budget: $\sum_k y_k = p$.
6. Variable bounds.

1. Optimization Problem Classification: ILP (with continuous flow variables)
2. Problem Scale: Medium (55 binary + 50 continuous variables, 111+ constraints)
3. Solution Method: Exact MILP solver (Gurobi)
