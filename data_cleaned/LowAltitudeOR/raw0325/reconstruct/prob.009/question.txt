EcoDrone Solutions needs to deploy a network of charging stations (CS) in a national park to support continuous UAV monitoring for wildfires.
**Problem Statement:**
- We have identified 4 potential Candidate Sites for building charging stations.
- There are 6 Critical Monitoring Zones (Targets) that must be covered.
- A target is considered "covered" if it is within the UAV's **Maximum Service Radius ($R_{max}$)** from an open charging station.
- **Goal:** Select the **minimum number** of charging stations to open such that all targets are covered.

**Parameters:**
- **UAV Service Radius ($R_{max}$):** 5.0 km
- **Cost:** We aim to minimize the *count* of stations (assuming uniform cost).

**Locations (Coordinates in km):**
*Candidate Stations ($J$):*
- CS1: (2, 2)
- CS2: (2, 8)
- CS3: (8, 2)
- CS4: (8, 8)

*Target Zones ($I$):*
- T1: (1, 1)  | Near CS1
- T2: (1, 9)  | Near CS2
- T3: (9, 1)  | Near CS3
- T4: (9, 9)  | Near CS4
- T5: (5, 5)  | Center point
- T6: (2, 5)  | Between CS1 and CS2

**Distance Analysis (Pythagorean):**
- Distance from CS1(2,2) to T5(5,5): $\sqrt{(5-2)^2 + (5-2)^2} = \sqrt{18} \approx 4.24$ km. (Covered!)
- Distance from CS1(2,2) to T2(1,9): $\sqrt{(1-2)^2 + (9-2)^2} = \sqrt{1 + 49} \approx 7.07$ km. (Not Covered)
- The central target T5 is within range of ALL candidates.
- The corner targets (T1, T2, T3, T4) are only close to their respective corner candidates.
