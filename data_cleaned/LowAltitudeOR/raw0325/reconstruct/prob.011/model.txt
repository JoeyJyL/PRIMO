### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if vertiport $ i $ is opened, for all $ i \in N $.
- $ z_k \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if OD route $ k = (i, j) $ is active, for all $ k \in K $.

These are the same variables as the original model; the key difference is in how the route activation logic is modeled.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the robust net profit.

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{k \in K} z_k \cdot (D_k^{nom} - \hat{D}_k) \cdot P_k^{air} \cdot Margin_k^{air} - \sum_{i \in N} F_i \, y_i
$$

Where:

- $ D_k^{nom} $ is the nominal daily demand for route $ k $.
- $ \hat{D}_k $ is the demand uncertainty margin for route $ k $.
- $ P_k^{air} $ is the Logit probability of choosing Air Taxi for route $ k $.
- $ Margin_k^{air} = Ticket_k - OpCost_k $ is the net revenue per air passenger.
- $ F_i $ is the fixed cost of opening vertiport $ i $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Route Activation via AND General Constraint:**

Instead of using two separate individual linking constraints ($ z_k \le y_i $ and $ z_k \le y_j $), we use a **logical AND constraint** that directly expresses the business logic: a route is active if and only if both endpoints are open.

For each route $ k = (i, j) \in K $:

$$
z_k = y_i \wedge y_j
$$

This is implemented as a solver-native **AND general constraint** (`addGenConstrAnd`). Under maximization, the original individual linking constraints only ensure $ z_k \le \min(y_i, y_j) $; the AND constraint additionally enforces $ z_k \ge y_i + y_j - 1 $, making the relationship an equivalence rather than a relaxation.

2. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
y_i, z_k \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall\, i \in N,\; k \in K
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in N $.
- $ z_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in K $.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{k \in K} z_k \cdot (D_k^{nom} - \hat{D}_k) \cdot P_k^{air} \cdot Margin_k^{air} - \sum_{i \in N} F_i \, y_i
$$

**Constraints:**

$$
z_k = y_i \wedge y_j \quad \forall\, k = (i,j) \in K
$$

$$
y_i, z_k \in \{0, 1\}
$$
