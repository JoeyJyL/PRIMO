BayCity Transport Authority is planning a UAM network to connect its mainland districts with offshore islands.
**Goal:** Select Vertiport locations to maximize the total daily passenger coverage under a budget constraint.
**Service Types:**
1. **Regular Shuttle (RS):** Fixed high-speed routes across the bay. An RS route is active ONLY if **BOTH** endpoints have an open Vertiport.
2. **On-Demand Mobility (ODM):** Flexible intra-city hops. An ODM demand zone is covered if **AT LEAST ONE** Vertiport is opened within its vicinity.

**Parameters:**
- **Total Budget:** $30 Million.
- **Construction Costs:** Vary by location.

**Candidate Vertiports:**
- **V1 (Downtown):** Cost $10M. Central hub.
- **V2 (Island Resort):** Cost $12M. Key tourist spot.
- **V3 (Tech Port):** Cost $8M. Industrial zone.
- **V4 (Suburbs):** Cost $6M. Residential area.

**Demand Data:**
*RS Routes (Requires Both Ends Open):*
- **Route A (Downtown <-> Island):** Demand 500 pax/day. (Requires V1 & V2)
- **Route B (Downtown <-> Tech):** Demand 300 pax/day. (Requires V1 & V3)

*ODM Zones (Requires One Nearby Open):*
- **Zone 1 (Downtown Core):** Demand 100. Covered by V1.
- **Zone 2 (Island Local):** Demand 50. Covered by V2.
- **Zone 3 (Tech Hub):** Demand 80. Covered by V3.
- **Zone 4 (Suburb Area):** Demand 150. Covered by V4.

**Analysis:**
- **Option 1 (V1 + V2):** Cost 22M. RS: Route A (500). ODM: Z1(100)+Z2(50). Total = 650.
- **Option 2 (V1 + V3 + V4):** Cost 10+8+6 = 24M. RS: Route B (300). ODM: Z1(100)+Z3(80)+Z4(150). Total = 630.
- **Option 3 (V1 + V2 + V4):** Cost 10+12+6 = 28M. RS: Route A (500). ODM: Z1+Z2+Z4 (100+50+150). Total = 800.
- **Option 4 (All):** Cost 36M (Over budget).
