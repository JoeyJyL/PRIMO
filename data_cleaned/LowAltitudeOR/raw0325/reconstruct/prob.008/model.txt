### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

We define the following decision variables, noting that the auxiliary ODM zone variables are **eliminated** by direct substitution.

- $ x_i \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating whether vertiport $ i $ is opened, for all $ i \in V $.
- $ y_r \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating whether RS route $ r $ is active, for all $ r \in R $.

The original zone-coverage variables $ w_z $ are eliminated: since $ w_z \le x_{v_z} $ under maximization forces $ w_z = x_{v_z} $, we substitute $ x_{v_z} $ directly.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The objective maximizes total served demand. With $ w_z $ eliminated and replaced by $ x_{v_z} $:

$$
\text{Maximize } \sum_{r \in R} D_r^{RS} \, y_r \;+\; \sum_{z \in Z} D_z^{ODM} \, x_{v_z}
$$

Where:

- $ D_r^{RS} $ is the demand of RS route $ r $.
- $ D_z^{ODM} $ is the demand of ODM zone $ z $.
- $ v_z $ is the vertiport that covers zone $ z $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Budget Constraint:**

$$
\sum_{i \in V} C_i \, x_i \le B
$$

2. **Combined RS Route Logic (Aggregated Linking):**

Instead of two separate individual linking constraints per route, we use a **single aggregated constraint**. For each route $ r \in R $ with origin $ o_r $ and destination $ d_r $:

$$
2 \, y_r \le x_{o_r} + x_{d_r}
$$

This is equivalent to the pair $ y_r \le x_{o_r},\; y_r \le x_{d_r} $ for binary variables: if either endpoint is closed (0), the right-hand side is at most 1, forcing $ y_r \le 0.5 $, hence $ y_r = 0 $.

3. **Product Linearization Tightening Cut:**

To strengthen the LP relaxation, we add a **McCormick lower-bound** cut for the implicit product $ y_r = x_{o_r} \cdot x_{d_r} $:

$$
y_r \ge x_{o_r} + x_{d_r} - 1
$$

Combined with the aggregated linking and the maximization objective, this fully linearizes the binary product.

4. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
x_i \in \{0,1\} \quad \forall\, i \in V, \qquad y_r \in \{0,1\} \quad \forall\, r \in R
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**

- $ x_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in V $.
- $ y_r \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ r \in R $.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\text{Maximize } \sum_{r \in R} D_r^{RS} \, y_r + \sum_{z \in Z} D_z^{ODM} \, x_{v_z}
$$

**Constraints:**

$$
\sum_{i \in V} C_i \, x_i \le B
$$

$$
2 \, y_r \le x_{o_r} + x_{d_r} \quad \forall\, r \in R
$$

$$
y_r \ge x_{o_r} + x_{d_r} - 1 \quad \forall\, r \in R
$$

$$
x_i, y_r \in \{0, 1\}
$$
