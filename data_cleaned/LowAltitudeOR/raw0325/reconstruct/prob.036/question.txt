MetroSky Vertiport Authority is managing the arrival scheduling of electric VTOL (Vertical Take-off and Landing) aircraft at a single urban vertiport.
**Goal:** Determine the optimal **landing sequence** for arriving aircraft to minimize the **total weighted deviation** between the Adjusted Estimated Time of Arrival (AETA) and the Scheduled Time of Arrival (STA), subject to minimum landing separation constraints.

**Background:**
- Unlike conventional airports, UAM vertiports are located in dense urban areas with very limited apron space.
- VTOL aircraft can hover at holding points, so enforcing an adjusted landing sequence is feasible.
- Minimizing ground time (time the aircraft occupies the vertiport pad) is critical for efficient vertiport utilization.
- The scheduling strategy aims to land aircraft as close as possible to their STA, reducing unnecessary ground occupancy.

**Scheduling Rules:**
- Each aircraft $i$ is assigned a landing sequence position $S_i \in \{1, 2, ..., N\}$ (a permutation).
- The Adjusted Estimated Time of Arrival is: $AETA_i = \max(ETA_i, \Delta L \times (S_i - 1) + t_0)$, where $\Delta L$ is the minimum landing separation time and $t_0$ is the current time (set to 0).
- The cost function minimizes: $\sum_i |AETA_i - STA_i| \times W_i$, where $W_i$ is the early operation weight.
- Early arrivals ($AETA_i \leq STA_i$) get weight $\alpha = 0.4$; late arrivals get weight $1 - \alpha = 0.6$.
- Any two aircraft must maintain at least $\Delta L$ seconds between their landing times.

**Data - Aircraft Fleet (8 VTOL aircraft):**
- Minimum Landing Separation: $\Delta L = 40$ seconds
- Current Time: $t_0 = 0$ seconds

| Aircraft | ETA (sec) | STA (sec) |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| A1       | 15        | 50        |
| A2       | 25        | 55        |
| A3       | 60        | 80        |
| A4       | 35        | 90        |
| A5       | 70        | 100       |
| A6       | 10        | 120       |
| A7       | 85        | 130       |
| A8       | 50        | 140       |

**Objective:** Find the landing sequence assignment that minimizes total weighted absolute deviation between AETA and STA for all aircraft.
