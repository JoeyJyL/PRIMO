### Step 1: Sets and Parameters

**Sets:**

- $ N = \{A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8\} $: set of arriving aircraft.

**Parameters:**

- $ ETA_i $: estimated time of arrival of aircraft $ i $ (seconds).
- $ STA_i $: scheduled time of arrival of aircraft $ i $ (seconds).
- $ \Delta L = 40 $: minimum landing separation time (seconds).
- $ t_0 = 0 $: current time (seconds).
- $ \alpha = 0.4 $: early operation weight.
- $ M $: Big-M constant (sufficiently large, e.g., $ M = \max(STA_i) + n \cdot \Delta L $).

### Step 2: Decision Variables

- $ AETA_i \geq 0 $: adjusted estimated time of arrival for aircraft $ i $.
- $ d_i^{+} \geq 0 $: late deviation $ \max(AETA_i - STA_i, 0) $.
- $ d_i^{-} \geq 0 $: early deviation $ \max(STA_i - AETA_i, 0) $.
- $ p_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $: 1 if aircraft $ i $ lands before aircraft $ j $ (pairwise ordering).

### Step 3: Objective Function

$$
\min \; Z = \sum_{i \in N} \left[ \alpha \cdot d_i^{-} + (1 - \alpha) \cdot d_i^{+} \right]
$$

### Step 4: Constraints

**1. AETA lower bound (ETA — cannot land earlier than physically possible):**

$$
AETA_i \geq ETA_i \quad \forall i \in N
$$

**2. Pairwise ordering (exactly one of $ i $ before $ j $ or $ j $ before $ i $):**

$$
p_{ij} + p_{ji} = 1 \quad \forall i, j \in N, \; i < j
$$

**3. AETA floor from implicit ordinal position via Big-M ordering:**

The position of aircraft $ i $ is implicitly determined by the pairwise ordering:

$$
\text{pos}(i) = 1 + \sum_{j \neq i} p_{ji}
$$

The AETA floor is then:

$$
AETA_i \geq \Delta L \cdot \sum_{j \neq i} p_{ji} \quad \forall i \in N
$$

**4. Transitivity (optional tightening):**

To ensure a valid total ordering, if $ i $ is before $ j $ and $ j $ before $ k $, then $ i $ must be before $ k $:

$$
p_{ij} + p_{jk} - p_{ik} \leq 1 \quad \forall i, j, k \in N, \; i \neq j \neq k \neq i
$$

**5. Deviation definition:**

$$
d_i^{+} - d_i^{-} = AETA_i - STA_i \quad \forall i \in N
$$

**6. Non-negativity:**

$$
AETA_i, \; d_i^{+}, \; d_i^{-} \geq 0; \quad p_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary

**Reformulation Technique:** Big-M Method — replaces the assignment matrix formulation $ x_{i,s} $ (which assigns each aircraft to a specific sequence position) with pairwise ordering variables $ p_{ij} $ and implicit position computation. The ordinal position of aircraft $ i $ is derived as $ \text{pos}(i) = 1 + \sum_{j \neq i} p_{ji} $, and the AETA floor is set via $ AETA_i \geq \Delta L \cdot \sum_{j \neq i} p_{ji} $. Transitivity constraints ensure a valid total ordering.

**Decision Variables:** $ AETA_i $ (continuous, 8), $ d_i^+, d_i^- $ (continuous, 16), $ p_{ij} $ (binary, $ 8 \times 7 = 56 $). Total: ~24 continuous + 56 binary.

**Constraints:** 8 ETA bounds, 28 ordering pairs, 8 AETA floor, 336 transitivity, 8 deviation definitions. Total: ~388 constraints.

Classification: MILP
Scale: Small
