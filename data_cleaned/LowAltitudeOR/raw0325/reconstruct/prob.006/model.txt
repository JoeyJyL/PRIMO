### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ x_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating if village $ i $ is served by facility $ j $ (1) or not (0).
- $ y_j $: a binary variable indicating if facility $ j $ is opened (1) or not (0).
- $ E_j $: a continuous auxiliary variable representing the total energy (round-trip distance in km) consumed at facility $ j $.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in I $ and $ j \in J $.
- $ y_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in J $.
- $ E_j \ge 0 $ for all $ j \in J $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the total covered demand weighted by the population of each village.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{j \in J} \sum_{i \in I} w_i x_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ w_i $ is the population weight of village $ i $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Budget of Facilities Constraint:**

Exactly $ P $ facilities must be opened.

$$
\sum_{j \in J} y_j = P
$$

2. **Energy Definition Constraint (Auxiliary Variable):**

The auxiliary variable $ E_j $ captures the total energy consumed at facility $ j $, defined as the sum of round-trip distances to all served villages.

For all $ j \in J $:

$$
E_j = \sum_{i \in I} (2 \cdot d_{ij}) \cdot x_{ij}
$$

3. **Merged Energy-Activation Constraint (replacing individual linking):**

Instead of using separate individual linking constraints $ x_{ij} \le y_j $ for each village-facility pair, we combine the energy capacity and activation logic into a single constraint. If facility $ j $ is closed ($ y_j = 0 $), then $ E_j = 0 $, which forces all $ x_{ij} = 0 $. If open, the total energy is bounded by the range budget.

For all $ j \in J $:

$$
E_j \le R_{max} \cdot y_j
$$

This single constraint per facility replaces both the individual linking constraints and the separate energy capacity constraint.

4. **Single Service Constraint:**

Each village is served at most once (by at most one facility).

For all $ i \in I $:

$$
\sum_{j \in J} x_{ij} \le 1
$$

5. **Binary and Non-negativity Constraints:**

$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad y_j \in \{0, 1\}, \quad E_j \ge 0
$$
