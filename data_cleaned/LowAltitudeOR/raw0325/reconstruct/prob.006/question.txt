
HealthDrone Inc. aims to deploy a drone launch station to deliver emergency vaccines to rural villages.
**Operational Logic:**
- **Hub-and-Spoke:** The drone starts at the Launch Station, flies to a village, drops the package, and returns to the station to reload battery/package. This counts as one trip.
- **Energy Budget:** The station has a limited daily energy budget (represented as total flight km) for its drone fleet.
- **Goal:** Select exactly **1** Launch Station to open from candidate sites to maximize the total population served.

**Constraints:**
- **Max Stations:** 1.
- **Max Range Budget:** The drone(s) at the opened station can fly a total of **40 km** (sum of all round trips) per day.
- **Round Trip:** Distance cost to serve a customer = $2 \times$ One-way Euclidean Distance.

**Locations & Population (Weight):**
*Candidate Stations:*
- Site A (ID: 1): (0, 0)
- Site B (ID: 2): (20, 0)

*Villages (Demand Points):*
- V1 (ID: 3): (0, 4)   | Pop: 10 | Near Site A
- V2 (ID: 4): (0, 8)   | Pop: 15 | Near Site A
- V3 (ID: 5): (3, 0)   | Pop: 20 | Near Site A
- V4 (ID: 6): (18, 0)  | Pop: 30 | Near Site B (High value)
- V5 (ID: 7): (20, 6)  | Pop: 25 | Near Site B (High value)

**Analysis:**
- Site A is surrounded by V1, V2, V3.
- Site B is near V4, V5.
- Because of the 40km total limit, you cannot serve everyone. You must solve the "Knapsack Problem" for the chosen site to pick the best combination of villages.
