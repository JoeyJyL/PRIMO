The Regional Emergency Response Center needs to establish a robust relief network for a mountainous region prone to landslides.
**Decision Problem:**
- **Stage 1 (Strategic):** Select which Relief Depots to activate from candidate sites. Activating a depot incurs a Fixed Cost.
- **Stage 2 (Operational):** Route Trucks and Drones to deliver emergency supplies (medicine/food) to affected villages under a **"Severe Disaster Scenario"** (Peak Demand).

**Logistics Logic:**
- **Trucks:** High capacity, serve "Heavy Demand" villages or villages reachable by road. Must start and end at an *Open Depot*.
- **Drones:** Low capacity, serve "Light Demand" villages within a limited radius ($R_{drone}$) from an *Open Depot*.
- **Robustness:** The plan must satisfy the *Peak Demand* of all villages.

**Parameters:**
- **Truck Cost:** $2.0 per km (Difficult terrain).
- **Drone Cost:** $0.2 per km.
- **Drone Range ($R_{drone}$):** 5.0 km.
- **Drone Capacity:** 10 units.

**Locations & Peak Data:**
*Candidate Depots:*
- Depot A (ID: 1): (0, 0)   | Fixed Cost: 500 | Central
- Depot B (ID: 2): (20, 0)  | Fixed Cost: 600 | Remote

*Affected Villages (Peak Demand):*
- V1 (ID: 3): (2, 2)   | Demand: 5   | Near A (Drone candidate)
- V2 (ID: 4): (18, 2)  | Demand: 8   | Near B (Drone candidate)
- V3 (ID: 5): (10, 10) | Demand: 50  | Central, Heavy Demand (Truck Only)
- V4 (ID: 6): (5, 5)   | Demand: 15  | Exceeds Drone Cap (Truck Only)

**Goal:**
Minimize Total Cost = (Fixed Cost) + (Truck Routing Cost) + (Drone Delivery Cost).
Determine the robust network configuration (Open Depots) and routing plan.
