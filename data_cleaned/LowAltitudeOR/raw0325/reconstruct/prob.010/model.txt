### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

- $ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if Depot $ k $ is open, for all $ k \in D $.
- $ z_{ik} \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if Village $ i $ is served by a drone from Depot $ k $, for all $ i \in V, k \in D $.
- $ v_i \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if Village $ i $ is served by a truck, for all $ i \in V $.
- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if the truck travels from node $ i $ to node $ j $, for all $ i, j \in N $.
- $ u_i \ge 0 $: continuous sequencing variable for subtour elimination via **indicator constraints**, for all $ i \in N $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost: fixed depot costs, drone operational costs, and truck travel costs.

$$
\min \sum_{k \in D} F_k y_k + \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{k \in D} c_{ik}^{drone} z_{ik} + \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in N} c_{ij}^{truck} x_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ F_k $ is the fixed cost of opening Depot $ k $.
- $ c_{ik}^{drone} = 2 \cdot d_{ik} \cdot \rho_{drone} $ is the drone round-trip cost from Depot $ k $ to Village $ i $.
- $ c_{ij}^{truck} = d_{ij} \cdot \rho_{truck} $ is the truck travel cost on arc $ (i,j) $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Service Guarantee Constraint:**

Every village must be served exactly once, either by a drone or by a truck.

For each Village $ i \in V $:

$$
\sum_{k \in D} z_{ik} + v_i = 1
$$

2. **Aggregated Drone-Depot Capacity (replaces individual linking):**

Instead of individual constraints $ z_{ik} \le y_k $ for each village-depot pair, we use a **single aggregated capacity constraint** per depot. The total number of villages served by drones from Depot $ k $ is bounded by $ |V| \cdot y_k $:

For each Depot $ k \in D $:

$$
\sum_{i \in V} z_{ik} \le |V| \cdot y_k
$$

When $ y_k = 0 $, this forces all $ z_{ik} = 0 $. When $ y_k = 1 $, all drone assignments are feasible. For binary variables, this is equivalent to the individual linking constraints.

(Note: Pre-processing filters ensure $ z_{ik} = 0 $ if Village $ i $ is out of drone range or exceeds drone capacity.)

3. **Truck Routing Constraints:**

For each node $ i \in N $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N \setminus \{i\}} x_{ij} = \alpha_i, \qquad \sum_{j \in N \setminus \{i\}} x_{ji} = \alpha_i
$$

Where $ \alpha_k = y_k $ for depots and $ \alpha_i = v_i $ for villages.

4. **Subtour Elimination via Indicator Constraints:**

Instead of the Big-M linearization $ u_j \ge u_i + 1 - M(1 - x_{ij}) $, we use **indicator constraints** that are handled natively by the solver without introducing a Big-M parameter:

For all $ i, j \in N \setminus \{1\},\; i \ne j $:

$$
x_{ij} = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; u_j \ge u_i + 1
$$

This eliminates subtours among non-root nodes. Node 1 (Depot A) serves as the sequencing root.

5. **At Least One Depot Open:**

$$
\sum_{k \in D} y_k \ge 1
$$

6. **Binary and Domain Constraints:**

$$
y_k, z_{ik}, v_i, x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad u_i \ge 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in D $.
- $ z_{ik} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in V, k \in D $.
- $ v_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in V $.
- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i, j \in N $.
- $ u_i \ge 0 $ for all $ i \in N $.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min \sum_{k \in D} F_k y_k + \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{k \in D} c_{ik}^{drone} z_{ik} + \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in N} c_{ij}^{truck} x_{ij}
$$

**Constraints:**

$$
\sum_{k \in D} z_{ik} + v_i = 1 \quad \forall\, i \in V
$$

$$
\sum_{i \in V} z_{ik} \le |V| \cdot y_k \quad \forall\, k \in D
$$

$$
\sum_{j \ne i} x_{ij} = \alpha_i, \quad \sum_{j \ne i} x_{ji} = \alpha_i \quad \forall\, i \in N
$$

$$
x_{ij} = 1 \;\Longrightarrow\; u_j \ge u_i + 1 \quad \forall\, i, j \in N \setminus \{1\},\; i \ne j
$$

$$
\sum_{k \in D} y_k \ge 1
$$
