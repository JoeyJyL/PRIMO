### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

**Sets:**
- $V$: Set of vertiports.
- $T$: Set of time windows (AM, PM).

**Parameters:**
- $n_{ijt}$: Passenger demand flow rate from vertiport $i$ to vertiport $j$ in time window $t$ (trips/hr).
- $\tau$: Flight time (hours).
- $t_c$: Charging time (hours).
- $C_{fleet}$: Unit cost per drone.
- $C_{space}$: Unit cost per parking space.
- $C_{reloc}$: Unit cost per relocation flight.

**Decision Variables:**

- $r_{ijt} \geq 0$: Number of empty relocation flights from vertiport $i$ to vertiport $j$ in time window $t$.
- $w_{it} \geq 1$: Number of idle drones at vertiport $i$ in time window $t$ (at least 1 for safety buffer).
- $F \geq 0$: Maximum total fleet size required across all time windows.
- $S_i \geq 0$: Maximum parking space capacity required at vertiport $i$ across all time windows.

**Auxiliary Variables (Decomposed Fleet and Charging Components):**

- $p_{ijt} \geq 0$: Number of drones simultaneously in-flight on link $(i,j)$ during period $t$ (in steady state: flow rate $\times$ flight time).
- $q_{it} \geq 0$: Number of drones simultaneously charging at vertiport $i$ during period $t$ (in steady state: arrival rate $\times$ charging time).

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total investment and operational costs:

$$
\min Z = C_{fleet} \cdot F + \sum_{i \in V} C_{space} \cdot S_i + \sum_{t \in T} \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V} C_{reloc} \cdot r_{ijt}
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Flow Conservation Constraint:**

In steady state, the total inflow must equal the total outflow at each vertiport in every time period.

For each vertiport $i \in V$ and time $t \in T$:

$$
\sum_{j \in V} (n_{jit} + r_{jit}) = \sum_{j \in V} (n_{ijt} + r_{ijt})
$$

2. **In-Flight Drone Auxiliary Definition:**

The auxiliary variable $p_{ijt}$ captures the number of drones simultaneously occupying link $(i,j)$ in steady state. By Little's Law, this equals the flow rate multiplied by the flight time.

For each $i, j \in V$ and $t \in T$:

$$
p_{ijt} = (n_{ijt} + r_{ijt}) \cdot \tau
$$

3. **Charging Drone Auxiliary Definition:**

The auxiliary variable $q_{it}$ captures the number of drones simultaneously charging at vertiport $i$ in steady state. This equals the total arrival rate at $i$ multiplied by the charging time.

For each $i \in V$ and $t \in T$:

$$
q_{it} = \sum_{j \in V} (n_{jit} + r_{jit}) \cdot t_c
$$

4. **Fleet Sizing Constraint (Decomposed Minimax):**

The fleet size must cover all drones in-flight, all drones charging, and all idle drones in every time period. Using the auxiliary variables, this decomposes into explicit components:

For each $t \in T$:

$$
F \geq \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V} p_{ijt} + \sum_{i \in V} q_{it} + \sum_{i \in V} w_{it}
$$

Note: Since $p_{ijt} = (n_{ijt} + r_{ijt}) \cdot \tau$ and $q_{it} = \sum_j (n_{jit} + r_{jit}) \cdot t_c$, we have $\sum_{ij} p_{ijt} + \sum_i q_{it} = \sum_{ij}(n_{ijt} + r_{ijt})(\tau + t_c)$, which is equivalent to the original aggregate constraint.

5. **Space Capacity Constraint (Using Charging Auxiliary):**

The parking capacity at each vertiport must accommodate idle drones and drones currently charging. Using the auxiliary variable $q_{it}$:

For each $i \in V$ and $t \in T$:

$$
S_i \geq w_{it} + q_{it}
$$

6. **Minimum Idle Stock Constraint:**

For each $i \in V$ and $t \in T$:

$$
w_{it} \geq 1
$$

7. **Non-negativity Constraints:**

$$
r_{ijt} \geq 0, \quad w_{it} \geq 1, \quad F \geq 0, \quad S_i \geq 0, \quad p_{ijt} \geq 0, \quad q_{it} \geq 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $r_{ijt} \geq 0$: relocation flights.
- $w_{it} \geq 1$: idle drones.
- $F, S_i \geq 0$: fleet size and parking capacity.

**Auxiliary Variables:**
- $p_{ijt} \geq 0$: in-flight drones on link $(i,j)$ in period $t$.
- $q_{it} \geq 0$: charging drones at vertiport $i$ in period $t$.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min Z = C_{fleet} \cdot F + \sum_{i} C_{space} \cdot S_i + \sum_{t,i,j} C_{reloc} \cdot r_{ijt}
$$

**Constraints:**

1. Flow conservation.
2. In-flight auxiliary: $p_{ijt} = (n_{ijt} + r_{ijt}) \cdot \tau$.
3. Charging auxiliary: $q_{it} = \sum_j (n_{jit} + r_{jit}) \cdot t_c$.
4. Fleet sizing (decomposed): $F \geq \sum_{ij} p_{ijt} + \sum_i q_{it} + \sum_i w_{it}$.
5. Space capacity: $S_i \geq w_{it} + q_{it}$.
6. Minimum idle: $w_{it} \geq 1$.
7. Non-negativity.

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP
2. Problem Scale: Medium
