### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

- $ y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $: binary variable indicating if edge $ (i,j) $ is opened, for all $ (i,j) \in E $.
- $ x_{ijk} \ge 0 $: continuous variable representing the flow on edge $ (i,j) $ destined for demand node $ k $, for all $ (i,j) \in E, k \in D $.

**Reformulation note (Indicator Constraint — eliminates Big-M):** The original formulation uses a Big-M topology constraint $ \sum_k x_{ijk} \le M \, y_{ij} $ with a loose $ M = 1000 $. In this reformulation, we replace the Big-M constraint with **indicator constraints**: for each edge $ (i,j) $ and each commodity $ k $, we impose $ y_{ij} = 0 \Rightarrow x_{ijk} = 0 $. This eliminates the need for a Big-M parameter entirely and expresses the logical relationship directly — flow on an edge is zero when that edge is closed. The indicator formulation avoids LP relaxation weakening caused by large Big-M values.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

$$
\min Z = \underbrace{\sum_{(i,j) \in E} d_{ij} \, y_{ij}}_{\text{Network Length}} + \alpha \underbrace{\sum_{k \in D} \sum_{(i,j) \in E} d_{ij} \, x_{ijk}}_{\text{Operational Cost}}
$$

Where $ \alpha = 0.01 $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Indicator Constraint (replaces Big-M topology):**

For each edge $ (i,j) \in E $ and each demand $ k \in D $:

$$
y_{ij} = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; x_{ijk} = 0
$$

Implemented via `addGenConstrIndicator(y_{ij}, 0, x_{ijk}, GRB.EQUAL, 0)`.

2. **Flow Conservation Constraint:**

For each node $ i \in N $ and demand node $ k \in D $:

$$
\sum_{j:(i,j) \in E} x_{ijk} - \sum_{j:(j,i) \in E} x_{jik} = 
\begin{cases} 
Dem_k & \text{if } i = S \\
-Dem_k & \text{if } i = k \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

3. **Binary and Non-negativity Constraints:**

$$
y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad x_{ijk} \ge 0
$$
