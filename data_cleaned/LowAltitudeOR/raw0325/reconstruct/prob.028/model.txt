### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

**Sets:**
- $V$: Set of vertiports, indexed by $i, j$.
- $T$: Set of time periods, indexed by $t$.

**Parameters:**
- $D_{ijt}$: Demand (trips/hr) from vertiport $i$ to vertiport $j$ in period $t$.
- $\tau_{fly}$: Flight time per trip.
- $\tau_{chg}$: Charging time per trip.
- $C_{fleet}$: Cost per drone.
- $C_{space,i}$: Cost per parking space at vertiport $i$.
- $C_{reloc}$: Cost per relocation (empty) flight.
- $MinIdle$: Minimum number of idle drones at each vertiport.

**Decision Variables:**

- $r_{ijt} \ge 0$: Number of empty relocation flights from vertiport $i$ to vertiport $j$ in period $t$.
- $w_{it} \ge MinIdle$: Number of idle drones at vertiport $i$ in period $t$.
- $Fleet \ge 0$: Global fleet size.
- $Spaces_i \ge 0$: Number of parking spaces at vertiport $i$.

**Auxiliary Variables (Aggregate Flow Decomposition):**

- $A_{it} \ge 0$: Total arrival rate at vertiport $i$ in period $t$ (demand + relocation arrivals).
- $O_{it} \ge 0$: Total departure rate from vertiport $i$ in period $t$ (demand + relocation departures).
- $Q_{it} \ge 0$: Number of drones charging at vertiport $i$ in period $t$.
- $F_t \ge 0$: Total network-wide flight volume in period $t$.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

Minimize the total cost consisting of fleet acquisition, infrastructure, and relocation costs:

$$
\min Z = C_{fleet} \cdot Fleet + \sum_{i \in V} C_{space,i} \cdot Spaces_i + \sum_{t \in T} \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V} C_{reloc} \cdot r_{ijt}
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Auxiliary Variable Definitions — Arrival Rate:**

For each $i \in V, t \in T$:

$$
A_{it} = \sum_{j \in V} (D_{jit} + r_{jit})
$$

2. **Auxiliary Variable Definitions — Departure Rate:**

For each $i \in V, t \in T$:

$$
O_{it} = \sum_{j \in V} (D_{ijt} + r_{ijt})
$$

3. **Auxiliary Variable Definitions — Charging Drones:**

For each $i \in V, t \in T$:

$$
Q_{it} = \tau_{chg} \cdot A_{it}
$$

4. **Auxiliary Variable Definitions — Total Flight Volume:**

For each $t \in T$:

$$
F_t = \sum_{i \in V} O_{it}
$$

5. **Steady-State Flow Balance:**

The total arrivals at each vertiport must equal its total departures within every time period:

For each $i \in V, t \in T$:

$$
A_{it} = O_{it}
$$

6. **Fleet Dimensioning Constraint:**

The fleet must accommodate all active (flying + charging) and idle drones in every period:

For each $t \in T$:

$$
Fleet \ge F_t \cdot (\tau_{fly} + \tau_{chg}) + \sum_{i \in V} w_{it}
$$

7. **Space Capacity Constraint:**

The parking capacity at each vertiport must accommodate idle and charging drones:

For each $i \in V, t \in T$:

$$
Spaces_i \ge w_{it} + Q_{it}
$$

8. **Non-negativity and Bound Constraints:**

$$
r_{ijt} \ge 0, \quad w_{it} \ge MinIdle, \quad Fleet \ge 0, \quad Spaces_i \ge 0
$$
$$
A_{it} \ge 0, \quad O_{it} \ge 0, \quad Q_{it} \ge 0, \quad F_t \ge 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $r_{ijt}$: relocation flights.
- $w_{it}$: idle drones.
- $Fleet$: global fleet size.
- $Spaces_i$: parking capacity.

**Auxiliary Variables:**
- $A_{it}$: aggregate arrival rate at vertiport $i$ in period $t$.
- $O_{it}$: aggregate departure rate from vertiport $i$ in period $t$.
- $Q_{it}$: charging drone count at vertiport $i$ in period $t$.
- $F_t$: total flight volume in period $t$.

**Objective:**
$$
\min Z = C_{fleet} \cdot Fleet + \sum_{i} C_{space,i} \cdot Spaces_i + \sum_{t,i,j} C_{reloc} \cdot r_{ijt}
$$

**Constraints:**
1. Arrival definition: $A_{it} = \sum_j (D_{jit} + r_{jit})$.
2. Departure definition: $O_{it} = \sum_j (D_{ijt} + r_{ijt})$.
3. Charging definition: $Q_{it} = \tau_{chg} \cdot A_{it}$.
4. Flight volume definition: $F_t = \sum_i O_{it}$.
5. Flow balance: $A_{it} = O_{it}$.
6. Fleet dimensioning: $Fleet \ge F_t (\tau_{fly} + \tau_{chg}) + \sum_i w_{it}$.
7. Space capacity: $Spaces_i \ge w_{it} + Q_{it}$.
8. Non-negativity and bounds.

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP
2. Problem Scale: Medium
