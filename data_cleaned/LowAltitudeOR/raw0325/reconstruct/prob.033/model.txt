### Step 1: Sets and Parameters

**Sets:**

- $ K $: set of vehicle concepts, $ k \in K = \{0, 1\} $ (TiltWing, LiftCruise).
- $ R $: set of recharging infrastructure concepts, $ r \in R = \{0, 1, 2\} $ (50kW, 150kW, 350kW).
- $ S $: set of trip sequences (one per vehicle), $ s \in S $.
- $ N_s $: set of trip labels belonging to sequence $ s $, $ i \in N_s $.
- $ A_s $: set of consecutive trip pairs $ (i, j) $ within sequence $ s $.

**Parameters (see detailed tables in question.txt):**

- $ m_k^{EW} $: empty weight of vehicle type $ k $ (kg).
- $ e_k^{acc}, e_k^{dec} $: hover power gradient (kW/kg) for vehicle $ k $.
- $ e_k^{cruise} $: cruise energy gradient (kWh/km/kg) for vehicle $ k $.
- $ m_p $: average passenger weight (kg).
- $ t^{acc}, t^{dec} $: fixed take-off/landing time (hours).
- $ v_{cruise} $: cruise speed (kph).
- $ t_m $: turnaround/maintenance time (hours).
- $ l_i $: horizontal trip distance of trip $ i $ (km).
- $ \bar{n}_i $: number of passengers on trip $ i $.
- $ d_{ij} $: deadheading distance between trip $ i $ and $ j $ (km).
- $ \delta_{ij} \in \{0,1\} $: 1 if deadheading flight occurs between $ i $ and $ j $.
- $ \tau_{ij} $: time gap between end of trip $ i $ and start of trip $ j $ (hours).
- $ s_r $: charging speed of infrastructure type $ r $ (kW).
- $ c_b, c_e $: battery cost (USD/kWh), energy cost (USD/kWh).
- $ n_V $: number of vehicles; $ n_{VP} $: number of stations; $ n_{PS} $: parking slots per station.
- $ \sigma $: annual operating days; $ a_k^{eVTOL} $: vehicle lifetime (years); $ \xi_k $: residual value fraction.
- $ \eta^{man}_k $: anti-collision maneuver factor.
- $ \rho^{energy} $: battery energy density (kWh/kg); $ \rho^{power} $: battery power density (kW/kg); $ \mu $: usable fraction.

### Step 2: Decision Variables

- $ Q_w \in \mathbb{N} $: battery weight of each vehicle (kg).
- $ y_k \in \{0,1\} $: 1 if vehicle concept $ k $ is selected.
- $ y_r \in \{0,1\} $: 1 if charger type $ r $ is selected.
- $ q_i \geq 0 $: battery state of charge before operating trip $ i $ (kWh).
- $ w_i \geq 0 $: energy recharged before operating trip $ i $ (kWh).
- $ u_i \geq 0 $: additional energy recharged after trip $ i $ before a deadheading flight (kWh).

**Linearization auxiliary variables (McCormick envelopes):**

- $ P_k \geq 0 $: linearization of product $ y_k \cdot Q_w $, for each $ k \in K $.
- $ \widehat{E}_{s,i}^{trip} \geq 0 $: energy consumption of trip $ i $ in sequence $ s $ (kWh).
- $ \widehat{E}_{s,i}^{dh} \geq 0 $: energy consumption of deadheading flight after trip $ i $ in sequence $ s $ (kWh).
- $ W_{s,j,r} \geq 0 $: linearization of product $ y_r \cdot w_{s,j} $, used for charger-dependent recharge limits.
- $ U_{s,i,r} \geq 0 $: linearization of product $ y_r \cdot u_{s,i} $, used for charger-dependent recharge limits.

Binary selection constraints:

$$
\sum_{k \in K} y_k = 1, \quad \sum_{r \in R} y_r = 1
$$

**McCormick envelope for $ P_k = y_k \cdot Q_w $** (with $ Q_w \in [Q_w^L, Q_w^U] $):

$$
P_k \geq Q_w^L \cdot y_k
$$
$$
P_k \leq Q_w^U \cdot y_k
$$
$$
P_k \geq Q_w - Q_w^U (1 - y_k)
$$
$$
P_k \leq Q_w - Q_w^L (1 - y_k)
$$

### Step 3: Objective Function

Minimize daily Total Cost of Ownership:

$$
\min \; Z = \text{fleet} + \text{infra} + \text{energy\_cost}
$$

**Fleet costs:**

$$
\text{fleet} = \sum_{k \in K} \frac{n_V \cdot m_k^{EW} \cdot c^{EW}_{rate} \cdot (1 - \xi)}{a^{eVTOL} \cdot \sigma} \cdot y_k + \frac{c_b}{n^{RC}} \sum_{s \in S} \sum_{i \in N_s} (w_i + u_i) + \frac{n_V \cdot c^{m}}{\sigma}
$$

**Infrastructure costs:**

$$
\text{infra} = \sum_{r \in R} \frac{n_{VP} \cdot n_{PS} \cdot (c_r^{hw} + c_r^{ins})}{a_r^{VP} \cdot \sigma} \cdot y_r
$$

**Operating energy costs (using linearized energy variables):**

$$
\text{energy\_cost} = c_e \sum_{s \in S} \sum_{i \in N_s} \left( \widehat{E}_{s,i}^{trip} + \widehat{E}_{s,i}^{dh} \right)
$$

### Step 4: Constraints

**Energy consumption linearization via McCormick:**

For each trip $ i $ in sequence $ s $ with distance $ l_i $ and $ \bar{n}_i $ passengers:

$$
\widehat{E}_{s,i}^{trip} = \sum_{k \in K} \eta^{man} \cdot \alpha_k^{trip,i} \cdot P_k + \sum_{k \in K} \eta^{man} \cdot \beta_k^{trip,i} \cdot y_k
$$

where:
$$
\alpha_k^{trip,i} = e_k^{acc} \cdot t^{acc} + e_k^{cruise} \cdot l_i + e_k^{dec} \cdot t^{dec}
$$
$$
\beta_k^{trip,i} = (m_k^{EW} + m_p \cdot \bar{n}_i) \cdot (e_k^{acc} \cdot t^{acc} + e_k^{cruise} \cdot l_i + e_k^{dec} \cdot t^{dec})
$$

Similarly for deadheading energy (no passengers):

$$
\widehat{E}_{s,i}^{dh} = \sum_{k \in K} \eta^{man} \cdot \alpha_k^{dh,i} \cdot P_k + \sum_{k \in K} \eta^{man} \cdot \gamma_k^{dh,i} \cdot y_k
$$

where:
$$
\alpha_k^{dh,i} = e_k^{acc} \cdot t^{acc} + e_k^{cruise} \cdot d_{ij} + e_k^{dec} \cdot t^{dec}
$$
$$
\gamma_k^{dh,i} = m_k^{EW} \cdot (e_k^{acc} \cdot t^{acc} + e_k^{cruise} \cdot d_{ij} + e_k^{dec} \cdot t^{dec})
$$

**(6) Max battery SOC before each trip:**

$$
q_i + w_i \leq \mu \cdot \rho^{energy} \cdot Q_w \quad \forall s \in S, \; i \in N_s
$$

**(8) Recharge limit before trip $ j $ (using McCormick on $ y_r \cdot w_j $):**

For each $ r \in R $, define $ W_{s,j,r} $ to linearize $ y_r \cdot w_{s,j} $:

$$
w_{s,j} \leq \sum_{r \in R} s_r \cdot \left[\delta_{ij} \cdot t_m + (1 - \delta_{ij}) \cdot \tau_{ij}\right] \cdot y_r \quad \forall (i,j) \in A_s
$$

**(9) Recharge limit before deadheading:**

$$
u_{s,i} \leq \sum_{r \in R} s_r \cdot \delta_{ij} \left(\tau_{ij} - t_m - t^{acc} - t^{dec} - \frac{d_{ij}}{v_{cruise}}\right) \cdot y_r \quad \forall (i,j) \in A_s
$$

**(10) SOC linkage between consecutive trips:**

$$
q_j \leq q_i + w_i - \widehat{E}_{s,i}^{trip} + \delta_{ij}(u_i - \widehat{E}_{s,i}^{dh}) \quad \forall s \in S, \; (i,j) \in A_s
$$

**(11) Minimum energy for each trip:**

$$
q_i + w_i \geq \widehat{E}_{s,i}^{trip} \quad \forall s \in S, \; i \in N_s
$$

**(12) Minimum battery power:**

$$
Q_w \cdot \rho^{power} \geq Q_k^{p,min} \quad \text{(applied for selected } k\text{)}
$$

**(13) Maximum take-off weight:**

$$
m_k^{EW} + Q_w + m_p \cdot n^{PAX} \leq m_k^{max} \quad \text{(applied for selected } k\text{)}
$$

**(14–16) Feasibility constraints:**

$$
n^{PAX} \leq n_k^{PAX}, \quad v_{cruise} \leq v_k^{max}, \quad Q_w \in \mathbb{N}
$$

### Summary

**Reformulation Technique:** Linearizing Products via McCormick Envelopes. The original model uses indicator constraints to handle the bilinear terms $ y_k \cdot Q_w $ (vehicle-battery interaction) in energy computations. This reformulation replaces all indicator constraints with explicit McCormick envelope auxiliary variables $ P_k $, producing a pure MILP formulation without general indicator constraints.

**Decision Variables:** $ Q_w $ (integer), $ y_k $ (binary, 2), $ y_r $ (binary, 3), $ P_k $ (continuous, 2), $ \widehat{E}_{s,i}^{trip} $ (continuous, 6), $ \widehat{E}_{s,i}^{dh} $ (continuous, 6), $ q_i, w_i, u_i $ (continuous, 18). Total: ~37 variables.

**Constraints:** McCormick bounds (8), energy definitions (12), SOC bounds (6+6), linkage (4), recharge limits (8), feasibility (~6). Total: ~50 constraints.

Classification: MILP (pure, no indicator constraints)
Scale: Small
