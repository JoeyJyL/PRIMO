
"VoltAir" Logistics is planning a UAV charging network to handle stochastic delivery demands.
**Core Problem:**
- **Stage 1 (Investment):** Build a Charging Station (CS) and determine its **Capacity** (Number of Charging Piles).
  - Investment Cost = Fixed Construction Cost + (Number of Piles * Cost per Pile).
- **Stage 2 (Operation):** Drones arrive at the CS based on demand scenarios.
  - If Drones > Piles, drones must queue.
  - Objective: Minimize **Expected Total Cost** (Investment + Weighted Queuing Penalty).

**Scenarios:**
- **Scenario A (Normal Day):** Probability 60%. Demand: **2 Drones** arrive simultaneously.
- **Scenario B (Peak Day):** Probability 40%. Demand: **5 Drones** arrive simultaneously.

**Parameters:**
- **Fixed Construction Cost:** $200.
- **Cost per Pile:** $50.
- **Queuing Penalty:** $60 per hour of waiting time.
- **Charging Duration:** 1 hour per drone.

**Queuing Logic:**
- Drones charge in batches.
- If Capacity = $k$, and Demand = $N$:
  - Batch 1 ($k$ drones): Wait 0 hours.
  - Batch 2 ($k$ drones): Wait 1 hour.
  - Batch 3 ($k$ drones): Wait 2 hours...
  - Total Wait = Sum of wait times for all $N$ drones.

**Decision Trade-off:**
- **Low Capacity (e.g., k=2):** Low investment ($300). Zero wait in Normal. High wait in Peak (3 drones queue).
- **High Capacity (e.g., k=5):** High investment ($450). Zero wait always. Expensive idle piles in Normal.
- **Goal:** Find the optimal $k$.
