### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

**Sets:**
- $S$: Set of demand scenarios (Normal, Peak).
- $B = \{0, 1, \ldots, B_{max}\}$: Set of batch indices, where $B_{max}$ is the maximum possible number of batches (equal to the maximum number of drones across all scenarios).

**Parameters:**
- $F_{fixed}$: Fixed construction cost of the charging station.
- $C_{pile}$: Cost per charging pile.
- $C_{wait}$: Queuing penalty per hour of waiting.
- $T_{charge}$: Charging duration (1 hour per drone).
- $Prob_s$: Probability of scenario $s$.
- $N_s$: Number of drones arriving in scenario $s$.
- $MaxCap$: Maximum possible number of piles.

**Decision Variables:**

- $y \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable indicating if the charging station is opened.
- $k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$: Number of charging piles (capacity).

**Auxiliary Variables (Batch Decomposition):**

- $g_{sb} \geq 0$: Continuous auxiliary variable representing the number of drones served in batch $b$ under scenario $s$. Each drone in batch $b$ waits $b \cdot T_{charge}$ hours before charging begins.
- $W_s \geq 0$: Total waiting cost in scenario $s$.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total expected cost:

$$
\min Z = (F_{fixed} \cdot y + C_{pile} \cdot k) + \sum_{s \in S} Prob_s \cdot W_s
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Station Opening Logic:**

The capacity can be positive only if the station is opened, and an open station must have at least one pile:

$$
k \leq MaxCap \cdot y
$$
$$
k \geq y
$$

2. **Batch Demand Fulfillment:**

All drones in each scenario must be assigned to exactly one batch. The total drones across all batches equals the scenario demand:

For each scenario $s \in S$:

$$
\sum_{b \in B} g_{sb} = N_s
$$

3. **Batch Capacity Constraint:**

Each batch can serve at most $k$ drones (the number of charging piles). This links the auxiliary batch variables to the capacity decision:

For each scenario $s \in S$ and batch $b \in B$:

$$
g_{sb} \leq k
$$

4. **Wait Cost Calculation via Batch Decomposition:**

Each drone assigned to batch $b$ waits $b \cdot T_{charge}$ hours. The total wait cost in scenario $s$ is the sum of wait times for all drones across all batches:

For each scenario $s \in S$:

$$
W_s = C_{wait} \cdot \sum_{b \in B} b \cdot T_{charge} \cdot g_{sb}
$$

Note: Since the objective minimizes cost and later batches incur higher wait penalties, the optimizer naturally assigns drones to earlier batches first (batch 0 up to $k$, then batch 1 up to $k$, etc.), reproducing the correct queuing behavior.

5. **Non-negativity and Domain Constraints:**

$$
y \in \{0, 1\}, \quad k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}, \quad g_{sb} \geq 0, \quad W_s \geq 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $y \in \{0, 1\}$: station opening indicator.
- $k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$: number of charging piles.

**Auxiliary Variables:**
- $g_{sb} \geq 0$: drones served in batch $b$ under scenario $s$.
- $W_s \geq 0$: total waiting cost in scenario $s$.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min Z = (F_{fixed} \cdot y + C_{pile} \cdot k) + \sum_{s \in S} Prob_s \cdot W_s
$$

**Constraints:**

1. Station opening logic: $k \leq MaxCap \cdot y$, $k \geq y$.
2. Batch demand fulfillment: $\sum_b g_{sb} = N_s$.
3. Batch capacity: $g_{sb} \leq k$.
4. Wait cost via batch decomposition: $W_s = C_{wait} \cdot \sum_b b \cdot T_{charge} \cdot g_{sb}$.
5. Non-negativity and domain constraints.

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP
2. Problem Scale: Small
