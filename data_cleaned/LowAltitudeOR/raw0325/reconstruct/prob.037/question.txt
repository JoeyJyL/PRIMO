SkyDrop Retail Inc. operates a drone-based delivery system from a central warehouse to serve an urban region.
**Goal:** Determine the optimal **drone delivery offer rates** (number of orders to accept for drone delivery) for each product category in each service zone to maximize **Total Net Profit** (Revenue - Opportunity Cost - Delivery Delay Cost - Variable Cost - Fixed Cost) over a single planning period, subject to drone fleet capacity.

**Background:**
- The retailer sells products in multiple categories with different profit margins.
- The service region is divided into zones based on distance from the warehouse.
- Each drone delivers one order per trip at constant speed, then returns to the warehouse.
- The retailer must decide how many drone deliveries to allocate to each product-zone combination.
- If demand exceeds the offer rate, unfulfilled orders incur an **opportunity cost** (lost sales/goodwill).
- If total accepted workload exceeds drone fleet capacity (normalized to 1.0), orders are delayed, incurring a **delivery delay cost** proportional to excess workload.
- Each delivery incurs a **variable operating cost**, and the fleet has a **fixed operating cost**.

**Drone Fleet & Operations:**
- Number of drones: $K = 8$
- Drone speed: $v = 40$ mph
- Cost of delivery delay: $c_w = \$100.00$ per excess workload unit
- Variable cost per delivery: $c_v = \$1.20$
- Fixed cost per drone per period: $c_F = \$0.50$

**Data - Service Zones:**
| Zone | Distance (miles) | Service Rate $\mu_i = v/(2 s_i)$ (trips/hr/drone) | Max Fleet Capacity $K \mu_i$ (orders/hr) |
|------|------------------|----------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Z1 (Short-range)  | 4  | 5.000 | 40.00 |
| Z2 (Medium-range) | 7  | 2.857 | 22.86 |
| Z3 (Long-range)   | 11 | 1.818 | 14.55 |

**Data - Product Categories:**
| Category | Unit Profit $w_{j}$ | Opportunity Cost $c_{j}$ per Lost Order |
|----------|---------------------|----------------------------------------|
| P1 (Low-margin)   | $2.00 | $0.60 |
| P2 (Mid-margin)   | $4.50 | $1.50 |
| P3 (High-margin)  | $8.00 | $3.00 |

**Data - Demand (units/period) by Zone and Category:**
| Zone \ Category | P1  | P2  | P3  |
|-----------------|-----|-----|-----|
| Z1              | 18  | 12  | 7   |
| Z2              | 14  | 9   | 5   |
| Z3              | 10  | 6   | 3   |

**Workload Normalization:** Each accepted order from zone $i$ consumes $\frac{1}{K \mu_i}$ of total fleet capacity. Fleet capacity is normalized to 1.0. Any excess workload beyond 1.0 generates delay costs at rate $c_w$.

**Objective:** Maximize total net profit. The retailer must prioritize high-margin orders and closer zones while balancing capacity overload penalties against lost-demand opportunity costs.
