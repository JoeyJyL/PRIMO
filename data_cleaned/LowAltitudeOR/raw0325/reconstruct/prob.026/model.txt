### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

**Sets:**
- $D$: Set of candidate Fulfillment Centers (FCs), indexed by $s$
- $C$: Set of customers, indexed by $i, j$
- $V = D \cup C$: Set of all nodes

**Parameters:**
- $\bar{t}_{ij}$: Nominal flight time on arc $(i, j)$
- $\hat{t}_{ij}$: Uncertainty margin for flight time on arc $(i, j)$
- $\rho$: Robustness parameter controlling the level of conservatism
- $t^R_{ij} = \bar{t}_{ij} + \rho \cdot \hat{t}_{ij}$: Robust (worst-case) flight time
- $d_i$: Parcel weight (demand) for customer $i$
- $Q$: Drone payload capacity
- $k$: Number of drones
- $\phi$: Maximum drones per FC
- $DS$: Maximum number of FCs to open
- $n = |C|$: Number of customers
- $U$: Upper bound on the maximum possible arrival time at any customer

**Decision Variables:**

- $z_s \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if FC $s$ is opened; 0 otherwise.
- $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if arc $(i,j)$ is used in some route; 0 otherwise.
- $a_i \in \{1, \ldots, n\}$: Integer variable representing the position of customer $i$ in its route (for subtour elimination).
- $w_i \geq 0$: Continuous variable representing the waiting time (latency) of customer $i$.
- $load_{ij} \geq 0$: Continuous variable representing the cargo load on arc $(i,j)$.

**Auxiliary Variables (Linearized Product for Wait Time Propagation):**

- $\delta_{ij} \geq 0$ for $i \in C, j \in C, i \neq j$: Continuous auxiliary variable representing the linearized product $(w_i + t^R_{ij}) \cdot x_{ij}$, i.e., the time contribution of predecessor $i$ to customer $j$'s arrival time.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total latency (sum of all customers' waiting times):

$$
\min Z = \sum_{i \in C} w_i
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

**1. Customer Visit Constraints:**

Each customer must be visited exactly once:

$$
\sum_{i \in V, i \neq j} x_{ij} = 1 \quad \forall j \in C
$$

**2. Flow Conservation:**

$$
\sum_{j \in V, j \neq i} x_{ji} = \sum_{j \in V, j \neq i} x_{ij} \quad \forall i \in C
$$

**3. Route Start/End:**

$$
\sum_{s \in D} \sum_{j \in C} x_{sj} = k, \quad \sum_{i \in C} \sum_{s \in D} x_{is} = k
$$

**4. FC Opening Constraints:**

$$
x_{sj} \leq z_s \quad \forall s \in D, j \in C
$$
$$
x_{is} \leq z_s \quad \forall i \in C, s \in D
$$

**5. Maximum FCs and FC Capacity:**

$$
\sum_{s \in D} z_s \leq DS
$$
$$
\sum_{j \in C} x_{sj} \leq \phi \quad \forall s \in D
$$

**6. MTZ Subtour Elimination:**

$$
a_j \geq a_i + 1 - n(1 - x_{ij}) \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j
$$

**7. Position Reset from FC:**

$$
a_j \leq 1 + n(1 - x_{sj}) \quad \forall s \in D, j \in C
$$

**8. Linearized Product Constraints (Replacing Big-M Wait Time Propagation):**

Instead of the Big-M inequality $w_j \geq w_i + t^R_{ij} - M(1 - x_{ij})$, we introduce auxiliary variables $\delta_{ij}$ that linearize the product $(w_i + t^R_{ij}) \cdot x_{ij}$ via McCormick envelope constraints.

For each arc $(i,j)$ where $i \in C, j \in C, i \neq j$:

$$
\delta_{ij} \leq U \cdot x_{ij}
$$
$$
\delta_{ij} \leq w_i + t^R_{ij}
$$
$$
\delta_{ij} \geq (w_i + t^R_{ij}) - U \cdot (1 - x_{ij})
$$
$$
\delta_{ij} \geq 0
$$

**9. Arrival Time Definition via Auxiliary Variables:**

Since exactly one arc enters each customer $j$, the arrival time equals the time contribution from the unique active predecessor. For FC→customer arcs, the contribution is simply $t^R_{sj} \cdot x_{sj}$ (parameter × binary, already linear):

$$
w_j = \sum_{s \in D} t^R_{sj} \cdot x_{sj} + \sum_{i \in C, i \neq j} \delta_{ij} \quad \forall j \in C
$$

**10. No Inter-Depot Arcs:**

$$
x_{s_1 s_2} = 0 \quad \forall s_1, s_2 \in D, s_1 \neq s_2
$$

**11. Payload Capacity and Load Flow:**

$$
load_{sj} \leq Q \cdot x_{sj}, \quad load_{sj} \geq d_j \cdot x_{sj} \quad \forall s \in D, j \in C
$$
$$
load_{ij} \leq Q \cdot x_{ij} \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j
$$
$$
load_{is} = 0 \quad \forall i \in C, s \in D
$$
$$
\sum_{s \in D} load_{si} + \sum_{j \in C, j \neq i} load_{ji} - \sum_{j \in C, j \neq i} load_{ij} - \sum_{s \in D} load_{is} = d_i \quad \forall i \in C
$$

**12. Variable Domains:**

$$
z_s \in \{0, 1\}, \quad x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad a_i \in \{1, \ldots, n\}, \quad w_i \geq 0, \quad \delta_{ij} \geq 0, \quad load_{ij} \geq 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $z_s \in \{0, 1\}$: FC opening decisions
- $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: Arc selection
- $a_i$: Customer position (MTZ subtour elimination)
- $w_i \geq 0$: Customer waiting time (latency)
- $load_{ij} \geq 0$: Arc cargo load

**Auxiliary Variables:**
- $\delta_{ij} \geq 0$: Linearized product $(w_i + t^R_{ij}) \cdot x_{ij}$ for customer-to-customer arcs

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min Z = \sum_{i \in C} w_i
$$

**Constraints:**
1. Customer visit (in-degree = 1)
2. Flow conservation
3. Route start/end balance
4. FC opening logic
5. Max FCs and FC capacity
6. MTZ subtour elimination
7. Position reset from FC
8. Linearized product (McCormick): $\delta_{ij} \leq U x_{ij}$, $\delta_{ij} \leq w_i + t^R_{ij}$, $\delta_{ij} \geq (w_i + t^R_{ij}) - U(1 - x_{ij})$
9. Arrival time definition: $w_j = \sum_s t^R_{sj} x_{sj} + \sum_i \delta_{ij}$
10. No inter-depot arcs
11. Payload capacity and load flow
12. Variable domains

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MINLP
2. Problem Scale: Small
