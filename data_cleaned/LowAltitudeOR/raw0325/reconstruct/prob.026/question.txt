SkyDrone Logistics is designing a drone-based last-mile delivery network to serve urban customers from multiple Fulfillment Centers (FCs).
**Goal:** Select which FCs to open and determine optimal drone routes to minimize the **Total Customer Waiting Time (Latency)** under a **Worst-Case Flight Time Scenario**.

**Problem Setting:**
- Drones are launched from opened FCs, serve multiple customers on a single trip, and return to an FC.
- Each drone can carry multiple packages but has limited payload capacity.
- Energy consumption depends nonlinearly on the drone's current load (heavier load = more energy).
- Flight times are uncertain due to wind and weather conditions. We optimize for the worst-case scenario.

**Fleet & Capacity:**
- Number of drones available: 2
- Drone payload capacity: 2.0 kg
- Drone battery capacity: 500 Wh
- Maximum FCs to open: 2
- Each FC can host at most 2 drones.

**Data - Candidate Fulfillment Centers:**
- **FC1 (Central Hub):** Location (50, 50)
- **FC2 (South Depot):** Location (20, 10)

**Data - Customers:**
| Customer | Location (X,Y) | Parcel Weight (kg) |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| C1       | (30, 40)       | 0.5               |
| C2       | (60, 30)       | 0.4               |
| C3       | (45, 70)       | 0.6               |
| C4       | (70, 60)       | 0.5               |

**Flight Time Data (in minutes):**
- Flight time between any two points is calculated as Euclidean distance / drone speed.
- Nominal flight times are subject to uncertainty (±20% deviation due to wind).

**Energy Consumption Model:**
- Energy consumption on arc (i,j) with load $l$: $E = k' \cdot (W + M + l)^{1.5} \cdot t_{ij}$
- Where: $W$ = drone frame weight (1.5 kg), $M$ = battery weight (0.5 kg), $k'$ = energy coefficient (0.05)
- Under worst-case uncertainty, flight times increase by the uncertainty margin (20%).

**Objective:**
Minimize total latency = sum of all customers' waiting times (time from dispatch until delivery).
