"MediDrone" is planning a robust delivery network for urgent medical supplies (e.g., anti-venom) in a rural area.
**Core Objective:** Unlike standard logistics that save fuel (distance), we must minimize the **Total Waiting Time (Latency)** of all patients.
   - **Latency:** The arrival time at a customer.
   - **Objective:** Minimize $\sum (\text{Arrival Times})$ + Fixed Costs.

**Uncertainty:**
- Flight times are uncertain due to wind. We use **Worst-Case Travel Times** for planning to ensure robustness.

**Decision Problem:**
- Select Fulfillment Centers (FC) to open.
- Route drones to customers to minimize the sum of arrival times.

**Data:**
*Candidate FCs:*
- **FC1:** (0, 0) | Fixed Cost: 20
- **FC2:** (10, 0) | Fixed Cost: 20

*Customers (Urgent Patients):*
- **C1:** (1, 0) | Near FC1
- **C2:** (2, 0) | Near FC1
- **C3:** (9, 0) | Near FC2
- **C4:** (8, 0) | Near FC2

*Parameters:*
- Drone Speed: 1 unit/min.
- Drone Capacity: Sufficient for all (Single trip for simplicity, or multi-stop).
- Service Time: 0.

**Scenario Analysis:**
- **Option A (Open Only FC1):**
  - Route: FC1 $\to$ C1 $\to$ C2 $\to$ C4 $\to$ C3.
  - Arrival Times: C1(1), C2(2), C4(8), C3(9).
  - Total Latency: $1+2+8+9 = 20$.
  - Cost: $20 (Fixed) + 20 (Latency) = 40$.
- **Option B (Open FC1 & FC2):**
  - Route 1: FC1 $\to$ C1 $\to$ C2. Times: C1(1), C2(2). Sum=3.
  - Route 2: FC2 $\to$ C3 $\to$ C4. Times: C3(1), C4(2). Sum=3.
  - Total Latency: $3+3=6$.
  - Cost: $40 (Fixed) + 6 (Latency) = 46$.
- **Conclusion:** While Option B is much faster for patients (Latency 6 vs 20), the high Fixed Cost of FCs might make Option A optimal if the budget is tight.
