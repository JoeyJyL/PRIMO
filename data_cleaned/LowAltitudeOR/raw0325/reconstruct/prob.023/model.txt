### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

**Sets:**
- $D$: Set of candidate depots (Fulfillment Centers).
- $C$: Set of customers (patients).
- $N = D \cup C$: Set of all nodes.

**Parameters:**
- $F_d$: Fixed cost of opening depot $d$.
- $\tau_{ij}$: Worst-case travel time from node $i$ to node $j$.
- $U$: A sufficiently large upper bound on the maximum possible arrival time at any customer.

**Decision Variables:**

- $y_d \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable indicating if depot $d$ is opened.
- $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable indicating if a drone travels directly from node $i$ to node $j$.
- $t_i \geq 0$: Continuous variable representing the arrival time (latency) at node $i$.

**Auxiliary Variables (Linearized Product):**

- $\delta_{ij} \geq 0$: Continuous auxiliary variable for each arc $(i,j)$ where $j \in C$. It represents the linearized product $(t_i + \tau_{ij}) \cdot x_{ij}$, i.e., the time contribution of arc $(i,j)$ to node $j$'s arrival time.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which consists of the fixed costs for opening depots and the total latency experienced by the customers.

$$
\min Z = \sum_{d \in D} F_d \, y_d + \sum_{i \in C} t_i
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Flow Conservation Constraints:**

Each customer must be visited exactly once.

For each customer $j \in C$:

$$
\sum_{i \in N, i \neq j} x_{ij} = 1
$$

For each customer $i \in C$:

$$
\sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ij} = 1
$$

2. **Depot Association Constraints:**

Routes must start at opened depots. If a depot is not opened, no flow can leave it.

For each depot $d \in D$:

$$
\sum_{j \in C} x_{dj} \leq M \, y_d
$$

where $M = |C|$.

3. **Depot Flow Balance:**

For each depot $d \in D$:

$$
\sum_{j \in C} x_{dj} = \sum_{j \in C} x_{jd}
$$

4. **Depot Start Time:**

For each depot $d \in D$:

$$
t_d = 0
$$

5. **Linearized Product Constraints (Replacing Big-M Time Propagation):**

Instead of using the Big-M inequality $t_j \geq t_i + \tau_{ij} - M(1 - x_{ij})$, we introduce auxiliary variables $\delta_{ij}$ that linearize the product $(t_i + \tau_{ij}) \cdot x_{ij}$ via McCormick envelope constraints.

For each arc $(i,j)$ where $j \in C$ and $i \neq j$:

$$
\delta_{ij} \leq U \cdot x_{ij}
$$
$$
\delta_{ij} \leq t_i + \tau_{ij}
$$
$$
\delta_{ij} \geq (t_i + \tau_{ij}) - U \cdot (1 - x_{ij})
$$
$$
\delta_{ij} \geq 0
$$

6. **Arrival Time Definition via Auxiliary Variables:**

Since exactly one arc enters each customer $j$ (from Constraint 1), the arrival time equals the single non-zero contribution:

For each customer $j \in C$:

$$
t_j = \sum_{i \in N, i \neq j} \delta_{ij}
$$

7. **No Inter-Depot Arcs:**

For all $d_1, d_2 \in D$:

$$
x_{d_1 d_2} = 0
$$

8. **Binary and Non-negativity Constraints:**

$$
y_d \in \{0, 1\}, \quad x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad t_i \geq 0, \quad \delta_{ij} \geq 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $y_d \in \{0, 1\}$: depot opening indicator.
- $x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: arc traversal indicator.
- $t_i \geq 0$: arrival time at node $i$.
- $\delta_{ij} \geq 0$: auxiliary variable for linearized product $(t_i + \tau_{ij}) \cdot x_{ij}$.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min Z = \sum_{d \in D} F_d \, y_d + \sum_{i \in C} t_i
$$

**Constraints:**

1. Flow conservation (each customer visited exactly once).
2. Depot association ($x_{dj}$ only if $y_d = 1$).
3. Depot flow balance.
4. Depot start time: $t_d = 0$.
5. Linearized product: $\delta_{ij} \leq U \cdot x_{ij}$, $\delta_{ij} \leq t_i + \tau_{ij}$, $\delta_{ij} \geq (t_i + \tau_{ij}) - U(1 - x_{ij})$.
6. Arrival time definition: $t_j = \sum_i \delta_{ij}$.
7. No inter-depot arcs.
8. Binary and non-negativity constraints.

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP
2. Problem Scale: Medium
