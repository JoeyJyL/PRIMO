"RapidDrone" Logistics is designing a double-layer urban air delivery network to serve a dense city district.
**Network Structure:**
- **Layer 1 (High Altitude):** Heavy-lift drones fly from a central **Supply Depot (S)** to selected **Transshipment Hubs (H)**.
- **Layer 2 (Low Altitude):** Small delivery drones fly from open **Transshipment Hubs (H)** to **Customer Zones (C)**.

**Decision Problem:**
1. **Hub Location:** Select which candidate Hubs to open (Fixed Cost).
2. **Assignment:** Assign each Customer Zone to exactly one open Hub.
3. **Routing:** Determine the flow from Supply Depot to open Hubs.

**Objectives (Weighted Sum):**
- Minimize **Fixed Costs** of opening Hubs.
- Minimize **Total Flight Distance** (Supply $\to$ Hub $\to$ Customer).
- **Load Balancing (BSD Proxy):** To mimic the paper's "Betweenness Standard Deviation" for reducing congestion, we add a quadratic penalty cost based on the load of each hub:
  $$ \text{Congestion Cost} = 2.0 \times (\text{Hub Load})^2 $$

**Data - Locations (km):**
*Supply Depot:*
- **S1:** (0, 0)

*Candidate Hubs ($H$):*
- **H1:** (5, 5)   | Fixed Cost: 500
- **H2:** (5, -5)  | Fixed Cost: 500
- **H3:** (10, 0)  | Fixed Cost: 600

*Customer Zones ($C$):*
- **C1:** (6, 6)   | Demand: 10 pkgs | Near H1
- **C2:** (6, 4)   | Demand: 10 pkgs | Near H1/H3
- **C3:** (6, -6)  | Demand: 10 pkgs | Near H2
- **C4:** (11, 1)  | Demand: 10 pkgs | Near H3

**Parameters:**
- **Transport Cost:** $1.0 per km-pkg.
- **Goal:** Minimize Total Cost = Fixed + Transport + Congestion.
