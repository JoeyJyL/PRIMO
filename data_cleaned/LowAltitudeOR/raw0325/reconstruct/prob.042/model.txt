1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP
2. Problem Size: Small

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

- $ I_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $: whether flight $ i \in \mathcal{F} $ takes path $ j \in \mathcal{P} $.
- $ t_i \geq 0 $: assigned departure time (seconds) of flight $ i $.
- $ d_i^1 \geq 0 $ (auxiliary): delay of flight $ i $ within the threshold segment $ [0, d] $ (seconds).
- $ d_i^2 \geq 0 $ (auxiliary): delay of flight $ i $ beyond the threshold $ d $ (seconds).
- $ D_i \geq 0 $: total delay cost ($) of flight $ i $, defined as $ D_i = r_i \cdot d_i^1 + R_i \cdot d_i^2 $.
- $ z_c \in \{0, 1\} $: equal to 1 if both flights in conflict $ c = (i,j,m,n) $ take their respective conflicting paths.
- $ X_c \in \{0, 1\} $: equal to 1 if flight $ i $ passes the conflict region before flight $ m $ for conflict $ c $.
- $ x_c \in \{0, 1\} $: equal to 1 if flight $ m $ passes the conflict region before flight $ i $ for conflict $ c $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

Minimize total system cost = sum of delay costs + sum of path costs:

$$
\min \sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} D_i + \sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} c_{ij} \cdot I_{ij}
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

**1. Auxiliary Variable Delay Decomposition (replaces piecewise linear epigraph):**

Instead of using two lower-bound constraints to define $ D_i $ as the maximum of two linear pieces, we decompose the total delay into two segments using auxiliary variables $ d_i^1 $ and $ d_i^2 $.

For each $ i \in \mathcal{F} $:

$$
d_i^1 + d_i^2 = t_i - s_i
$$

$$
0 \leq d_i^1 \leq d
$$

$$
d_i^2 \geq 0
$$

$$
D_i = r_i \cdot d_i^1 + R_i \cdot d_i^2
$$

Since $ R_i > r_i $ and the objective minimizes $ D_i $, the optimizer will always fill the cheaper segment $ d_i^1 $ first (up to the threshold $ d $) before allocating any delay to $ d_i^2 $. This naturally reproduces the piecewise linear cost without needing an epigraph formulation.

**2. Conflict Resolution — Flight $ i $ passes first:**

For each $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
t_i + e_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - X_c) + B_c + t_m
$$

**3. Conflict Resolution — Flight $ m $ passes first:**

For each $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
t_m + E_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - x_c) + b_c + t_i
$$

**4. Linearization of $ z_c = I_{ij} \cdot I_{mn} $:**

For each $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
z_c \leq I_{ij}
$$

$$
z_c \leq I_{mn}
$$

$$
z_c + 1 \geq I_{ij} + I_{mn}
$$

**5. Conflict Ordering:**

For each $ c \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
X_c + x_c = z_c
$$

**6. Departure Time Feasibility:**

For each $ i \in \mathcal{F} $:

$$
t_i \geq s_i
$$

**7. Path Selection:**

For each $ i \in \mathcal{F} $:

$$
\sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} I_{ij} = 1
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Sets:**

- $ \mathcal{F} = \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $: UAV flights.
- $ \mathcal{P} = \{0, 1\} $: alternative paths.
- $ \mathcal{C} = \{C1, C2, C3, C4\} $: spatial conflict pairs, each $ c = (i, j, m, n) $.

**Parameters:**

- $ s_i $: scheduled departure time of flight $ i $ (seconds).
- $ c_{ij} $: path cost of flight $ i $ taking path $ j $.
- $ r_i $: unit delay cost ($/s) when delay $ \leq d $.
- $ R_i = 2r_i $: unit delay cost ($/s) when delay $ > d $.
- $ d = 300 $: delay cost threshold (seconds).
- $ t_0 = 10 $: safety separation buffer (seconds).
- $ M = 10000 $: Big-M constant.
- $ b_c, e_c, B_c, E_c $: travel times to entry/exit of conflict regions (seconds).

**Decision Variables:**

- $ I_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $, $ j \in \mathcal{P} $.
- $ t_i \geq 0 $ continuous for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ d_i^1 \in [0, d] $ continuous for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $ (auxiliary: delay in segment 1).
- $ d_i^2 \geq 0 $ continuous for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $ (auxiliary: delay in segment 2).
- $ D_i \geq 0 $ continuous for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ z_c, X_c, x_c \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ c \in \mathcal{C} $.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min \sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} D_i + \sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} c_{ij} \cdot I_{ij}
$$

**Constraints:**

1. $ d_i^1 + d_i^2 = t_i - s_i $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $
2. $ 0 \leq d_i^1 \leq d $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $
3. $ d_i^2 \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $
4. $ D_i = r_i \cdot d_i^1 + R_i \cdot d_i^2 $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $
5. $ t_i + e_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - X_c) + B_c + t_m $ for all $ c \in \mathcal{C} $
6. $ t_m + E_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - x_c) + b_c + t_i $ for all $ c \in \mathcal{C} $
7. $ z_c \leq I_{ij},\ z_c \leq I_{mn},\ z_c + 1 \geq I_{ij} + I_{mn} $ for all $ c \in \mathcal{C} $
8. $ X_c + x_c = z_c $ for all $ c \in \mathcal{C} $
9. $ t_i \geq s_i $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $
10. $ \sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} I_{ij} = 1 $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $
11. $ I_{ij}, z_c, X_c, x_c \in \{0, 1\} $; $ t_i, d_i^1, d_i^2, D_i \geq 0 $
