### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ y_i $: a binary variable indicating if Skyport $ i $ is opened.
- $ x_r $: a binary variable indicating if Route $ r $ is active (served by UAM).

Thus, we have:

- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $ (representing S1, S2, S3, S4).
- $ x_r \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ r \in \{1, 2, 3\} $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the expected total number of UAM passengers. The ridership for a route depends on the probability of choosing UAM ($ P(UAM)_r $) multiplied by the total demand ($ D_r $), but this only counts if the route is active ($ x_r = 1 $).

First, let's calculate the constants for each route based on the Logit Model:
$ V = -0.5 \times \text{Time} - 0.1 \times \text{Cost} + \text{AcceptanceFactor} $

* **Route 1 (S1 $\to$ S3):**
    * $ V_{ground} = -0.5(60) - 0.1(10) + 0 = -31 $
    * $ V_{uam} = -0.5(15) - 0.1(50) + 0 = -12.5 $
    * $ P(UAM)_1 = \frac{e^{-12.5}}{e^{-12.5} + e^{-31}} \approx 1.0 $
    * Passengers $\approx 2000 \times 1.0 = 2000$

* **Route 2 (S2 $\to$ S3):**
    * $ V_{ground} = -0.5(50) - 0.1(8) + 0 = -25.8 $
    * $ V_{uam} = -0.5(12) - 0.1(45) + 0 = -10.5 $
    * $ P(UAM)_2 = \frac{e^{-10.5}}{e^{-10.5} + e^{-25.8}} \approx 1.0 $
    * Passengers $\approx 1500 \times 1.0 = 1500$

* **Route 3 (S1 $\to$ S4):**
    * $ V_{ground} = -0.5(40) - 0.1(12) + 0 = -21.2 $
    * $ V_{uam} = -0.5(20) - 0.1(40) + 2 = -12 $
    * $ P(UAM)_3 = \frac{e^{-12}}{e^{-12} + e^{-21.2}} \approx 1.0 $
    * Passengers $\approx 500 \times 1.0 = 500$

The objective function is:

$$
\max 2000 x_1 + 1500 x_2 + 500 x_3
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Budget (Skyport Count) Constraint:**

Exactly 2 locations must be selected to build Skyports.

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} y_i = 2
$$

2. **Route Activation Logic Constraints:**

A route is served by UAM only if **BOTH** origin and destination Skyports are open.

* **Route 1 (S1 - S3):** Requires $ y_1 $ and $ y_3 $.
    $$ x_1 \le y_1 $$
    $$ x_1 \le y_3 $$

* **Route 2 (S2 - S3):** Requires $ y_2 $ and $ y_3 $.
    $$ x_2 \le y_2 $$
    $$ x_2 \le y_3 $$

* **Route 3 (S1 - S4):** Requires $ y_1 $ and $ y_4 $.
    $$ x_3 \le y_1 $$
    $$ x_3 \le y_4 $$

3. **Airspace Corridor Conflict Constraint:**

S1 and S3 cannot both be operational due to overlapping flight corridors over the city center.

$$
y_1 + y_3 \le 1
$$

4. **Binary Constraints:**

The decision variables must be binary.

For all $ i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $ and $ r \in \{1, 2, 3\} $:

$$
y_i \in \{0, 1\}, \quad x_r \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $.
- $ x_r \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ r \in \{1, 2, 3\} $.

**Objective Function:**

Maximize:

$$
\max 2000 x_1 + 1500 x_2 + 500 x_3
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Budget (Skyport Count) Constraint:**

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{4} y_i = 2
$$

2. **Route Activation Logic Constraints:**

$$
x_1 \le y_1, \quad x_1 \le y_3
$$
$$
x_2 \le y_2, \quad x_2 \le y_3
$$
$$
x_3 \le y_1, \quad x_3 \le y_4
$$

3. **Airspace Corridor Conflict Constraint:**

$$
y_1 + y_3 \le 1
$$

4. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
y_i, x_r \in \{0, 1\}
$$
