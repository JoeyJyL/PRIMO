### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ y_k $: a binary variable indicating if Depot $ k $ is open.
- $ z_{ik} $: a binary variable indicating if Village $ i $ is served by a drone from Depot $ k $.
- $ v_i $: a binary variable indicating if Village $ i $ is served by a truck.
- $ x_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating if the truck travels from node $ i $ to node $ j $.
- $ u_i $: a continuous variable used for subtour elimination constraints (auxiliary variable).

Thus, we have:

- $ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in D $.
- $ z_{ik} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in V, k \in D $.
- $ v_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in V $.
- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i, j \in N $.
- $ u_i \ge 0 $ for all $ i \in N $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which consists of the fixed costs for opening depots, the drone operational costs, and the truck travel costs.

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{k \in D} F_k y_k + \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{k \in D} c_{ik}^{drone} z_{ik} + \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in N} c_{ij}^{truck} x_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ F_k $ is the fixed cost of opening Depot $ k $.
- $ c_{ik}^{drone} $ is the drone round-trip cost from Depot $ k $ to Village $ i $.
- $ c_{ij}^{truck} $ is the truck travel cost from node $ i $ to node $ j $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Service Guarantee Constraint:**

Every village must be served exactly once, either by a drone or by a truck.

For each Village $ i \in V $:

$$
\sum_{k \in D} z_{ik} + v_i = 1
$$

2. **Drone Feasibility Constraint:**

A village can only be served by a drone from Depot $ k $ if Depot $ k $ is open.

For each Village $ i \in V $ and Depot $ k \in D $:

$$
z_{ik} \le y_k
$$

(Note: Pre-processing filters ensure $ z_{ik} = 0 $ if out of range or demand exceeds drone capacity).

3. **Truck Emission Constraint:**

Due to mountain ecosystem emission control regulations, at most 2 villages can be served by truck.

$$
\sum_{i \in V} v_i \le 2
$$

4. **Truck Routing Constraints:**

The truck route must visit all villages assigned to the truck ($ v_i = 1 $).

For each Village $ i \in V $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N} x_{ij} = v_i
$$
$$
\sum_{j \in N} x_{ji} = v_i
$$

Truck routes must originate and terminate at open depots.

For each Depot $ k \in D $:

$$
\sum_{j \in V} x_{kj} \le M y_k
$$
$$
\sum_{j \in V} x_{kj} = \sum_{j \in V} x_{jk}
$$

5. **Subtour Elimination Constraint:**

To ensure valid routing without isolated loops (using MTZ formulation).

For all $ i, j \in V $:

$$
u_i - u_j + |N| x_{ij} \le |N| - 1
$$

6. **Depot Availability:**

At least one depot must be open.

$$
\sum_{k \in D} y_k \ge 1
$$

7. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
y_k, z_{ik}, v_i, x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad u_i \ge 0
$$
