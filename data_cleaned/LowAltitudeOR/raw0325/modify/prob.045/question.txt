# Flying Sidekick Traveling Salesman Problem (FSTSP) - Original Formulation

## Background
Amazon and other companies have proposed using unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs/drones) for last-mile parcel delivery. This paper introduces the **Flying Sidekick Traveling Salesman Problem (FSTSP)**, where a drone works in tandem with a delivery truck. The truck follows a TSP route while the drone is launched from the truck to deliver packages to nearby customers, then returns to the truck at a later stop.

The objective is to minimize the total delivery time (makespan) — the time at which both the truck and drone return to the depot.

## Problem Description
The company must determine:
1. The truck's delivery route (visiting a subset of customers)
2. Which customers are served by the drone (UAV sorties)
3. The launch node, customer node, and retrieval node for each drone sortie

**Key assumptions:**
- The drone serves **one customer per sortie**
- The drone must return to the truck (not the depot) after each sortie, unless it's the last sortie
- The drone cannot return to the same node it launched from
- The truck must visit both the launch and retrieval nodes for each sortie
- Some customers may be ineligible for drone delivery (e.g., heavy parcels)

## Simplified Scenario

We have **1 depot** (node 0 = start, node 6 = end depot) and **5 customers** (nodes 1, 2, 3, 4, 5).

- **Drone-eligible customers**: C' = {1, 2, 3, 4} (customer 5 is truck-only due to heavy parcel)
- The drone can serve **one customer per sortie**

## Parameters

### Node Coordinates (km)
| Node | X    | Y    | Description          |
|------|------|------|----------------------|
| 0    | 0.0  | 0.0  | Starting depot       |
| 1    | 2.0  | 5.0  | Customer 1 (drone OK)|
| 2    | 5.0  | 2.0  | Customer 2 (drone OK)|
| 3    | 7.0  | 6.0  | Customer 3 (drone OK)|
| 4    | 1.0  | 8.0  | Customer 4 (drone OK)|
| 5    | 9.0  | 3.0  | Customer 5 (truck only, heavy parcel)|
| 6    | 0.0  | 0.0  | Ending depot         |

### Travel Times
- **Truck speed**: 25 miles/h (≈ 40.23 km/h), travels on Manhattan metric roads
- **Drone speed**: 50 miles/h (≈ 80.47 km/h), flies Euclidean (straight-line) paths
- Truck travel time s_ij = Manhattan_distance(i,j) / truck_speed (hours)
- Drone travel time σ_ij = Euclidean_distance(i,j) / drone_speed (hours)

### Drone Parameters
- **Flight endurance**: e = 0.5 hours (30 minutes)
- **Launch time**: s_L = 1/60 hours (1 minute) — time for driver to load parcel and launch drone
- **Recovery time**: s_R = 1/60 hours (1 minute) — time for driver to recover drone

## Decision
Find the optimal truck route and drone sortie assignments to minimize total delivery time (makespan), subject to:
- Each customer served exactly once (by truck or drone)
- Customer 5 must be served by truck (not drone-eligible)
- Drone endurance constraint: each sortie ≤ e hours
- Time synchronization: truck and drone must meet at retrieval nodes
- Drone can only launch/retrieve at nodes visited by the truck (including the end depot for the last sortie)
- Drone cannot return to the same node it launched from
- Drone may launch from the depot (node 0) for the first sortie
- **Dependency constraint**: Due to delivery zone bundling requirements in the eastern corridor, if Customer 3 is assigned to drone delivery, then Customer 2 must also be assigned to drone delivery. This reflects the policy that drone operations near the eastern zone must include all customers along the flight path to maximize sortie efficiency.
