Identifying the decision variables, objective function, and constraints for the given optimization problem.

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the two-stage stochastic drone relief network.

Let's denote:

- $ x_{k} $: a binary variable indicating whether facility $ k $ is opened (1) or not (0).
- $ y_{dk} $: a binary variable indicating whether drone $ d $ is assigned to facility $ k $ (1) or not (0).
- $ z_{idk} $: a binary variable indicating whether drone $ d $ (assigned to $ k $) is scheduled to visit site $ i $ (1) or not (0).
- $ v_{k} $: the inventory quantity prepositioned at facility $ k $.
- $ w_{ik}^{\xi} $: the total items delivered from facility $ k $ to site $ i $ in scenario $ \xi $.
- $ q_{idk}^{\xi} $: the items delivered by drone $ d $ from facility $ k $ to site $ i $ in scenario $ \xi $.
- $ u_{i}^{\xi} $: the unsatisfied demand at site $ i $ in scenario $ \xi $.
- $ r_{k}^{\xi} $: the unused inventory at facility $ k $ in scenario $ \xi $.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_{k}, y_{dk}, z_{idk} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in K, d \in D, i \in I $.
- $ v_{k} \geq 0 $ for all $ k \in K $.
- $ w_{ik}^{\xi}, q_{idk}^{\xi}, u_{i}^{\xi}, r_{k}^{\xi} \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in I, k \in K, d \in D, \xi \in \Xi $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which includes the first-stage costs (opening, inventory, management) and the expected second-stage recourse costs (delivery, penalties).

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{k \in K} (C_{OPEN} x_k + C_{INV} v_k) + \sum_{i \in I}\sum_{d \in D}\sum_{k \in K} C_{MANA} z_{idk} + \sum_{\xi \in \Xi} p_{\xi} (\text{Recourse Cost}_{\xi})
$$

Where:

- $ C_{OPEN} $ is the cost to open a facility.
- $ C_{INV} $ is the unit cost of prepositioned inventory.
- $ C_{MANA} $ is the management cost for scheduling a drone visit.
- $ p_{\xi} $ is the probability of scenario $ \xi $.
- $ \text{Recourse Cost}_{\xi} $ includes delivery costs and penalties for unsatisfied demand and unused inventory.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Assignment Logic Constraints:**

A drone must be assigned to an open facility, each drone can be assigned to at most one facility, and a drone can only visit a site if it is assigned to that facility.

For all $ d \in D, k \in K, i \in I $:

$$
y_{dk} \leq x_k
$$

$$
\sum_{k \in K} y_{dk} \leq 1
$$

$$
z_{idk} \leq y_{dk}
$$

2. **Coverage Constraint:**

Each demand site $ i $ must be visited by at least one drone.

For each site $ i \in I $:

$$
\sum_{d \in D}\sum_{k \in K} z_{idk} \geq 1
$$

3. **Range Constraint:**

The total distance traveled by drone $ d $ must not exceed its maximum range $ B_{MAX} $.

For all $ d \in D $:

$$
\text{Total Distance}_d \leq B_{MAX}
$$

4. **Inventory Capacity Constraint:**

The inventory at facility $ k $ cannot exceed its capacity $ W_{cap} = 40 $, and can only exist if the facility is open.

For each facility $ k \in K $:

$$
v_k \leq W_{cap} x_k
$$

5. **Demand Balance Constraint (Stage 2):**

The total items delivered plus unsatisfied demand must equal the demand at the site.

For all $ i \in I, \xi \in \Xi $:

$$
\sum_{k \in K} w_{ik}^{\xi} + u_{i}^{\xi} = Demand_{i}^{\xi}
$$

6. **Supply Balance Constraint (Stage 2):**

The total items delivered plus unused inventory must equal the prepositioned inventory.

For all $ k \in K, \xi \in \Xi $:

$$
\sum_{i \in I} w_{ik}^{\xi} + r_{k}^{\xi} = v_k
$$

7. **Drone Flow Constraint (Stage 2):**

The total flow from $ k $ to $ i $ is the sum of flows by individual drones.

For all $ i \in I, k \in K, \xi \in \Xi $:

$$
w_{ik}^{\xi} = \sum_{d \in D} q_{idk}^{\xi}
$$

8. **Payload Limit Constraint (Stage 2):**

A drone cannot carry more than its maximum payload $ L_{MAX} $, and can only carry items if scheduled.

For all $ i \in I, d \in D, k \in K, \xi \in \Xi $:

$$
q_{idk}^{\xi} \leq L_{MAX} z_{idk}
$$
