
The Regional Disaster Management Bureau needs to design a drone-supported humanitarian logistics network to prepare for potential earthquakes.
The decision-making process is divided into two stages:
   - **Stage 1 (Pre-disaster):** Determine location of relief facilities, preposition inventory, assign drones to facilities, and plan drone visits to potential disaster sites.
   - **Stage 2 (Post-disaster):** Based on the actual damage scenario, determine the quantity of relief items (e.g., blood, medicine) delivered to each site.

**Uncertainty:**
The demand at disaster sites is uncertain. We consider two potential scenarios:
- **Scenario 1 (Moderate):** Limited damage, lower demand.
- **Scenario 2 (Severe):** Widespread damage, higher demand.
The goal is to minimize the total cost (Fixed Opening + Inventory + Path Management + Expected Post-disaster Costs including Penalties).

**Parameters:**
- **Drone Capacity ($L_{MAX}$):** 15 units per trip.
- **Drone Battery Range ($B_{MAX}$):** 50 km.
- **Facility Inventory Capacity ($W_l$):** 40 units (reduced from 100 due to limited cold-chain storage infrastructure at field sites).
- **Drone Fleet ($D$):** 2 Drones (D1, D2).

**Locations & Coordinates:**
*Candidate Facilities ($K$):*
- Site A (ID: 1): (0, 0) | Opening Cost: 500
- Site B (ID: 2): (10, 0) | Opening Cost: 600

*Disaster Demand Sites ($I$):*
- Zone 1 (ID: 1): (0, 5)
- Zone 2 (ID: 2): (5, 5)
- Zone 3 (ID: 3): (10, 5)

**Scenario Data (Demand $D_{i}^{\xi}$):**
| Scenario | Prob. | Zone 1 | Zone 2 | Zone 3 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| S1 (Moderate) | 0.5 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| S2 (Severe) | 0.5 | 20 | 25 | 20 |

**Costs:**
- Inventory Prepositioning ($c_{INV}$): $2 / unit
- Path Management ($c_{MANA}$): $10 / link
- Delivery ($c_{DELI}$): $1 / unit
- Unmet Demand Penalty ($c_{UNS}$): $100 / unit
- Unused Inventory Penalty ($c_{UNU}$): $5 / unit
