### Decision Variables

- $ \theta_r \in \{0,1\} $: 1 if truck-drone route $ r \in \Omega $ is selected

### Objective Function

Minimize total duration (sum of travel + waiting time across all truck routes):

$$
\min \sum_{r \in \Omega} (t_r + w_r) \theta_r
$$

### Constraints

1. Each customer visited exactly once (truck or drone, across all routes):

$$
\sum_{r \in \Omega} (\alpha_{ir} + \beta_{ir}) \theta_r = 1, \quad \forall i \in N
$$

2. Fleet size limit:

$$
\sum_{r \in \Omega} \theta_r \leq |K| = 2
$$

3. Co-assignment dependency (C2 and C3 must be in the same truck route):

Only routes that contain both C2 and C3 together, or neither, are considered feasible. This is enforced during route generation by excluding any route that serves exactly one of {C2, C3} without the other.

Equivalently, in the set-partitioning formulation, define $ \Omega' \subseteq \Omega $ as the set of routes where C2 and C3 are either both present or both absent:

$$
\theta_r = 0, \quad \forall r \in \Omega \setminus \Omega'
$$

4. Binary:

$$
\theta_r \in \{0,1\}, \quad r \in \Omega
$$

### Route Feasibility Conditions

A truck-drone route $ r = (v_0, v_1(H_1), \ldots, v_i(H_i)) $ is feasible if:

- Capacity: $ \sum_{i \in \text{truck nodes}} b_i + \sum_{j \in \text{drone nodes}} b_j \leq Q = 20 $
- Drone battery: $ 2 \, t_{v_i, j} \leq B = 40 $, for each drone node $ j \in H_i $ (round-trip flight time must not exceed battery)
- Drone payload: $ g_j \leq D = 10 $, for each drone node $ j $
- Time windows: service starts within $ [e_i, l_i] $ for all nodes (both truck-visited and drone-visited)
- Max drones: $ |H_i| \leq m = 2 $ at each truck node $ v_i $
- Co-assignment: if C2 $ \in $ route, then C3 $ \in $ route (and vice versa)

### Ready Time Recursion

$$
\rho_i^t = \delta_{i-1}^t + \tau_{(i-1)i}(\delta_{i-1}^t)
$$

$$
\rho_{ij}^d = \rho_i^t + t_{ij}, \quad \forall h_{ij} \in H_i
$$

$$
\delta_{ij}^d = \rho_{ij}^d + s_j^d, \quad \forall h_{ij} \in H_i
$$

$$
\delta_i^t = \max\left(\rho_i^t + s_i^t,\ \max_{h_{ij} \in H_i}(\delta_{ij}^d + t_{ji})\right)
$$

### Route Cost

$$
t_r + w_r = \delta_{\text{end}}^t - 0 \quad \text{(total duration from depot departure to return)}
$$

### Instance-Specific Notes

- K = 2 trucks, m = 2 drones per truck, Q = 20 per truck
- Total demand = 35 > Q = 20, so both trucks must be used
- Co-assignment C2+C3 (demand = 14) constrains partitioning: remaining capacity on that truck is only 6
- Time windows are differentiated: C5 has a tight deadline [0, 60], C4 has a late start [60, 180], C6 has a late start [80, 200]
- C2 (weight = 12 > D = 10) cannot be drone-delivered
