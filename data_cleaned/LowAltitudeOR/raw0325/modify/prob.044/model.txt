Identifying the decision variables, objective function, and constraints for the Flying Sidekick Traveling Salesman Problem (FSTSP) based on the paper "The flying sidekick traveling salesman problem with stochastic travel time: A reinforcement learning approach" by Liu, Li, and Khojandi (2022).

The paper extends the FSTSP with stochastic travel time (FSTSP-STT) and solves it using DRL. For the MIP formulation, we use the deterministic FSTSP model from Murray and Chu (2015), which is referenced in the paper.

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the FSTSP problem.

Let's denote:

- Nodes: N = {0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5} (0 = start depot, 5 = end depot, 1-4 = customers)
- C = {1, 2, 3, 4}: set of all customers
- C' = {1, 2, 3, 4}: set of drone-eligible customers (all customers)
- N_0 = {0, 1, 2, 3, 4}: nodes from which a vehicle may depart
- N_+ = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5}: nodes to which a vehicle may visit

Decision variables:
- x_ij ∈ {0,1}: equals 1 if truck travels from node i to node j
- y_ijk ∈ {0,1}: equals 1 if drone is launched from node i, visits customer j, and returns to truck at node k
- t_j ≥ 0: time at which truck arrives at node j
- t'_j ≥ 0: time at which drone arrives at node j
- p_ij ∈ {0,1}: equals 1 if customer i is visited before customer j in the truck's path
- u_i ∈ [1, c+2]: position of node i in the truck's path (subtour elimination)

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total delivery time (makespan):

$$\min t_{c+1}$$

where t_{c+1} is the truck's return time to the ending depot. Due to constraints (14) and (15) in the paper, this is equivalent to minimizing max{t_{c+1}, t'_{c+1}}.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Customer service constraint**: Each customer must be served exactly once.
$$\sum_{i \in N_0, i \neq j} x_{ij} + \sum_{i \in N_0, i \neq j} \sum_{k \in N_+} y_{ijk} = 1, \quad \forall j \in C$$

2. **Truck departure from depot**: Truck departs from depot exactly once.
$$\sum_{j \in N_+} x_{0j} = 1$$

3. **Truck return to depot**: Truck returns to depot exactly once.
$$\sum_{i \in N_0} x_{i,c+1} = 1$$

4. **Subtour elimination**: MTZ-style constraints.
$$u_i - u_j + 1 \leq (c+2)(1 - x_{ij}), \quad \forall i \in C, j \in N_+ \setminus \{i\}$$

5. **Flow conservation**: Truck visiting node j must also depart from j.
$$\sum_{i \in N_0, i \neq j} x_{ij} = \sum_{k \in N_+, k \neq j} x_{jk}, \quad \forall j \in C$$

6. **Drone launch constraint**: Drone may launch from any node at most once.
$$\sum_{j \in C, j \neq i} \sum_{k \in N_+} y_{ijk} \leq 1, \quad \forall i \in N_0$$

7. **Drone retrieval constraint**: Drone may rendezvous at any node at most once.
$$\sum_{i \in N_0, i \neq k} \sum_{j \in C} y_{ijk} \leq 1, \quad \forall k \in N_+$$

8. **Truck must visit launch and retrieval nodes**: If drone launches from customer i and retrieves at node k, truck must visit both.
$$2 y_{ijk} \leq \sum_{h \in N_0, h \neq i} x_{hi} + \sum_{l \in C, l \neq k} x_{lk}, \quad \forall i \in C, j \in C \setminus \{i\}, k \in N_+$$

9. **Drone launch from depot**: If drone launches from depot and retrieves at k, truck must visit k.
$$y_{0jk} \leq \sum_{h \in N_0, h \neq k} x_{hk}, \quad \forall j \in C, k \in N_+$$

10. **Ordering constraint**: If drone launches from i and retrieves at k, truck must visit i before k.
$$u_k - u_i \geq 1 - (c+2)(1 - \sum_{j \in C} y_{ijk}), \quad \forall i \in C, k \in N_+ \setminus \{i\}$$

11. **Time synchronization at launch node**: Truck and drone depart together from launch node.
$$t'_i \geq t_i - M(1 - \sum_{j \in C, j \neq i} \sum_{k \in N_+} y_{ijk}), \quad \forall i \in C$$
$$t'_i \leq t_i + M(1 - \sum_{j \in C, j \neq i} \sum_{k \in N_+} y_{ijk}), \quad \forall i \in C$$

12. **Time synchronization at retrieval node**: Truck and drone arrive together at retrieval node.
$$t'_k \geq t_k - M(1 - \sum_{i \in N_0, i \neq k} \sum_{j \in C} y_{ijk}), \quad \forall k \in N_+$$
$$t'_k \leq t_k + M(1 - \sum_{i \in N_0, i \neq k} \sum_{j \in C} y_{ijk}), \quad \forall k \in N_+$$

13. **Truck travel time**: Truck arrival time at node k from node h.
$$t_k \geq t'_h + s_{hk} + s_L \sum_{l \in C, l \neq k} \sum_{m \in N_+} y_{klm} + s_R \sum_{i \in N_0, i \neq k} \sum_{j \in C} y_{ijk} - M(1 - x_{hk}), \quad \forall h \in N_0, k \in N_+$$

14. **Drone travel time from launch to customer**: Drone arrival time at customer j from launch node i.
$$t'_j \geq t'_i + \sigma_{ij} - M(1 - \sum_{k \in N_+} y_{ijk}), \quad \forall j \in C', i \in N_0 \setminus \{j\}$$

15. **Drone travel time from customer to retrieval**: Drone arrival time at retrieval node k from customer j.
$$t'_k \geq t'_j + \sigma_{jk} + s_R - M(1 - \sum_{i \in N_0} y_{ijk}), \quad \forall j \in C', k \in N_+ \setminus \{j\}$$

16. **Drone endurance constraint**: Total drone flight time per sortie ≤ e.
$$t'_k - (t'_j - \sigma_{ij}) \leq e + M(1 - y_{ijk}), \quad \forall k \in N_+, j \in C \setminus \{k\}, i \in N_0$$

17. **Consecutive sortie ordering**: If drone launches from i and retrieves at k, and later launches from l, then t'_l ≥ t'_k.
$$t'_l \geq t'_k - M(3 - \sum_{j \in C} y_{ijk} - \sum_{m \in C} y_{lmn} - p_{il}), \quad \forall i, k, l$$

18. **Ordering variable constraints**:
$$u_i - u_j \geq 1 - (c+2) p_{ij}, \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j$$
$$u_i - u_j \leq 1 + (c+2)(1 - p_{ij}), \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j$$
$$p_{ij} + p_{ji} = 1, \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j$$

19. **Initial conditions**:
$$t_0 = t'_0 = 0$$

20. **Variable bounds**:
$$x_{ij} \in \{0,1\}, \quad y_{ijk} \in \{0,1\}, \quad 1 \leq u_i \leq c+2, \quad t_i, t'_i \geq 0, \quad p_{ij} \in \{0,1\}$$

21. **Mutual exclusion for drone service (Customer 3 and Customer 4)**: Due to a no-fly zone regulation in the eastern high-altitude corridor, the drone cannot serve both Customer 3 and Customer 4.
$$\sum_{\langle i,3,k \rangle \in P} y_{i,3,k} + \sum_{\langle i,4,k \rangle \in P} y_{i,4,k} \leq 1$$
