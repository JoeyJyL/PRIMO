### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ y_j $: a binary variable indicating if Transshipment Hub $ j $ is opened.
- $ x_{jk} $: a binary variable indicating if Customer $ k $ is assigned to Hub $ j $.
- $ L_j $: a continuous variable representing the total load (packages) processed by Hub $ j $.

Thus, we have:

- $ y_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in H $.
- $ x_{jk} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in H $ and $ k \in C $.
- $ L_j \ge 0 $ for all $ j \in H $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which consists of the fixed costs for opening hubs, the transportation costs (supply to hub and hub to customer), and a quadratic penalty for congestion at the hubs.

The objective function is:

$$
\min Z = \sum_{j \in H} F_j y_j + \sum_{k \in C} \sum_{j \in H} D_k (d_{sj} + d_{jk}) c_{trans} x_{jk} + \sum_{j \in H} \alpha (L_j)^2
$$

Where:

- $ F_j $ is the fixed cost to open Hub $ j $.
- $ D_k $ is the demand of Customer $ k $.
- $ d_{sj} $ is the distance from the Supply depot to Hub $ j $.
- $ d_{jk} $ is the distance from Hub $ j $ to Customer $ k $.
- $ c_{trans} $ is the transport cost per unit distance per package.
- $ \alpha = 2.0 $ is the congestion penalty coefficient.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Service Guarantee Constraint:**

Each customer must be assigned to exactly one open Hub.

For each Customer $ k \in C $:

$$
\sum_{j \in H} x_{jk} = 1
$$

A customer can only be assigned to Hub $ j $ if Hub $ j $ is actually opened.

For each Hub $ j \in H $ and Customer $ k \in C $:

$$
x_{jk} \le y_j
$$

2. **Load Definition Constraint:**

The load at Hub $ j $ is defined as the sum of demand from all customers assigned to it.

For each Hub $ j \in H $:

$$
L_j = \sum_{k \in C} D_k x_{jk}
$$

3. **Minimum Hub Requirement:**

For operational redundancy and airspace coverage, at least 3 hubs must be opened.

$$
\sum_{j \in H} y_j \ge 3
$$

4. **Binary and Non-negativity Constraints:**

The decision variables must adhere to their respective domains.

For all $ j \in H $ and $ k \in C $:

$$
y_j \in \{0, 1\}, \quad x_{jk} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad L_j \ge 0
$$
