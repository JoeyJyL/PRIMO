### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ y_m $: a binary variable indicating if Micro-Depot (MD) $ m $ is opened.
- $ z_{im} $: a binary variable indicating if customer $ i $ is served by a drone from MD $ m $.
- $ v_i $: a binary variable indicating if customer $ i $ is served directly by the truck.
- $ x_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating if the truck travels from node $ i $ to node $ j $.
- $ u_i $: a continuous variable representing the cumulative position or load in the truck tour for Subtour Elimination (MTZ).

Thus, we have:

- $ y_m \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ m \in M $.
- $ z_{im} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in C, m \in M $.
- $ v_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in C $.
- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i, j \in N $.
- $ u_i \ge 0 $ for all $ i \in N $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which consists of the fixed costs for opening Micro-Depots, the drone delivery costs, and the truck transportation costs.

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{m \in M} F_m y_m + \sum_{i \in C} \sum_{m \in M} c_{im}^{drone} z_{im} + \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in N} c_{ij}^{truck} x_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ F_m $ is the fixed cost to open MD $ m $.
- $ c_{im}^{drone} $ is the drone round-trip cost from MD $ m $ to customer $ i $.
- $ c_{ij}^{truck} $ is the truck travel cost from node $ i $ to node $ j $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Service Assignment Constraint:**

Each customer must be served exactly once, either by a drone from an open MD or directly by the truck.

For each customer $ i \in C $:

$$
\sum_{m \in M} z_{im} + v_i = 1
$$

2. **MD Availability and Range Constraint:**

A customer can only be assigned to an MD if that MD is open and within the drone's range $ R_{max} $.

For each customer $ i \in C $ and MD $ m \in M $:

$$
z_{im} \le y_m
$$

Additionally, if the distance is beyond range (preprocessing step):

$$
z_{im} = 0 \quad \text{if } dist(i, m) > R_{max}
$$

3. **Truck Capacity Constraint (Cold-Chain Compliance):**

Due to cold-chain temperature compliance, the truck can serve at most 2 customers per trip.

$$
\sum_{i \in C} v_i \le 2
$$

4. **Truck Flow Conservation:**

The truck flow must be conserved at every node. If a node is active (CDC, open MD, or truck-served customer), the truck must enter and leave it exactly once.

For the CDC (node 0):

$$
\sum_{j \in N} x_{0j} = 1, \quad \sum_{j \in N} x_{j0} = 1
$$

For each MD $ m \in M $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N} x_{mj} = \sum_{j \in N} x_{jm} = y_m
$$

For each customer $ i \in C $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N} x_{ij} = \sum_{j \in N} x_{ji} = v_i
$$

5. **Subtour Elimination (MTZ):**

To prevent subtours in the truck route, we apply Miller-Tucker-Zemlin constraints.

For all $ i, j \in N \setminus \{CDC\} $:

$$
u_i - u_j + |N| \cdot x_{ij} \le |N| - 1
$$

6. **Binary and Non-negativity Constraints:**

$$
y_m, z_{im}, v_i, x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad u_i \ge 0
$$
