### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ y $: a binary variable indicating if the charging station is opened.
- $ k $: a non-negative integer variable representing the number of charging piles (capacity).
- $ z_c $: a binary variable indicating if the capacity level is chosen as $ c $.
- $ W_s $: a continuous variable representing the total waiting cost in scenario $ s $.

Thus, we have:

- $ y \in \{0, 1\} $.
- $ k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0} $.
- $ z_c \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ c \in C $.
- $ W_s \ge 0 $ for all $ s \in S $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which consists of the fixed investment costs (opening cost and pile cost) and the expected operational costs (waiting penalty) across all scenarios.

The objective function is:

$$
\min Z = (F_{fixed} \cdot y + C_{pile} \cdot k) + \sum_{s \in S} Prob_s \cdot W_s
$$

Where:

- $ F_{fixed} = 200 $ is the fixed cost to open the station.
- $ C_{pile} = 50 $ is the variable cost per charging pile.
- $ C_{wait} = 100 $ is the queuing penalty per hour of waiting time (increased to reflect higher urgency).
- $ Prob_s $ is the probability of scenario $ s $.
- $ S $ is the set of scenarios (Normal, Peak).

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1.  **Capacity Definition and Logic Constraints:**

The capacity $ k $ is determined by the selected capacity level $ c $. Exactly one capacity level must be chosen.

$$
k = \sum_{c \in C} c \cdot z_c
$$

$$
\sum_{c \in C} z_c = 1
$$

The capacity $ k $ can be positive only if the station is opened.

$$
k \le MaxCap \cdot y
$$

Where:

- $ C $ is the set of possible capacity levels.
- $ MaxCap $ is the maximum possible capacity.

2.  **Wait Cost Calculation:**

The waiting cost $ W_s $ for each scenario is calculated based on the number of drones $ N_{drones,s} $ and the chosen capacity level $ c $. This utilizes pre-calculated wait times.

For each scenario $ s \in S $:

$$
W_s = C_{wait} \cdot \sum_{c \in C} WaitTime(N_{drones,s}, c) \cdot z_c
$$

Where:

- $ C_{wait} = 100 $ is the penalty cost per hour of waiting.
- $ WaitTime(n, c) $ is a parameter representing the total wait time for $ n $ drones given $ c $ piles.

3.  **Binary, Integer, and Non-negativity Constraints:**

The decision variables must adhere to their respective domains.

$$
y \in \{0, 1\}, \quad z_c \in \{0, 1\}, \quad k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, \quad W_s \ge 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y \in \{0, 1\} $.
- $ k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0} $.
- $ z_c \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ c \in C $.
- $ W_s \ge 0 $ for all $ s \in S $.

**Objective Function:**

Minimize:

$$
\min Z = (F_{fixed} \cdot y + C_{pile} \cdot k) + \sum_{s \in S} Prob_s \cdot W_s
$$

**Constraints:**

1.  **Capacity Definition and Logic:**

$$
k = \sum_{c \in C} c \cdot z_c
$$
$$
\sum_{c \in C} z_c = 1
$$
$$
k \le MaxCap \cdot y
$$

2.  **Wait Cost Calculation:**

$$
W_s = C_{wait} \cdot \sum_{c \in C} WaitTime(N_{drones,s}, c) \cdot z_c, \quad \forall s \in S
$$

3.  **Variable Domains:**

$$
y \in \{0, 1\}, \quad z_c \in \{0, 1\}, \quad k \in \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}, \quad W_s \ge 0
$$
