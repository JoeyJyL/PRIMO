### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the UAM network planning problem.

- $ x_i \in \{0, 1\} $: whether vertiport $ i $ is built ($ i \in \{1, 2, 3\} $).
- $ y_r \in \{0, 1\} $: whether Regional Shuttle route $ r $ is activated ($ r \in \{A, B\} $).
- $ w_z \in \{0, 1\} $: whether On-Demand Metro zone $ z $ is served ($ z \in \{1, 2, 3\} $).

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

Maximize total expected daily demand served:

$$
\max \sum_{r \in R} D_r \cdot y_r + \sum_{z \in Z} D_z \cdot w_z
$$

Where $ D_r $ and $ D_z $ denote the expected daily demand for RS route $ r $ and ODM zone $ z $, respectively.

Substituting values:

$$
\max\ 100 y_A + 110 y_B + 20 w_1 + 30 w_2 + 30 w_3
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Budget Constraint:**

$$
10 x_1 + 15 x_2 + 12 x_3 \le 25
$$

2. **RS Route Activation (AND logic):**

Route A requires both V1 and V2:

$$
y_A \le x_1, \quad y_A \le x_2
$$

Route B requires both V1 and V3:

$$
y_B \le x_1, \quad y_B \le x_3
$$

3. **ODM Zone Service:**

Each zone requires its associated vertiport:

$$
w_1 \le x_1, \quad w_2 \le x_2, \quad w_3 \le x_3
$$

4. **Airspace Mutual Exclusion Constraint:**

Due to overlapping southern approach corridor conflicts, V1 and V3 cannot both be built:

$$
x_1 + x_3 \le 1
$$

5. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
x_i, y_r, w_z \in \{0, 1\}
$$
