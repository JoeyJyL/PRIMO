# UAM Vertiport Network Design Problem - Simplified Scenario

## Background
Urban Air Mobility (UAM) is emerging as a promising solution to alleviate urban congestion. A key challenge in implementing UAM systems is determining optimal vertiport locations that balance infrastructure costs, passenger accessibility, and network connectivity. Unlike traditional facility location problems, UAM vertiport planning must ensure that selected vertiports form a connected network within the maximum flight range constraint.

## Problem Description
A city transportation authority wants to establish a UAM vertiport network by selecting optimal locations from a set of candidate sites. The authority needs to determine:
1. Which candidate locations should be selected for vertiport construction
2. How to assign passenger demand points to selected vertiports
3. How to ensure all selected vertiports form a connected network

The goal is to minimize total system cost (vertiport construction cost + passenger transportation cost) while ensuring network connectivity.

**Additional Constraint (Policy Mandate):** Due to urban development policy, Location 7 (coordinates (10, 16)) must be included as a vertiport site, as the city has already allocated land and approved construction permits for this location. This is a mandatory selection that cannot be overridden by the optimizer.

## Decision-Making Stages
**Stage 1:** Select P vertiport locations from candidate sites (with Location 7 mandatory)
**Stage 2:** Assign each demand point to exactly one selected vertiport
**Stage 3:** Ensure selected vertiports form a connected network within maximum flight range

## Parameters

### Network Configuration
- **Number of Candidate Locations:** 10 sites
- **Number of Vertiports to Build (P):** 3 vertiports
- **Maximum Flight Range (D_max):** 15 km (connectivity constraint)

### Cost Parameters
- **Unit Transportation Cost (α):** $3.5 per person per km
- **Fixed Vertiport Construction Cost (f_j):** Varies by location (see mock data)

### Operational Constraints
- Each demand point must be assigned to exactly one vertiport
- Selected vertiports must form a connected network
- Any two connected vertiports must be within D_max distance
- **Location 7 must be selected** (mandatory policy constraint)

## Objective
Minimize total cost = (Sum of construction costs for selected vertiports) + (Sum of passenger transportation costs)

Where passenger transportation cost = α × demand × distance from demand point to assigned vertiport

Subject to:
- Exactly P vertiports must be selected
- Each demand point is assigned to exactly one selected vertiport
- Selected vertiports form a connected network
- Distance between any two connected vertiports ≤ D_max
- The number of edges in the connected network = P - 1 (spanning tree structure)
- **Location 7 must be selected** (mandatory policy constraint)
