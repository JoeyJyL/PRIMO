1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP
2. Problem Size: Small

### Sets:

- $ I $: Set of demand points.
- $ J' $: Set of candidate vertiport locations.
- $ J = J' \cup \{0\} $: Set including candidate locations and virtual sink node.
- $ N_j $: Set of candidate locations within $ D_{max} $ from location $ j $.

### Parameters:

- $ P $: Number of vertiports to be constructed.
- $ \alpha $: Unit transportation cost per person per kilometer.
- $ h_i $: Demand at demand point $ i $ (number of passengers).
- $ d_{ij} $: Euclidean distance between demand point $ i $ and candidate location $ j $.
- $ f_j $: Fixed construction cost at candidate location $ j $.
- $ D_{max} $: Maximum allowable flight distance between vertiports.

### Decision Variables:

- $ X_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in J' $.
- $ Y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in I $, $ j \in J' $.
- $ l_{jk} \geq 0 $ for all $ j, k \in J $.

### Objective Function:

Minimize:

$$
\alpha \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J'} h_i d_{ij} Y_{ij} + \sum_{j \in J'} f_j X_j
$$

### Constraints:

1. **Facility Selection Constraint:**

$$
\sum_{j \in J'} X_j = P
$$

2. **Demand Assignment Constraint:**

For each $ i \in I $:

$$
\sum_{j \in J'} Y_{ij} = 1
$$

3. **Assignment Feasibility Constraint:**

For each $ i \in I $, $ j \in J' $:

$$
Y_{ij} \leq X_j
$$

4. **Flow Balance Constraint (Connectivity):**

For each $ j \in J' $:

$$
X_j + \sum_{n \in N_j} l_{nj} = \sum_{m \in N_j} l_{jm} + l_{j0}
$$

5. **Flow Capacity Constraint:**

For all $ j \in J' $, $ k \in N_j $:

$$
l_{jk} \leq (P - 1) X_j
$$

6. **Sink Node Inflow Constraint:**

For all $ j \in J' $:

$$
l_{0j} = 0
$$

7. **Total Sink Flow Constraint:**

$$
\sum_{j \in J'} l_{j0} = P
$$

8. **Mandatory Location Constraint (Policy):**

Location 7 must be selected:

$$
X_7 = 1
$$

9. **Variable Domains:**

$ X_j \in \{0, 1\} $, $ Y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $, $ l_{jk} \geq 0 $
