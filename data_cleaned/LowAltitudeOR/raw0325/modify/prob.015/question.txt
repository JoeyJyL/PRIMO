SkyLondon is planning the deployment of Vertiports for air taxi services.
**Core Challenge:** Vertiports have limited processing capacity (landing pads/gates). As passenger demand at a specific vertiport increases, **Queuing Delays** rise non-linearly.
**Goal:** Select Vertiport locations from candidates to minimize the Total System Cost:
   - **Fixed Cost:** Construction cost of opening a vertiport.
   - **Access Cost:** Passengers travel distance to their assigned vertiport.
   - **Congestion Cost:** A penalty representing queuing delays.
     $$ \text{Congestion Cost}_j = 0.05 \times (\text{Total Load}_j)^2 $$
     *(Note: This quadratic term captures the exponential nature of queuing delays).*

**Parameters:**
- **Max Vertiports:** No strict limit, driven by cost trade-off.
- **Access Cost:** $1.0 per km per passenger.
- **Vertiport Capacity:** Due to limited landing pad infrastructure, each vertiport can handle at most **200** passengers. $$ L_j \le 200, \quad \forall j \in J $$

**Locations & Demand:**
*Candidate Vertiports ($J$):*
- **V1 (City Center):** (5, 5) | Fixed Cost: **2000** (Expensive land).
- **V2 (West End):** (2, 5)   | Fixed Cost: **1000**.
- **V3 (East End):** (8, 5)   | Fixed Cost: **1000**.

*Passenger Zones ($I$):*
- **Z1:** (2, 8) | Demand: 100 | Near V2.
- **Z2:** (5, 8) | Demand: 120 | Near V1.
- **Z3:** (8, 8) | Demand: 100 | Near V3.
- **Z4:** (5, 2) | Demand: 80  | Near V1.

**Analysis:**
- **Scenario A (Centralize):** Open only V1.
  - Load = 400 > 200 capacity. **Infeasible.**
- **Scenario B (Distribute V2+V3):** Open V2, V3.
  - Z1→V2(100), Z3→V3(100). Z2(120)+Z4(80) must split: but both assigned to one of V2/V3 could exceed 200.
  - If Z2→V3, Z4→V2: V2=180, V3=220 > 200. **Infeasible.**
  - Need to open V1 for extra capacity.
- **Scenario C (All three):** Open V1, V2, V3.
  - Z1→V2(100), Z3→V3(100), Z2→V1(120), Z4→V1(80): V1=200, V2=100, V3=100. All ≤ 200. **Feasible.**
  - Fixed=4000, Access=1200, Congestion=0.05*(40000+10000+10000)=3000. Total=8200.
- The model must find the balance under the capacity constraint.
