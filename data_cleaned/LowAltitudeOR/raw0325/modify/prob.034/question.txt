A logistics company needs to perform **contactless parcel delivery** in an epidemic area using a collaborative truck-drone system. The goal is to **minimize the total completion time** (makespan) for delivering parcels to all customers.

**Goal:** Route one truck and one drone cooperatively to deliver parcels to 8 customers, minimizing the time until all deliveries are complete and both vehicles return to the depot.

**System Overview:**
- The truck departs from the depot, visits a subset of customers, and returns.
- The drone launches from the depot or a truck stop, serves up to 3 nearby customers in a single sortie, and returns to a later truck stop or the depot.
- Each customer is served exactly once, either by the truck or the drone.
- The truck must wait at the drone's return point until the drone arrives (synchronization).
- The drone has payload capacity and energy consumption constraints.

**Vehicle Parameters:**
- Truck speed: 50 km/h
- Drone speed: 75 km/h
- Drone self-weight: 1.5 kg
- Drone max payload: 3 kg
- Drone max battery energy: 500 Wh
- Drone energy consumption (linearized): Power(W) ≈ 80 + 30 × payload(kg)

**Customer Data:**
| Customer | X (km) | Y (km) | Parcel (kg) |
|----------|--------|--------|-------------|
| 1        | 4      | 10     | 0.8         |
| 2        | 8      | 2      | 1.0         |
| 3        | 12     | 0      | 0.5         |
| 4        | 10     | 9      | 0.7         |
| 5        | 16     | 3      | 1.2         |
| 6        | 20     | 1      | 1.5         |
| 7        | 6      | -2     | 0.6         |
| 8        | 14     | 7      | 0.9         |

**Depot:** Located at origin (0, 0). Truck and drone depart from and return to the depot.

**Constraints:**
- Each customer served exactly once (by truck or drone).
- Truck follows a connected route from depot through truck-served customers back to depot.
- Drone launches from a truck-visited node, serves ≤3 customers, returns to a truck-visited node.
- Drone total payload ≤ 3 kg; total energy consumption ≤ 500 Wh.
- Truck waits for drone at the rendezvous point before departing.
- Makespan = max(truck return time, drone return time).
- **Mandatory Truck Delivery:** Customer 6 has a fragile, high-value parcel requiring a personal hand-off and signature confirmation. Therefore, Customer 6 must be served by the truck (drone delivery is not permitted for this customer).
