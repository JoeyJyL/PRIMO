 
An Urban Air Mobility (UAM) fleet operator in a metropolitan region wants to minimize the **Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)** for one operating day of eVTOL flight operations.
 
**Goal:** Select the optimal **vehicle concept**, **battery capacity**, and **charging infrastructure** to minimize the daily TCO, including fleet acquisition, battery, maintenance, infrastructure, and energy costs. The operator must ensure all simulated flight sequences (including deadheading flights) can be completed with sufficient battery charge at all times.
 
**Strategic Decision Variables:**
1. **Vehicle Concept (k):** Choose one homogeneous fleet type.
   - k=0: TiltWing (A3 Vahana-like) — lightweight, efficient cruise, 1 PAX, max 200 kph.
   - k=1: LiftCruise (Wisk Cora-like) — heavier, 2 PAX capacity, max 180 kph.
2. **Battery Weight (Qw):** Integer kg, determines energy capacity (kWh = Qw × 0.4 kWh/kg).
3. **Charging Infrastructure (r):** Choose one charger type for all stations.
   - r=0: 50 kW DC ($28,401 hardware + $45,506 installation)
   - r=1: 150 kW DC ($75,000 hardware + $47,781 installation)
   - r=2: 350 kW DC ($140,000 hardware + $65,984 installation)
 
**Fleet & Operations:**
- Fleet size: 2 vehicles, 4 stations with 2 parking slots each.
- Cruise speed: 150 kph. Hover time: 60 seconds each for take-off and landing.
- Turnaround time: 3 minutes. Passenger weight: 90 kg.
- Partial recharging is allowed (vehicles can depart without full charge).
 
**Energy Consumption (linearized, per kg of vehicle mass):**
- TiltWing: Hover power = 0.055 kW/kg; Cruise energy = 0.00028 kWh/km/kg.
- LiftCruise: Hover power = 0.042 kW/kg; Cruise energy = 0.00035 kWh/km/kg.
 
**Battery Parameters:**
- Energy density: 400 Wh/kg | Power density: 3000 W/kg | Usable fraction: 80%
- Battery cost: $285/kWh | Energy price: $0.25/kWh | Cycle life: 2000 cycles
 
**Cost Structure:**
- Vehicle acquisition: $770/kg of empty weight, 13-year life, 30% residual value, 330 ops days/year.
- Annual maintenance: $51,300/vehicle/year.
- Charger lifetime: 13 years.
 
**Flight Sequences (2 vehicles, 3 trips each):**
 
*Vehicle 0:*
| Trip | Distance | PAX | Deadhead After? | DH Dist | Time Gap |
|------|----------|-----|-----------------|---------|----------|
| 0    | 18 km    | 1   | No              | -       | 0.5 h    |
| 1    | 25 km    | 1   | Yes             | 12 km   | 0.8 h    |
| 2    | 15 km    | 1   | -               | -       | -        |
 
*Vehicle 1:*
| Trip | Distance | PAX | Deadhead After? | DH Dist | Time Gap |
|------|----------|-----|-----------------|---------|----------|
| 0    | 22 km    | 1   | Yes             | 8 km    | 0.6 h    |
| 1    | 30 km    | 1   | No              | -       | 0.4 h    |
| 2    | 20 km    | 1   | -               | -       | -        |
 
**Constraints:**
- Battery SOC must be sufficient for every trip and deadheading flight.
- Recharging is limited by available time and charger power.
- Vehicle max take-off weight and min battery power must be satisfied.
- Exactly one vehicle concept and one charger type must be selected.
- **Fast-Charging Mandate:** Due to regulatory requirements for rapid turnaround and operational efficiency in high-density urban airspace, only fast-charging infrastructure rated at 150 kW or above is permitted at all vertiport stations. The 50 kW DC charger option is no longer available.
