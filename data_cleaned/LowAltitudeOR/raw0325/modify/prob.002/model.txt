### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ y_j $: a binary variable indicating whether hub $ j $ is built (1) or not (0), for all $ j \in H $.
- $ x_{j,i,s} $: a binary variable indicating whether demand point $ i $ is served by hub $ j $ in scenario $ s $ (1) or not (0), for all $ j \in H $, $ i \in C $, $ s \in S $.
- $ Z $: a continuous variable representing the worst-case operational cost across all scenarios.

Thus, we have:

- $ y_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in H $.
- $ x_{j,i,s} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in H $, $ i \in C $, $ s \in S $.
- $ Z \ge 0 $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total construction cost plus the worst-case operational cost.

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{j \in H} f_j \cdot y_j + Z
$$

Where:

- $ H $ is the set of candidate hubs.
- $ f_j $ is the construction cost of hub $ j $.
- $ Z $ is the worst-case operational cost.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Robust Constraint (Min-Max Linearization):**

The worst-case cost $ Z $ must be at least as large as the operational cost in any scenario.

For each scenario $ s \in S $:

$$
Z \ge \sum_{j \in H} \sum_{i \in C} d_{ji} \cdot q_{is} \cdot c \cdot x_{j,i,s}
$$

Where:
- $ d_{ji} $ is the distance from hub $ j $ to demand point $ i $.
- $ q_{is} $ is the demand at point $ i $ in scenario $ s $.
- $ c $ is the unit transportation cost.

2. **Mandatory Coverage Constraint:**

In every scenario, each demand point must be assigned to exactly one active hub within range.

For each $ i \in C $ and each $ s \in S $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N_i} x_{j,i,s} = 1
$$

Where:
- $ N_i $ is the set of hubs within service range of demand point $ i $.

3. **Linking Constraint:**

A demand point can only be assigned to a hub that has been built.

For each $ j \in H $, $ i \in C $, $ s \in S $ where $ d_{ji} \le R $:

$$
x_{j,i,s} \le y_j
$$

4. **Infrastructure Dependency Constraint:**

If hub H2 (Plains) is built, hub H3 (City Center) must also be built.

$$
y_{H2} \le y_{H3}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in H $.
- $ x_{j,i,s} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all valid $ (j, i, s) $ pairs.
- $ Z \ge 0 $.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min \sum_{j \in H} f_j \cdot y_j + Z
$$

**Constraints:**

$$
Z \ge \sum_{j \in H} \sum_{i \in C} d_{ji} \cdot q_{is} \cdot c \cdot x_{j,i,s} \quad \forall s \in S
$$

$$
\sum_{j \in N_i} x_{j,i,s} = 1 \quad \forall i \in C, \forall s \in S
$$

$$
x_{j,i,s} \le y_j \quad \forall j \in H, \forall i \in C, \forall s \in S \text{ where } d_{ji} \le R
$$

$$
y_{H2} \le y_{H3}
$$
