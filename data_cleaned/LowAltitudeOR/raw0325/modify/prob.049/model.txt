Identifying the decision variables, objective function, and constraints for the Multi-visit Drone Routing Problem (k-MVDRP) based on the truck-and-drone delivery framework.

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ x_o $: a binary variable indicating whether operation $ o $ is selected as part of the solution (1) or not (0).
- $ t_o $: a continuous variable representing the time required to complete operation $ o $.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_o \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ o \in O $.
- $ t_o \ge 0 $ for all $ o \in O $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total route completion time.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Minimize } \sum_{o \in O} t_o \cdot x_o
$$

Where:
- $ O $ is the set of all feasible operations.
- $ t_o $ is the time required for operation $ o $.
- $ x_o $ indicates if operation $ o $ is selected.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Operation Time - Truck Constraint:**

The time for an operation must be at least as long as the truck travel time:

For all $ o \in O $:

$$
t_o \ge truckTime(o)
$$

Where:
- $ truckTime(o) $ is the time for the truck to travel from the launch point to the retrieval point of operation $ o $.

2. **Operation Time - Drone Constraint:**

The time for an operation must be at least as long as the longest drone flight time:

For all $ o \in O $, $ i \in \{1, 2, ..., k\} $:

$$
t_o \ge droneTime_i(o)
$$

Where:
- $ droneTime_i(o) $ is the flight time of drone $ i $ in operation $ o $.
- $ k $ is the number of drones.

3. **Customer Coverage:**

Every customer must be serviced by exactly one operation:

For all $ j \in \{1, 2, ..., |C|\} $:

$$
\sum_{o \in O} cover(o, j) \cdot x_o \ge 1
$$

Where:
- $ C $ is the set of customers.
- $ cover(o, j) = 1 $ if operation $ o $ services customer $ c_j $, otherwise $ cover(o, j) = 0 $.

4. **Flow Conservation:**

The truck tour must form a closed path. The number of times the truck arrives at a location must equal the number of times it departs:

For all $ l \in V $:

$$
\sum_{o \in O} rp(o, l) \cdot x(o) = \sum_{o \in O} lp(o, l) \cdot x(o)
$$

Where:
- $ V $ is the set of launch/landing locations.
- $ lp(o, l) = 1 $ if operation $ o $ begins at location $ l $, otherwise $ lp(o, l) = 0 $.
- $ rp(o, l) = 1 $ if operation $ o $ terminates at location $ l $, otherwise $ rp(o, l) = 0 $.

5. **Subtour Elimination:**

The truck tour must not contain disconnected subtours. Standard subtour elimination constraints apply to ensure connectivity.

6. **Energy Feasibility (Reduced Battery):**

All drone flights within selected operations must be energy feasible. This is enforced implicitly by only including feasible operations in set $ O $. Due to cold-weather battery degradation, the effective battery capacity is reduced from 540 J to EMAX = 400 J, which limits the set of feasible operations.

For a drone sortie from $ v_i $ visiting customers $ c_{s_1}, c_{s_2}, ..., c_{s_n} $ and returning to $ v_j $:

$$
e(v_i, c_{s_1}, w_{sum}) \cdot td(v_i, c_{s_1}) + \sum_{j=2}^{n} e(c_{s_{j-1}}, c_{s_j}, w_{sum} - \sum_{a=1}^{j-1} w_{s_a}) \cdot td(c_{s_{j-1}}, c_{s_j}) + e(c_{s_n}, v_j, 0) \cdot td(c_{s_n}, v_j) + HOV \cdot \max(0, tt(v_i, v_j) - droneTime) \le EMAX = 400
$$

Where:
- $ w_{sum} $ is the combined weight of all packages in the sortie.
- $ e(v_i, v_j, w) $ is the energy drain per unit time carrying weight $ w $.
- $ td(v_i, v_j) $ is the drone travel time.
- $ tt(v_i, v_j) $ is the truck travel time.
- $ HOV $ is the hovering energy rate.
- $ EMAX = 400 $ J is the reduced battery capacity.

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the k-MVDRP is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- $ x_o \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ o \in O $.
- $ t_o \ge 0 $ for all $ o \in O $.

**Parameters:**
- Number of drones $ k = 2 $.
- Truck speed: 10 m/s, Drone speed: 10 m/s.
- Battery capacity: $ EMAX = 400 $ J (reduced from 540 J).
- Launch penalty: 30 s.
- Energy rate: $ e(W) = 31.0 + 5.6W $ J/m.
- Hovering rate: $ HOV = 31.0 $ J/s.

**Objective Function:**

Minimize:

$$
\sum_{o \in O} t_o \cdot x_o
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Operation Time - Truck:**

$$
t_o \ge truckTime(o), \quad \forall o \in O
$$

2. **Operation Time - Drone:**

$$
t_o \ge droneTime_i(o), \quad \forall o \in O, i \in \{1, 2, ..., k\}
$$

3. **Customer Coverage:**

$$
\sum_{o \in O} cover(o, j) \cdot x_o \ge 1, \quad \forall j \in \{1, 2, ..., |C|\}
$$

4. **Flow Conservation:**

$$
\sum_{o \in O} rp(o, l) \cdot x(o) = \sum_{o \in O} lp(o, l) \cdot x(o), \quad \forall l \in V
$$

5. **Subtour Elimination:**

Standard subtour elimination constraints to ensure truck tour connectivity.

6. **Energy Feasibility (Reduced Battery):**

Implicitly enforced by restricting $ O $ to only feasible operations with $ EMAX = 400 $ J.
