### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

**Sets:**
- $I$: Set of OTR (overdose-triggered request) locations
- $J$: Set of candidate drone base locations
- $I_j \subseteq I$: OTR locations within coverage radius of base $j$
- $J_i \subseteq J$: Drone bases that can cover OTR location $i$

**Parameters:**
- $\lambda_i$: Arrival rate of OTRs at location $i$ (Poisson process)
- $d_{ij}$: Distance between base $j$ and OTR location $i$
- $v$: Drone flight speed
- $E[\xi]$: Expected non-travel service time
- $E[S_{ij}] = \frac{d_{ij}}{v} + E[\xi]$: Expected service time for OTR at $i$ served by base $j$
- $p$: Total number of drones available
- $q = 4$: Maximum number of drone bases to open
- $M$: Maximum drones per base (= 2)
- $r$: Drone coverage radius

**Airspace Corridor Constraint:**
DB1 and DB4 cannot both be opened due to conflicting low-altitude flight corridors:
$$x_{DB1} + x_{DB4} \leq 1$$

**Decision Variables:**
- $x_j \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary, 1 if drone base is opened at location $j$
- $y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary, 1 if OTR at location $i$ is assigned to base $j$
- $K_j \in \mathbb{Z}^+$: Number of drones deployed at base $j$ (bounded by $M$)
- $\gamma_j^{(m)} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary, 1 if exactly $m$ drones are placed at base $j$

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The objective is to minimize the average response time across all OTRs.

**Arrival Rate at Base j:**
$$\eta_j = \sum_{i \in I_j} \lambda_i \cdot y_{ij}$$

**Average Response Time (Objective):**
$$\min R = \frac{1}{\sum_{l \in I} \lambda_l} \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J_i} \lambda_i \cdot y_{ij} \cdot \left( E[Q_j] + \frac{d_{ij}}{v} \right)$$

For computational tractability, the queueing delay is approximated via a penalty proportional to utilization.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

**1. Assignment Constraint:**
Each OTR location must be assigned to exactly one drone base:
$$\sum_{j \in J_i} y_{ij} = 1, \quad \forall i \in I$$

**2. Base Opening Constraint:**
OTRs can only be assigned to opened bases:
$$y_{ij} \leq x_j, \quad \forall i \in I, j \in J_i$$

**3. Maximum Bases Constraint:**
$$\sum_{j \in J} x_j \leq q$$

**4. Drone Deployment Constraint:**
Drones can only be placed at opened bases:
$$x_j \leq K_j \leq M \cdot x_j, \quad \forall j \in J$$

**5. Total Drones Constraint:**
$$\sum_{j \in J} K_j = p$$

**6. Stability Constraint:**
Ensure queueing system stability at each base:
$$\sum_{i \in I_j} \lambda_i \cdot y_{ij} \cdot E[S_{ij}] < K_j, \quad \forall j \in J$$

**7. Binary Representation of K:**
$$K_j = \sum_{m=1}^{M} m \cdot \gamma_j^{(m)}, \quad \forall j \in J$$
$$\sum_{m=1}^{M} \gamma_j^{(m)} = x_j, \quad \forall j \in J$$

**8. Variable Domains:**
$$x_j, y_{ij}, \gamma_j^{(m)} \in \{0, 1\}$$
$$K_j \in \mathbb{Z}^+$$
