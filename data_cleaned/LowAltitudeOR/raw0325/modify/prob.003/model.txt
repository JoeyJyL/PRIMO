### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the Location-Routing Problem (LRP).

Let's denote:

- $ y_k $: a binary variable indicating whether depot $ k $ is open (1) or not (0), for all $ k \in D $.
- $ x_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating if a UAV traverses the arc from node $ i $ to node $ j $ (1) or not (0), for all $ (i,j) \in A $.
- $ u_i $: a continuous variable representing the cumulative distance traveled upon arriving at node $ i $, for all $ i \in N $.

Thus, we have:

- $ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in D $.
- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ (i,j) \in A $.
- $ u_i \ge 0 $ for all $ i \in N $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which includes the fixed opening costs for depots and the variable routing costs (distance).

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{k \in D} F_k y_k + \sum_{(i,j) \in A} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ F_k $ is the fixed opening cost for depot $ k $.
- $ c_{ij} $ is the distance from node $ i $ to node $ j $.
- $ D $ is the set of candidate depots and $ A $ is the set of arcs.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Target Coverage Constraint:**

Each target point must be entered and left exactly once.

For each target $ j \in T $:

$$
\sum_{i \in N} x_{ij} = 1
$$

For each target $ i \in T $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N} x_{ij} = 1
$$

2. **Depot Logic & Flow Conservation Constraint:**

Routes must start and end at the same open depot. If a route leaves depot $ k $, then depot $ k $ must be open.

For each depot $ k \in D $:

$$
\sum_{j \in T} x_{kj} = \sum_{j \in T} x_{jk}
$$

$$
\sum_{j \in T} x_{kj} \le M \cdot y_k
$$

Where $ M $ is a Big-M parameter.

3. **Subtour Elimination & Connectivity (MTZ) Constraint:**

Ensure valid loops connected to depots and eliminate subtours.

For depot nodes: $ u_k = 0 $ for all $ k \in D $.

For arcs from any node to a target node:

$$
u_j \ge u_i + c_{ij} - M(1 - x_{ij}) \quad \forall i \in N, j \in T
$$

4. **Range Constraint:**

The total distance of any route must not exceed the maximum UAV range $ L = 22.0 $ km.

For each target $ i \in T $ and depot $ k \in D $:

$$
u_i + c_{ik} \le L + M(1 - x_{ik})
$$

Also:

$$
u_i \le L \quad \forall i \in T
$$

5. **No Depot-to-Depot Arcs:**

$$
\sum_{k_1 \in D} \sum_{k_2 \in D, k_2 \ne k_1} x_{k_1 k_2} = 0
$$

6. **Binary and Non-negativity Constraints:**

$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad y_k \in \{0, 1\}, \quad u_i \ge 0
$$
