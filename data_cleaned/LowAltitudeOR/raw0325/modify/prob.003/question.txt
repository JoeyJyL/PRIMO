The City Police Department plans to deploy a UAV network for daily patrols of key urban areas.
There are 2 candidate locations for building UAV hangars (Depots) and 4 designated surveillance points (Targets) that must be visited.
**Operational Rules:**
1. **Location Decision:** Select which depots to open. Each open depot incurs a fixed construction cost.
2. **Routing:** Assign UAVs to open depots. Each UAV starts from a depot, visits a sequence of targets, and **must return to the same depot**.
3. **Constraints:**
   - Every target must be visited exactly once.
   - The total flight distance of any route must not exceed the UAV's maximum battery range ($L$).

**Parameters:**
- **UAV Max Range ($L$):** 22.0 km (reduced from 30.0 km due to recently enacted municipal drone battery safety regulations that require a 25% energy reserve at all times)
- **Travel Cost:** $1.0 per km

**Locations (Coordinates in km):**
*Candidate Depots:*
- Depot A (ID: 1): (0, 0)   | Fixed Cost: 100
- Depot B (ID: 2): (10, 0)  | Fixed Cost: 120 (More expensive land)

*Target Points:*
- T1 (ID: 3): (0, 5)
- T2 (ID: 4): (2, 5)
- T3 (ID: 5): (8, 5)
- T4 (ID: 6): (10, 5)

**Goal:**
Minimize the Total Cost = (Sum of Fixed Costs of Open Depots) + (Total Flight Distance Cost).
