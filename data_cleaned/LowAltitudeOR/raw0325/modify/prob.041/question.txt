SkyCity UAM Inc. aims to deploy air taxi vertiports in a metropolitan area to maximize total ridership coverage.
The company wants to select a limited number of vertiport sites from candidates to maximize covered passenger demand.
The multi-objective model simultaneously maximizes ridership, minimizes facility cost, and minimizes travel distance, scalarized via weighted sum.

**Operational Constraints:**
1. **Hub Proximity:** Each selected vertiport must be within the hub distance threshold ($r$) of at least one existing mobility hub (metro station, train station, or airport), enabling seamless multimodal transfers.
2. **Customer Coverage:** A demand point is considered "covered" if at least one selected vertiport is within the service radius ($R$). This ensures air taxi passengers can access the vertiport within acceptable ground travel distance.
3. **Budget:** The total number of selected vertiports cannot exceed $p$.
4. **Assignment:** Each demand point must be assigned to exactly one selected vertiport.

**Budget Reduction:** Due to fiscal tightening, the company's budget has been reduced, and the maximum number of vertiports is now $p = 2$ (previously $p = 3$). This forces harder trade-offs between ridership coverage, cost, and travel distance.

**Parameters:**
- Service Radius ($R$): 3.0 km
- Hub Distance Threshold ($r$): 1.5 km
- Max Vertiports ($p$): 2
- Facility cost per vertiport ($c_c + c_o$): 500 (hundred USD)
- Objective weights: $w_1 = 1.0$ (ridership), $w_2 = 0.001$ (cost), $w_3 = 0.01$ (distance)
