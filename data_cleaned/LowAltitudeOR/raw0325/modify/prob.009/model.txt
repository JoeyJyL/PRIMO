### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ x_j $: a binary variable indicating if charging station $ j $ is opened.
- $ w_i $: a binary variable indicating if target zone $ i $ is covered.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in J $.
- $ w_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in I $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the total number of covered target zones under the station budget.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in I} w_i
$$

Where:

- $ I $ is the set of target zones.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Coverage Logic Constraint:**

A target zone $ i $ can only be marked as covered if at least one open station covers it.

For each target zone $ i \in I $:

$$
w_i \le \sum_{j \in J} a_{ij} x_j
$$

Where:

- $ a_{ij} $ is a binary parameter, $ a_{ij} = 1 $ if $ d_{ij} \le R_{max} $, else 0.

2. **Station Budget Constraint:**

At most 3 stations can be opened due to funding limitations.

$$
\sum_{j \in J} x_j \le 3
$$

3. **Binary Constraints:**

The decision variables must be binary.

For all $ j \in J $:

$$
x_j \in \{0, 1\}
$$

For all $ i \in I $:

$$
w_i \in \{0, 1\}
$$
