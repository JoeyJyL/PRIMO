A UAM (Urban Air Mobility) system operates multiple aircraft in a 2D urban area with ground base stations (BSs). Each aircraft must fly from its departure point toward its destination while jointly optimizing its flight trajectory and the bandwidth allocation from the serving BS.
**Goal:** Maximize the combined objective of remaining battery power (energy efficiency) and information freshness (minimizing Age of Information, AoI) across all aircraft, subject to collision avoidance, bandwidth sharing, and AoI threshold constraints.
**Service Logic:**
1. **Trajectory Control:** At each discrete time slot, each aircraft selects one of 8 compass directions (E, NE, N, NW, W, SW, S, SE) with a fixed step size of 50 m.
2. **Bandwidth Allocation:** Each serving BS allocates a discrete fraction of its total bandwidth to each aircraft. An aircraft's data is considered "fresh" (AoI resets to 0) only if the achieved data rate is sufficient to transmit the full data packet in one slot.
3. **Collision Avoidance:** Any two aircraft must maintain a minimum separation distance at all times.

**Parameters:**
- **Number of aircraft (M):** 2.
- **Number of base stations (N):** 4 (2×2 grid).
- **Time horizon (T):** 10 time slots.
- **Urban area:** 500 × 500 m.
- **Aircraft altitude:** h = 100 m (fixed).
- **Speed:** v = 50 m/s (one step per slot).
- **Total bandwidth per BS:** B_max = 10 MHz.
- **Bandwidth levels:** {0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8} of B_max.
- **Transmit power:** P_tran = 1 W.
- **Carrier frequency:** f = 2.8 GHz.
- **Antenna gains:** G_tran = G_rec = 2.
- **Noise PSD:** σ² = −174 dBm/Hz.
- **Data packet size:** α_max = 65 Mbits.
- **Max AoI threshold:** AoI_max = 5 time slots.
- **Initial remaining power:** P_init = 50 (normalized units).
- **Max power budget:** P_max = 100 (normalized units).
- **Power per time slot (simplified):** P_slot = 5 units/slot.
- **Minimum UAM-UAM separation:** D_col = 50 m.
- **No obstacles in simplified instance.**

**Base Station Positions:**
- **BS1:** (125, 125, 0).
- **BS2:** (375, 125, 0).
- **BS3:** (125, 375, 0).
- **BS4:** (375, 375, 0).

**Aircraft Start/Destination:**
- **Aircraft 1:** Start (50, 50, 100), Destination (450, 450, 100).
- **Aircraft 2:** Start (450, 50, 100), Destination (50, 450, 100).

**Communication Model:**
- Distance: d_m,n(t) = sqrt((x_m(t)−x_n)² + (y_m(t)−y_n)² + (h_m−h_n)²).
- Free-space path loss: η = (4πf·d/c)².
- Received power: P_rec = P_tran·G_tran·G_rec / η.
- Data rate: R_m,n(t) = β_m,n(t)·B_max·log₂(1 + SNR).

**AoI Update Rule:**
- AoI resets to 0 if the data rate in that slot ≥ α_max (full packet transmitted).
- AoI increments by 1 otherwise.

**Question:** For the simplified instance, find the optimal trajectory (sequence of directions) and bandwidth allocation for each aircraft over T=10 time slots to maximize the combined objective of remaining power and AoI freshness, subject to collision avoidance, bandwidth capacity, and AoI threshold constraints.
