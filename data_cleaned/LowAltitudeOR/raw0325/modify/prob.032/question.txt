A metropolitan city transport authority is planning to launch an Air Taxi (eVTOL) service for airport access. The goal is to select optimal **Skyport** locations from a set of candidate zones to **maximize total Air Taxi Revenue** from passengers traveling to airports.

**Goal:** Select p = 2 skyport locations (from 5 candidate zones) to maximize the total revenue generated from air taxi trips to 2 airports, considering travelers' mode choice behavior between ground taxi and air taxi, subject to a mutual exclusion constraint on certain skyport pairs.

**Problem Structure (Single-Allocation Hub Location with Elastic Demand):**
- The city has 5 origin zones, each generating demand to 2 airports.
- Each origin zone is also a candidate skyport location.
- Each origin-airport pair is assigned to exactly **one** skyport (single allocation).
- Travelers choose between **Ground Taxi** (direct to airport) and **Air Taxi** (ground access to skyport + flight to airport) based on a **Binary Logit Mode Choice Model**.
- The air taxi choice probability is pre-computed for each origin-skyport-airport combination and used as an objective coefficient (linearization).
- **Mutual Exclusion:** Skyports at locations 1 and 2 cannot both be opened simultaneously due to airspace conflict in their overlapping flight corridors.

**Mode Choice Model (Binary Logit):**
- Ground Taxi Utility: V_ground = 0.0313 × TripTime(min) − 0.0125 × TripFare($)
- Air Taxi Utility: V_air = 0.018 × AerialDist(miles) − 0.0213 × TotalAirFare($)
- Choice Probability: P_air = exp(V_air) / (exp(V_air) + exp(V_ground))

**Fare Structure:**
- Ground Taxi Fare: BaseFee($3) + $1.50/mile + $0.30/min
- Air Taxi Flight Fare: $5.73/air-mile (Short Term pricing)
- Transfer Cost: $0.30/min × 15 min = $4.50 (total transfer time at skyports)
- Total Air Fare: GroundAccessFare + TransferCost + FlightFare
