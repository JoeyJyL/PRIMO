"LongHaul Drone Co." needs to establish a flight path from a central Warehouse to a remote Customer.
**Problem:**
- Direct flight distance is 22 km.
- Drone Maximum Range is **12 km** (reduced from 15 km due to updated battery degradation model under cold weather conditions).
- Direct flight is impossible without a stop.
- We must open at least one **Charging Station (CS)** to bridge the gap.

**Candidate Stations:**
- **CS1 (Mid-point Direct):** Located exactly halfway (11km). 
  - Flight Path: 0 -> CS1 -> Dest. (11 + 11 = 22 km). Each leg 11 km ≤ 12 km. **Feasible.**
  - **Fixed Cost:** $500 (Premium location).
- **CS2 (Detour North):** Located slightly off-path.
  - Flight Path: 0 -> CS2 -> Dest. (12.08 + 12.08 = 24.16 km). Each leg 12.08 km > 12 km. **Infeasible.**
  - **Fixed Cost:** $100 (Cheap land).
- **CS3 (Detour South):** Symmetric to CS2. Also infeasible (12.08 km > 12 km).

**Parameters:**
- **Operational Cost:** $1.0 per km.
- **Goal:** Minimize Total Cost = Fixed Cost + Operational Cost.

**Trade-off Analysis:**
- **Option A (CS1):** Cost = 500 (Fixed) + 22 (Ops) = **522**. Feasible.
- **Option B (CS2):** Each leg = 12.08 km > 12 km range. **Infeasible.**
- **Decision:** Only CS1 is reachable under the reduced range.
