1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP
2. Problem Size: Small

### Sets:

- $ \mathcal{F} = \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $: Set of UAV flights.
- $ \mathcal{P} = \{0, 1\} $: Set of alternative paths (0 = optimal, 1 = alternative).
- $ \mathcal{C} = \{C1, C2, C3, C4\} $: Set of spatial conflict flight-path pairs.

### Parameters:

- $ s_i $: Scheduled departure time of flight $ i $ (seconds).
- $ c_{ij} $: Path cost of flight $ i $ taking path $ j $.
- $ r_i $: Unit delay cost when delay $ \leq d $.
- $ R_i = 2r_i $: Unit delay cost when delay $ > d $.
- $ d = 300 $: Delay cost threshold (seconds).
- $ t_0 = 10 $: Safety separation buffer (seconds).
- $ M = 10000 $: Big-M constant.
- $ b_c, e_c, B_c, E_c $: Travel times to entry/exit points of conflict regions.

### Decision Variables:

- $ I_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $, $ j \in \mathcal{P} $.
- $ t_i \geq 0 $ (continuous) for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ D_i \geq 0 $ (continuous) for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ z_c, X_c, x_c \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ c \in \mathcal{C} $.

### Objective Function:

Minimize:

$$
\sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} D_i + \sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} c_{ij} \cdot I_{ij}
$$

### Constraints:

1. **Piecewise Linear Delay Cost:**

$ D_i \geq r_i (t_i - s_i) $ and $ D_i \geq r_i d + R_i (t_i - s_i - d) $ for all $ i $

2. **Conflict Resolution (i first):**

$ t_i + e_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - X_c) + B_c + t_m $ for each $ c = (i,j,m,n) $

3. **Conflict Resolution (m first):**

$ t_m + E_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - x_c) + b_c + t_i $ for each $ c = (i,j,m,n) $

4. **Linearization of $ z_c $:**

$ z_c \leq I_{ij} $, $ z_c \leq I_{mn} $, $ z_c + 1 \geq I_{ij} + I_{mn} $

5. **Conflict Ordering:**

$ X_c + x_c = z_c $ for all $ c $

6. **Departure Feasibility:**

$ t_i \geq s_i $ for all $ i $

7. **Path Selection:**

$ \sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} I_{ij} = 1 $ for all $ i $

8. **Mandatory Rerouting (Airspace Restriction):**

Flight 1 must take the alternative path:

$$
I_{1,1} = 1
$$

9. **Variable Domains:**

$ I_{ij}, z_c, X_c, x_c \in \{0, 1\} $; $ t_i, D_i \geq 0 $
