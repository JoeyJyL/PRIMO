SkyCity Transport Authority is planning a deterministic Urban Air Mobility (UAM) network to serve known daily commuter demands between key urban zones.

**Goal:** Select exactly $p$ vertiport locations to build from a set of candidate locations to maximize the Total Profit (Total Revenue - Total Operating Cost - Total Fixed Construction Cost).

**Operational Constraints:**
- Exactly $p$ vertiports must be selected and built.
- An OD pair can either be fully served by the UAM network or remain unserved.
- If an OD pair is served, all its demand must be routed through exactly one origin vertiport and one destination vertiport.
- Demand can only be routed through vertiports that are selected and built.

| Vertiport ID | Name           | Fixed Cost $f_i$ ($/day) |
|--------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| 1            | Downtown       | 5000                     |
| 2            | Tech Park      | 4500                     |
| 3            | Airport        | 6000                     |
| 4            | University     | 4000                     |
| 5            | Shopping Mall  | 5500                     |

| 4          | Zone C      | Zone D    | 100                       | 120                      |
| 5          | Zone A      | Zone D    | 50                        | 180                      |
