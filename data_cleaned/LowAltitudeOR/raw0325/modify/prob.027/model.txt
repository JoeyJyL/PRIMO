Identifying the decision variables, objective function, and constraints for the deterministic UAM vertiport siting and demand routing problem.

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ x_i $: a binary variable indicating whether candidate vertiport $ i $ is selected to be built (1) or not (0), where $ i \in M $.
- $ y_{km}^{tr} $: a binary variable indicating whether the demand for OD pair $ tr $ is routed via origin vertiport $ k $ and destination vertiport $ m $ (1) or not (0), where $ tr \in TR $, and $ k, m \in M $ ($ k \neq m $).

Thus, we have:

- $ x_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i = 1, 2, ..., 5 $.
- $ y_{km}^{tr} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ tr = 1, 2, ..., 6 $ and $ k, m \in M $ ($ k \neq m $).

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the total profit, which is the sum of the revenue from served demands minus the routing operating costs and the fixed costs of building the vertiports.

The objective function is:

$$
\max \sum_{tr \in TR} \sum_{k \in M} \sum_{m \in M, m \neq k} d^{tr} (R^{tr} - c_{km}) y_{km}^{tr} - \sum_{i \in M} f_i x_i
$$

Where:

- $ d^{tr} (R^{tr} - c_{km}) $ represents the net profit of serving all passengers of OD pair $ tr $ through vertiports $ k $ and $ m $.
- $ f_i $ is the fixed cost of opening vertiport $ i $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Vertiport Siting Constraint (Budget Reduction):**

Exactly $ p = 2 $ vertiports must be selected from the candidate set $ M $.

$$
\sum_{i \in M} x_i = 2
$$

2. **Routing Validity Constraints (Origin):**

An OD pair can only be routed through origin vertiport $ k $ if vertiport $ k $ is selected and built.

For each OD pair $ tr \in TR $ and each origin vertiport $ k \in M $:

$$
\sum_{m \in M, m \neq k} y_{km}^{tr} \leq x_k
$$

3. **Routing Validity Constraints (Destination):**

An OD pair can only be routed through destination vertiport $ m $ if vertiport $ m $ is selected and built.

For each OD pair $ tr \in TR $ and each destination vertiport $ m \in M $:

$$
\sum_{k \in M, k \neq m} y_{km}^{tr} \leq x_m
$$

4. **Single Routing Constraint:**

Each OD pair $ tr $ can be served at most once (it is either routed through exactly one pair of vertiports or not served at all).

For each OD pair $ tr \in TR $:

$$
\sum_{k \in M} \sum_{m \in M, m \neq k} y_{km}^{tr} \leq 1
$$

5. **Binary Constraints:**

The decision variables must be binary.

For all $ i \in M $:

$$
x_i \in \{0, 1\}
$$

For all $ tr \in TR $ and $ k, m \in M $ ($ k \neq m $):

$$
y_{km}^{tr} \in \{0, 1\}
$$
