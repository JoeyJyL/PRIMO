### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

**Sets:**
- $D$: Set of candidate Fulfillment Centers (FCs), indexed by $s$
- $C$: Set of customers, indexed by $i, j$
- $V = D \cup C$: Set of all nodes
- $L = \{0, 1, ..., N\}$: Set of levels in the multi-layer network (level $r$ represents the $r$-th customer from the end of a route)

**Decision Variables:**
- $z_s \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if FC $s$ is opened; 0 otherwise.
- $x_i^r \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if customer $i$ is visited at level $r$; 0 otherwise.
- $y_{ij}^r \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if node $j$ is visited immediately after node $i$ with $r$ nodes remaining to visit; 0 otherwise.
- $v_{sj} \geq 0$: Load carried by drone on arc $(s, j)$ departing from FC $s$.
- $u_{ij} \geq 0$: Load carried by drone on arc $(i, j)$ after serving customer $i$.
- $e_i \geq 0$: Accumulated energy consumption upon arrival at node $i$.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total latency (sum of customers' waiting times) under worst-case flight time uncertainty.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{c \in C, c \neq i} \sum_{r \in L} r \cdot (\bar{t}_{ic} + \rho \cdot \hat{t}_{ic}) \cdot y_{ic}^r
$$

Where:
- $\bar{t}_{ic}$: Nominal flight time on arc $(i, c)$
- $\hat{t}_{ic}$: Uncertainty margin for flight time on arc $(i, c)$
- $\rho$: Robustness parameter controlling the level of conservatism
- $r$: Level number representing how many customers remain after visiting customer $c$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

**1. Customer Visit Constraints:**
Each customer must be visited exactly once (at exactly one level):

$$
\sum_{r \in L} x_i^r = 1 \quad \forall i \in C
$$

**2. Drone Dispatch Constraints:**
Exactly $k = 1$ drone is dispatched:

$$
\sum_{s \in D} \sum_{j \in C} \sum_{r \in L, r \neq 1} y_{sj}^r = 1
$$

**3. Drone Retrieval Constraints:**
The dispatched drone must be retrieved:

$$
\sum_{i \in C} x_i^1 = 1
$$

**4. Flow Conservation Constraints:**
Connectivity between consecutive visits:

$$
\sum_{j \in C, j \neq i} y_{ij}^r = x_i^{r+1} \quad \forall i \in C, \forall r \in L, r \neq N
$$

$$
\sum_{s \in D} y_{js}^0 = x_j^1 \quad \forall j \in C
$$

$$
\sum_{i \in V, i \neq j} y_{ij}^r = x_j^r \quad \forall j \in C, \forall r \in L, r \neq N
$$

**5. FC Opening Constraints:**
A route can only start from an opened FC:

$$
z_s \geq y_{sj}^r \quad \forall s \in D, \forall j \in C, \forall r \in L, r \neq 1
$$

**6. Maximum FC Constraint:**
Limit the number of opened FCs:

$$
\sum_{s \in D} z_s \leq DS
$$

**7. FC Capacity Constraints:**
Each FC can host at most $\phi$ drones:

$$
\sum_{j \in C} \sum_{r \in L} y_{sj}^r \leq \phi \quad \forall s \in D
$$

**8. Payload Capacity Constraints:**
Load bounds for arcs from FCs:

$$
v_{sj} \geq d_j \sum_{r \in L, r \neq 1} y_{sj}^r \quad \forall s \in D, \forall j \in C
$$

$$
v_{sj} \leq Q \sum_{r \in L, r \neq 1} y_{sj}^r \quad \forall s \in D, \forall j \in C
$$

Load bounds for arcs between customers:

$$
u_{ij} \geq d_j \sum_{r \in L, r \neq N} y_{ij}^r \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j
$$

$$
u_{ij} \leq (Q - d_i) \sum_{r \in L, r \neq N} y_{ij}^r \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j
$$

**9. Load Flow Conservation:**

$$
\sum_{s \in D} v_{si} + \sum_{j \in C, j \neq i} u_{ji} - \sum_{j \in C, j \neq i} u_{ij} = d_i \quad \forall i \in C
$$

**10. Robust Energy Consumption Constraints:**
Energy accumulation from FC to first customer:

$$
e_s + k'(W + M + v_{sj})^{1.5} \cdot (\bar{t}_{sj} + \rho \cdot \hat{t}_{sj}) \leq e_j + M_{sj}(1 - \sum_{r \in L, r \neq 1} y_{sj}^r) \quad \forall s \in D, \forall j \in C
$$

Energy accumulation between consecutive customers:

$$
e_i + k'(W + M + u_{ij})^{1.5} \cdot (\bar{t}_{ij} + \rho \cdot \hat{t}_{ij}) \leq e_j + M'_{ij}(1 - \sum_{r \in L, r \neq N} y_{ij}^r) \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j
$$

Battery capacity constraint at route end:

$$
e_j + k'(W + M)^{1.5} \cdot \sum_{s \in D} (\bar{t}_{js} + \rho \cdot \hat{t}_{js}) \cdot y_{js}^0 \leq B \quad \forall j \in C
$$

Initial energy at FCs:

$$
e_s = 0 \quad \forall s \in D
$$

**11. Variable Domains:**

$$
z_s \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall s \in D
$$

$$
x_i^r \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in C, \forall r \in L
$$

$$
y_{ij}^r \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i, j \in V, i \neq j, \forall r \in L
$$

$$
v_{sj}, u_{ij}, e_i \geq 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $z_s \in \{0, 1\}$: FC opening decisions
- $x_i^r \in \{0, 1\}$: Customer-level assignment
- $y_{ij}^r \in \{0, 1\}$: Arc selection at each level
- $v_{sj}, u_{ij} \geq 0$: Load flow variables
- $e_i \geq 0$: Energy accumulation variables
