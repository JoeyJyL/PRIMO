### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition
 
Let's define the decision variables for the problem.
 
Let's denote:
 
- $ x_{i,s} $: a binary variable equal to 1 if aircraft $ i $ is assigned to landing sequence position $ s $, and 0 otherwise.
- $ AETA_i $: a continuous variable representing the adjusted estimated time of arrival of aircraft $ i $.
- $ d_i^{+} $: a continuous variable representing the positive (late) deviation, i.e., $ \max(AETA_i - STA_i, 0) $.
- $ d_i^{-} $: a continuous variable representing the negative (early) deviation, i.e., $ \max(STA_i - AETA_i, 0) $.
 
Thus, we have:
 
- $ x_{i,s} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in N $, $ s \in S $.
- $ AETA_i \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in N $.
- $ d_i^{+}, d_i^{-} \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in N $.
 
### Step 2: Objective Function Construction
 
The goal is to minimize the total weighted absolute deviation between each aircraft's adjusted arrival time and its scheduled arrival time. Early arrivals receive weight $ \alpha $ and late arrivals receive weight $ 1 - \alpha $.
 
The objective function is:
 
$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{i \in N} \left[ \alpha \cdot d_i^{-} + (1 - \alpha) \cdot d_i^{+} \right]
$$
 
Where:
 
- $ \alpha = 0.4 $ is the early operation weight.
- $ d_i^{-} = \max(STA_i - AETA_i, 0) $ is the early deviation.
- $ d_i^{+} = \max(AETA_i - STA_i, 0) $ is the late deviation.
 
### Step 3: Constraint Construction
 
1. **Assignment Constraints (each aircraft gets exactly one sequence position):**
 
For each aircraft $ i \in N $:
 
$$
\sum_{s \in S} x_{i,s} = 1
$$
 
2. **Assignment Constraints (each sequence position is occupied by exactly one aircraft):**
 
For each position $ s \in S $:
 
$$
\sum_{i \in N} x_{i,s} = 1
$$
 
3. **AETA Lower Bound (ETA):**
 
The adjusted arrival time cannot be earlier than the physical estimated time of arrival:
 
$$
AETA_i \geq ETA_i \quad \forall i \in N
$$
 
4. **AETA Lower Bound (Sequence-based time):**
 
The adjusted arrival time must respect the minimum landing separation based on the assigned sequence position:
 
$$
AETA_i \geq \sum_{s \in S} x_{i,s} \cdot \left[ \Delta L \times (s - 1) + t_0 \right] \quad \forall i \in N
$$
 
5. **Deviation Definition:**
 
$$
d_i^{+} - d_i^{-} = AETA_i - STA_i \quad \forall i \in N
$$
 
$$
d_i^{+}, d_i^{-} \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in N
$$
 
6. **Diplomatic Priority Constraint (A6 must land in positions 1-3):**
 
Aircraft A6 is carrying a government delegation and must be assigned to one of the first 3 landing sequence positions:
 
$$
\sum_{s=1}^{3} x_{A6, s} = 1
$$
 
7. **Binary Constraints:**
 
$$
x_{i,s} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in N, s \in S
$$
 
### Summary of the Mathematical Model
 
**Sets:**
 
- $ N = \{A1, A2, A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8\} $: set of arriving aircraft.
- $ S = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8\} $: set of sequence positions.
 
**Parameters:**
 
- $ ETA_i $: estimated time of arrival of aircraft $ i $ (seconds).
- $ STA_i $: scheduled time of arrival of aircraft $ i $ (seconds).
- $ \Delta L = 40 $: minimum landing separation time (seconds).
- $ t_0 = 0 $: current time (seconds).
- $ \alpha = 0.4 $: early operation weight.
