### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ y_s $: a binary variable equal to 1 if station site $ s $ is opened, and 0 otherwise.
- $ z_e $: a binary variable equal to 1 if trip $ e $ is covered (both pickup and drop-off within range of some open station), and 0 otherwise.

Thus, we have:

- $ y_s \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ s \in S $.
- $ z_e \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ e \in E $.

### Step 2: Pre-processing

Before building the model, we pre-compute coverage relationships using the Haversine distance formula:

$$
d(i,j) = r \times \text{hav}^{-1}\left(\text{hav}(\gamma_j - \gamma_i) + \cos(\gamma_i) \cos(\gamma_j) \text{hav}(\lambda_j - \lambda_i)\right)
$$

where $ r = 3958.8 $ miles (Earth's radius), $ \gamma $ = latitude, $ \lambda $ = longitude.

We define:

- $ P_{es} = 1 $ if the pickup location of trip $ e $ is within $ R $ miles of station $ s $, 0 otherwise.
- $ Q_{es} = 1 $ if the drop-off location of trip $ e $ is within $ R $ miles of station $ s $, 0 otherwise.

### Step 3: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total fixed cost of opening stations:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{s \in S} F_s \cdot y_s
$$

Where $ F_s $ is the annualized fixed cost of opening station $ s $.

### Step 4: Constraint Construction

1. **Pickup Coverage:**

A trip's pickup is covered if at least one open station is within range:

$$
\sum_{s \in S} P_{es} \cdot y_s \geq z_e \quad \forall e \in E
$$

2. **Drop-off Coverage:**

A trip's drop-off is covered if at least one open station is within range:

$$
\sum_{s \in S} Q_{es} \cdot y_s \geq z_e \quad \forall e \in E
$$

3. **Minimum Demand Fulfillment (Coverage Rate):**

At least $ CS \times |E| $ trips must be covered:

$$
\sum_{e \in E} z_e \geq \lceil CS \times |E| \rceil
$$

4. **Mandatory Station Constraint (S4 must be opened):**

Due to federal aviation security regulations, the World Trade Center station must be opened:

$$
y_{S4} = 1
$$

5. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
y_s \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall s \in S, \qquad z_e \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall e \in E
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Sets:**

- $ S = \{S1, S2, \ldots, S10\} $: set of candidate station sites.
- $ E = \{T1, T2, \ldots, T20\} $: set of estimated air taxi trips (demand points).

**Parameters:**

- $ F_s $: fixed cost of opening station $ s $ (\$K/year).
- $ R = 1.0 $ mile: maximum on-road travel distance.
- $ CS = 0.70 $: minimum demand fulfillment rate (70%).
- $ P_{es} \in \{0,1\} $: 1 if pickup of trip $ e $ is within $ R $ miles of station $ s $.
- $ Q_{es} \in \{0,1\} $: 1 if drop-off of trip $ e $ is within $ R $ miles of station $ s $.

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_s \in \{0, 1\} $: whether to open station $ s $.
- $ z_e \in \{0, 1\} $: whether trip $ e $ is covered.

**Objective Function:**

Minimize:

$$
Z = \sum_{s \in S} F_s \cdot y_s
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Pickup coverage:**
$$
\sum_{s \in S} P_{es} \cdot y_s \geq z_e \quad \forall e \in E
$$

2. **Drop-off coverage:**
$$
\sum_{s \in S} Q_{es} \cdot y_s \geq z_e \quad \forall e \in E
$$

3. **Minimum coverage rate:**
$$
\sum_{e \in E} z_e \geq \lceil CS \times |E| \rceil
$$

4. **Mandatory station (S4):**
$$
y_{S4} = 1
$$

5. **Binary constraints:**
$$
y_s \in \{0, 1\}, \quad z_e \in \{0, 1\}
$$

1. Optimization Problem Classification: ILP
2. Problem Scale: Small
