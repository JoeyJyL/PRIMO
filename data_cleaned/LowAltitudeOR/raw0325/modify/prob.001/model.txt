### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ X_j $: a binary variable indicating whether station site $ j $ is selected/active (1) or not (0).
- $ Y_k $: a binary variable indicating whether customer $ k $ is covered (1) or not (0).
- $ Z_{ij} $: the integer flow amount representing path connection from site $ i $ to site $ j $.

Thus, we have:

- $ X_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in S $.
- $ Y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in C $.
- $ Z_{ij} \ge 0 $ and integer for all $ i, j \in S $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the total covered demand of the customers.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Maximize } \sum_{k \in C} a_k Y_k
$$

Where:

- $ C $ is the set of customer nodes.
- $ a_k $ is the demand weight of customer $ k $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Coverage Definition:**

A customer $ k $ is covered only if at least one active station is within the allowable range ($ N_k $).

For each customer $ k \in C $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N_k} X_j \ge Y_k
$$

Where:
- $ N_k $ is the set of candidate sites within distance $ f_d $ of customer $ k $.

2. **Station Budget:**

The total number of active stations (including the warehouse) must not exceed the budget $ p $.

$$
\sum_{i \in S} X_i = p
$$

Where:
- $ S $ is the set of all candidate station sites.
- $ p $ is the maximum number of stations to locate.

3. **Flow Capacity:**

Flow can only exist on a link from $ i $ to $ j $ if station $ i $ is active.

For all $ i \in S $:

$$
\sum_{j \in \Omega_i} Z_{ij} \le (M-1) X_i
$$

Where:
- $ \Omega_i $ is the set of candidate sites within distance $ f_p $ of site $ i $.
- $ M $ is a large number (e.g., $ |S| $).

4. **Flow Conservation (Connectivity to Warehouse):**

For any non-warehouse station $ i $, if it is active, it must send at least 1 unit of flow out (towards the warehouse) more than it receives. This ensures a valid path back to the root.

For all $ i \in S \setminus W $:

$$
\sum_{j \in \Omega_i} Z_{ij} - \sum_{j \in \Omega_i} Z_{ji} \ge X_i
$$

Where:
- $ W $ is the set of warehouses.

5. **Root Node Flow Sink:**

The warehouse absorbs the flow from the network.

For all $ i \in W $:

$$
\sum_{j \in \Omega_i} Z_{ij} - \sum_{j \in \Omega_i} Z_{ji} \le X_i - M
$$

6. **Mandatory Coverage (Medical Supply Depot):**

Customer C4 (the medical supply depot) must be covered regardless of the optimization outcome.

$$
Y_{C4} = 1
$$
