### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ r_{ijt} $: the relocation flow (number of empty flights) from vertiport $ i $ to vertiport $ j $ in time period $ t $.
- $ w_{it} $: the number of idle drones at vertiport $ i $ in time period $ t $.
- $ Fleet $: the global fleet size required.
- $ Spaces_i $: the capacity (number of parking spaces) required at vertiport $ i $.

Thus, we have:

- $ r_{ijt} \ge 0 $ for all $ i, j \in V $ and $ t \in T $.
- $ w_{it} \ge MinIdle $ for all $ i \in V $ and $ t \in T $.
- $ Fleet \ge 0 $.
- $ Spaces_i \ge 0 $ for all $ i \in V $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which consists of the fleet acquisition cost, the infrastructure (space) cost at vertiports, and the operational relocation costs.

The objective function is:

$$
\min Z = C_{fleet} \cdot Fleet + \sum_{i \in V} C_{space,i} \cdot Spaces_i + \sum_{t \in T} \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V} C_{reloc} \cdot r_{ijt}
$$

Where:

- $ C_{fleet} = 500 $ is the cost per drone.
- $ C_{space, i} $ is the cost per parking space at vertiport $ i $ (V1: $100, V2: $20, V3: $20).
- $ C_{reloc} = 5 $ is the cost per relocation flight.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1.  **Steady-State Flow Balance (Per Period):**

For each vertiport $ i \in V $ and time period $ t \in T $:

$$
\sum_{j \in V} (D_{jit} + r_{jit}) = \sum_{j \in V} (D_{ijt} + r_{ijt})
$$

Where:

- $ D_{ijt} $ is the demand from $ i $ to $ j $ in period $ t $.

2.  **Fleet Dimensioning Constraint:**

The total fleet size must be sufficient to cover all active drones (flying), charging drones, and idle drones in any time period.

For each time period $ t \in T $:

$$
Fleet \ge \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V} (D_{ijt} + r_{ijt}) (\tau_{fly} + \tau_{chg}) + \sum_{i \in V} w_{it}
$$

Where:

- $ \tau_{fly} = 0.5 $ hrs is the flight time.
- $ \tau_{chg} = 0.5 $ hrs is the charging time (increased from 0.3 hrs due to battery degradation).

3.  **Space Capacity Constraint:**

For each vertiport $ i \in V $ and time period $ t \in T $:

$$
Spaces_i \ge w_{it} + \tau_{chg} \sum_{j \in V} (D_{jit} + r_{jit})
$$

4.  **Non-negativity and Bound Constraints:**

$$
r_{ijt} \ge 0, \quad w_{it} \ge MinIdle, \quad Fleet \ge 0, \quad Spaces_i \ge 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**

- $ r_{ijt} \ge 0 $ for all $ i,j \in V, t \in T $.
- $ w_{it} \ge MinIdle $ for all $ i \in V, t \in T $.
- $ Fleet \ge 0 $.
- $ Spaces_i \ge 0 $ for all $ i \in V $.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\min Z = C_{fleet} \cdot Fleet + \sum_{i \in V} C_{space,i} \cdot Spaces_i + \sum_{t \in T} \sum_{i \in V} \sum_{j \in V} C_{reloc} \cdot r_{ijt}
$$

**Constraints:**

$$
\sum_{j \in V} (D_{jit} + r_{jit}) = \sum_{j \in V} (D_{ijt} + r_{ijt}), \quad \forall i \in V, t \in T
$$

$$
Fleet \ge \sum_{i,j} (D_{ijt} + r_{ijt}) (\tau_{fly} + \tau_{chg}) + \sum_{i} w_{it}, \quad \forall t \in T
$$

$$
Spaces_i \ge w_{it} + \tau_{chg} \sum_{j} (D_{jit} + r_{jit}), \quad \forall i \in V, t \in T
$$

$$
r_{ijt} \ge 0, \quad w_{it} \ge 1, \quad Fleet \ge 0, \quad Spaces_i \ge 0
$$
