### Step 1: Sets and Parameters

**Sets:**

- $ K $: set of vehicle concepts, $ k \in K = \{0, 1\} $ (TiltWing, LiftCruise).
- $ R $: set of recharging infrastructure concepts, $ r \in R = \{0, 1, 2\} $ (50kW, 150kW, 350kW).
- $ S $: set of trip sequences (one per vehicle), $ s \in S $.
- $ N_s $: set of trip labels belonging to sequence $ s $, $ i \in N_s $.
- $ A_s $: set of consecutive trip pairs $ (i, j) $ within sequence $ s $.

**Parameters (see detailed tables in question.txt):**

- $ m_k^{EW} $: empty weight of vehicle type $ k $ (kg).
- $ e_k^{acc}, e_k^{dec} $: hover power gradient (kW/kg) for vehicle $ k $.
- $ e_k^{cruise} $: cruise energy gradient (kWh/km/kg) for vehicle $ k $.
- $ m_p $: average passenger weight (kg).
- $ t^{acc}, t^{dec} $: fixed take-off/landing time (hours).
- $ v_{cruise} $: cruise speed (kph).
- $ t_m $: turnaround/maintenance time (hours).
- $ l_i $: horizontal trip distance of trip $ i $ (km).
- $ \bar{n}_i $: number of passengers on trip $ i $.
- $ d_{ij} $: deadheading distance between trip $ i $ and $ j $ (km).
- $ \delta_{ij} \in \{0,1\} $: 1 if deadheading flight occurs between $ i $ and $ j $.
- $ \tau_{ij} $: time gap between end of trip $ i $ and start of trip $ j $ (hours).
- $ s_r $: charging speed of infrastructure type $ r $ (kW).
- $ c_b, c_e $: battery cost (USD/kWh), energy cost (USD/kWh).
- $ n_V $: number of vehicles; $ n_{VP} $: number of stations; $ n_{PS} $: parking slots per station.
- $ \sigma $: annual operating days; $ a_k^{eVTOL} $: vehicle lifetime (years); $ \xi_k $: residual value fraction.
- $ \eta^{man}_k $: anti-collision maneuver factor.
- $ \rho^{energy} $: battery energy density (kWh/kg); $ \rho^{power} $: battery power density (kW/kg); $ \mu $: usable fraction.

### Step 2: Decision Variables

- $ Q_w \in \mathbb{N} $: battery weight of each vehicle (kg).
- $ k \in K $: selected vehicle concept (modeled via binary $ y_k $).
- $ r \in R $: selected charging infrastructure (modeled via binary $ y_r $).
- $ q_i \geq 0 $: battery state of charge before operating trip $ i $ (kWh).
- $ w_i \geq 0 $: energy recharged before operating trip $ i $ (kWh).
- $ u_i \geq 0 $: additional energy recharged after trip $ i $ before a deadheading flight (kWh).

Binary selection variables:

$$
\sum_{k \in K} y_k = 1, \quad \sum_{r \in R} y_r = 1
$$

### Step 3: Objective Function

Minimize daily Total Cost of Ownership:

$$
\min \; Z = \text{fleet} + \text{infra} + \text{oper.tr} + \text{oper.dh}
$$

**Fleet costs (Eq. 2):**

$$
\text{fleet} = \frac{n_V \cdot c_k^{EW} \cdot (1 - \xi_k)}{a_k^{eVTOL} \cdot \sigma} + \frac{c_b}{n^{RC,bat}} \sum_{s \in S} \sum_{i \in N_s} (w_i + u_i) + \frac{n_V \cdot c_k^{m}}{\sigma}
$$

where $ c_k^{EW} = m_k^{EW} \times 770 $ USD.

**Infrastructure costs (Eq. 3):**

$$
\text{infra} = \frac{n_{VP} \cdot n_{PS} \cdot (c_r^{hw} + c_r^{ins})}{a_r^{VP} \cdot \sigma}
$$

**Operating costs — passenger trips (Eq. 4):**

$$
\text{oper.tr} = c_e \sum_{s \in S} \sum_{i \in N_s} \eta^{man}_k \left[ e_k^{acc}(m_k^{EW} + Q_w + m_p \bar{n}_i) t^{acc} + e_k^{cruise}(m_k^{EW} + Q_w + m_p \bar{n}_i) l_i + e_k^{dec}(m_k^{EW} + Q_w + m_p \bar{n}_i) t^{dec} \right]
$$

**Operating costs — deadheading flights (Eq. 5):**

$$
\text{oper.dh} = c_e \sum_{s \in S} \sum_{(i,j) \in A_s} \delta_{ij} \cdot \eta^{man}_k \left[ e_k^{acc}(m_k^{EW} + Q_w) t^{acc} + e_k^{cruise}(m_k^{EW} + Q_w) d_{ij} + e_k^{dec}(m_k^{EW} + Q_w) t^{dec} \right]
$$

### Step 4: Constraints

**(6) Max battery SOC before each trip:**

$$
q_i + w_i \leq \mu \cdot Q_w \cdot \rho^{energy} \quad \forall s \in S, \; i \in N_s
$$

**(7) Max SOC including deadheading energy does not exceed capacity** (implicit in linkage).

**(8) Recharge limit before trip $ j $:**

$$
0 \leq w_j \leq s_r \cdot \left[ \delta_{ij} \cdot t_m + (1 - \delta_{ij}) \cdot \tau_{ij} \right] \quad \forall s \in S, \; (i,j) \in A_s
$$

**(9) Recharge limit before deadheading:**

$$
0 \leq u_i \leq s_r \cdot \delta_{ij} \left( \tau_{ij} - t_m - t^{acc} - t^{dec} - \frac{d_{ij}}{v_{cruise}} \right) \quad \forall s \in S, \; (i,j) \in A_s
$$

**(10) SOC linkage between consecutive trips:**

$$
q_j \leq q_i + w_i - E_i^{trip} + \delta_{ij}(u_i - E_{ij}^{dh}) \quad \forall s \in S, \; (i,j) \in A_s
$$

where $ E_i^{trip} $ and $ E_{ij}^{dh} $ are the energy consumption of trip $ i $ and deadheading flight $ (i,j) $ respectively.

**(11) Minimum energy for each trip:**

$$
q_i + w_i \geq E_i^{trip} \quad \forall s \in S, \; i \in N_s
$$

**(12) Minimum battery power:**

$$
Q_k^{p,min} \leq Q_w \cdot \rho^{power} \quad \forall k \in K
$$

**(13) Maximum take-off weight:**

$$
m_k^{EW} + Q_w + m_p \cdot n^{PAX,MATSim} \leq m_k^{max} \quad \forall k \in K
$$

**(14) Passenger capacity:** $ n^{PAX,MATSim} \leq n_k^{PAX} $

**(15) Speed constraint:** $ v_{cruise} \leq v_k^{max} $

**(16) Integer battery weight:** $ Q_w \in \mathbb{N} $

### Summary

**Decision Variables:** $ Q_w $ (integer), $ y_k $ (binary, 2 vars), $ y_r $ (binary, 3 vars), $ q_i, w_i, u_i $ (continuous, 18 vars). Total: ~23 decision variables + auxiliaries.

**Constraints:** 6 SOC upper bounds, 6 min energy, 4 SOC linkages, 4 recharge limits, 4 u-limits, 2 init conditions, feasibility constraints, selection constraints. Total: ~30+ constraints.

**Objective:** Minimize daily TCO (linear in decision variables given fixed k, r; bilinear terms linearized via indicator constraints).

Classification: MILP
Scale: Small
