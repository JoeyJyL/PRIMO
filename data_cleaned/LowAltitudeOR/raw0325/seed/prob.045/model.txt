Identifying the decision variables, objective function, and constraints for the Flying Sidekick Traveling Salesman Problem (FSTSP) based on the original paper "The flying sidekick traveling salesman problem: Optimization of drone-assisted parcel delivery" by Murray and Chu (2015).

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the FSTSP problem.

Let's denote:

- C = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5}: Set of all customers.
- C' = {1, 2, 3, 4}: Set of drone-eligible customers (customer 5 is truck-only).
- N_0 = {0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5}: Nodes from which a vehicle may depart (depot + customers).
- N_+ = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}: Nodes to which a vehicle may visit (customers + ending depot).
- P: Set of valid drone sorties (i, j, k) where:
  - i ∈ N_0 (launch node)
  - j ∈ C' (drone-served customer, j ≠ i)
  - k ∈ N_+ (retrieval node, k ≠ j, k ≠ i)
  - σ_ij + σ_jk ≤ e (endurance constraint)

Decision variables:
- x_ij ∈ {0,1}: equals 1 if truck travels from node i ∈ N_0 to node j ∈ N_+, i ≠ j
- y_ijk ∈ {0,1}: equals 1 if drone is launched from node i, visits customer j, and returns to truck at node k; for all (i,j,k) ∈ P
- t_j ≥ 0: time at which truck arrives at node j ∈ N
- t'_j ≥ 0: time at which drone arrives at node j ∈ N
- p_ij ∈ {0,1}: equals 1 if customer i is visited before customer j in the truck's path; for i ∈ C, j ∈ C, i ≠ j
- u_i ∈ [1, c+2]: position of node i ∈ N_+ in the truck's path (subtour elimination variable)

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total delivery time (makespan):

$$\min t_{c+1}$$

where t_{c+1} is the truck's return time to the ending depot (node 6). Due to time synchronization constraints, this is equivalent to minimizing max{t_{c+1}, t'_{c+1}}.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Customer service constraint**: Each customer must be served exactly once (by truck or drone).
$$\sum_{i \in N_0, i \neq j} x_{ij} + \sum_{\substack{i \in N_0, k \in N_+ \\ \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P}} y_{ijk} = 1, \quad \forall j \in C$$

2. **Truck departure from depot**: Truck departs from depot exactly once.
$$\sum_{j \in N_+} x_{0j} = 1$$

3. **Truck return to depot**: Truck returns to ending depot exactly once.
$$\sum_{i \in N_0} x_{i,c+1} = 1$$

4. **Subtour elimination (MTZ)**: Prevents truck subtours.
$$u_i - u_j + 1 \leq (c+2)(1 - x_{ij}), \quad \forall i \in C, j \in N_+ \setminus \{i\}$$

5. **Flow conservation**: Truck visiting node j must also depart from j.
$$\sum_{i \in N_0, i \neq j} x_{ij} = \sum_{k \in N_+, k \neq j} x_{jk}, \quad \forall j \in C$$

6. **Drone launch constraint**: Drone may launch from any node at most once.
$$\sum_{\substack{j \in C', k \in N_+ \\ \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P}} y_{ijk} \leq 1, \quad \forall i \in N_0$$

7. **Drone retrieval constraint**: Drone may rendezvous at any node at most once.
$$\sum_{\substack{i \in N_0, j \in C' \\ \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P}} y_{ijk} \leq 1, \quad \forall k \in N_+$$

8. **Truck must visit launch and retrieval nodes**: If drone launches from customer i and retrieves at customer k, truck must visit both.
$$2 y_{ijk} \leq \sum_{h \in N_0, h \neq i} x_{hi} + \sum_{l \in C, l \neq k} x_{lk}, \quad \forall i \in C, j \in C' \setminus \{i\}, k \in N_+, \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P$$

9. **Drone launch from depot**: If drone launches from depot and retrieves at k, truck must visit k.
$$y_{0jk} \leq \sum_{h \in N_0, h \neq k} x_{hk}, \quad \forall j \in C', k \in N_+, \langle 0,j,k \rangle \in P$$

10. **Ordering constraint**: If drone launches from i and retrieves at k, truck must visit i before k.
$$u_k - u_i \geq 1 - (c+2)\left(1 - \sum_{j \in C'} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall i \in C, k \in N_+ \setminus \{i\}$$

11. **Time synchronization at launch node**: Truck and drone depart together from launch node i.
$$t'_i \geq t_i - M\left(1 - \sum_{\substack{j \in C', k \in N_+ \\ \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P}} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall i \in C$$
$$t'_i \leq t_i + M\left(1 - \sum_{\substack{j \in C', k \in N_+ \\ \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P}} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall i \in C$$

12. **Time synchronization at retrieval node**: Truck and drone arrive together at retrieval node k.
$$t'_k \geq t_k - M\left(1 - \sum_{\substack{i \in N_0, j \in C' \\ \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P}} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall k \in N_+$$
$$t'_k \leq t_k + M\left(1 - \sum_{\substack{i \in N_0, j \in C' \\ \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P}} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall k \in N_+$$

13. **Truck travel time**: Truck arrival time at k from h, incorporating launch/recovery service times.
$$t_k \geq t'_h + s_{hk} + s_L \sum_{\substack{l \in C', m \in N_+ \\ \langle k,l,m \rangle \in P}} y_{klm} + s_R \sum_{\substack{i \in N_0, j \in C' \\ \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P}} y_{ijk} - M(1 - x_{hk}), \quad \forall h \in N_0, k \in N_+, h \neq k$$

14. **Drone travel time from launch to customer**: Drone arrival time at customer j from launch node i.
$$t'_j \geq t'_i + \sigma_{ij} - M\left(1 - \sum_{k \in N_+} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall j \in C', i \in N_0 \setminus \{j\}, \langle i,j,\cdot \rangle \in P$$

15. **Drone travel time from customer to retrieval**: Drone arrival time at retrieval node k from customer j.
$$t'_k \geq t'_j + \sigma_{jk} + s_R - M\left(1 - \sum_{i \in N_0} y_{ijk}\right), \quad \forall j \in C', k \in N_+ \setminus \{j\}, \langle \cdot,j,k \rangle \in P$$

16. **Drone endurance constraint**: Total drone flight time per sortie ≤ e.
$$t'_k - (t'_j - \sigma_{ij}) \leq e + M(1 - y_{ijk}), \quad \forall \langle i,j,k \rangle \in P$$

17. **Consecutive sortie ordering**: If drone launches from i and retrieves at k, and later launches from l (p_il = 1), then t'_l ≥ t'_k.
$$t'_l \geq t'_k - M\left(3 - \sum_{j \in C'} y_{ijk} - \sum_{m \in C'} y_{lmn} - p_{il}\right), \quad \forall i \in N_0, k \in N_+, l \in C, l \neq i, l \neq k$$

18. **Ordering variable constraints**:
$$u_i - u_j \geq 1 - (c+2) p_{ij}, \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j$$
$$u_i - u_j \leq 1 + (c+2)(1 - p_{ij}), \quad \forall i, j \in C, i \neq j$$
$$p_{ij} + p_{ji} = 1, \quad \forall i, j \in C, i < j$$

19. **Initial conditions**:
$$t_0 = t'_0 = 0$$

20. **Variable bounds**:
$$x_{ij} \in \{0,1\}, \quad y_{ijk} \in \{0,1\}, \quad 1 \leq u_i \leq c+2, \quad t_i, t'_i \geq 0, \quad p_{ij} \in \{0,1\}$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Sets:**
- C = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5}: Set of all customers.
- C' = {1, 2, 3, 4}: Set of drone-eligible customers.
- N_0 = {0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5}: Nodes from which a vehicle may depart.
- N_+ = {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}: Nodes to which a vehicle may visit.
- P: Set of valid drone sorties (i, j, k) with σ_ij + σ_jk ≤ e.

**Parameters:**
- s_ij: Truck travel time from i to j (Manhattan distance / truck speed).
- σ_ij: Drone travel time from i to j (Euclidean distance / drone speed).
- e = 0.5 h: Drone flight endurance.
- s_L = 1/60 h: Drone launch time.
- s_R = 1/60 h: Drone recovery time.
- M: Sufficiently large constant.
- c = 5: Number of customers.

**Decision Variables:**
- x_ij ∈ {0,1}: Truck routing variable.
- y_ijk ∈ {0,1}: Drone sortie variable.
- t_j ≥ 0: Truck arrival time at node j.
- t'_j ≥ 0: Drone arrival time at node j.
- p_ij ∈ {0,1}: Ordering variable (1 if i visited before j by truck).
- u_i ∈ [1, c+2]: Position variable for subtour elimination.

**Objective Function:**
$$\min t_{c+1}$$

**Constraints:** (as listed above, constraints 1-20)
