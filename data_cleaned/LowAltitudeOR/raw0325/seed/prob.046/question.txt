A disaster response company operates a collaborative truck-and-drone system to serve rescue tasks after a disaster.
The system must route trucks and drones from a depot to serve all customer nodes with delivery and/or surveillance tasks, and then return to the depot.
Travel times for both trucks and drones are uncertain due to road debris and weather conditions, modeled by cardinality-constrained uncertainty sets.

**Operational Constraints:**
1. **Flow Conservation:** All trucks and drones must depart from the starting depot and return to the ending depot. Flow conservation holds at all intermediate customer nodes.
2. **Capacity:** Trucks have a weight capacity $L^K$. Drones have a weight capacity $L^D$ and a maximum flight duration $T^D$. Each truck can carry at most $L^R$ drones when traveling on a link.
3. **Task Coverage:** Every node must have its rescue task (delivery or surveillance) served by either a truck or a drone. An unserved task incurs a large penalty $B$.
4. **Truck-Drone Collaboration:** Drones are dispatched from trucks at visited nodes and can return to any truck (flexible collaboration). Trucks can continue traveling after dispatching a drone.
5. **Time Synchronization:** A drone can only depart from or land at a truck node when a truck is present at that node.
6. **Travel Time Uncertainty:** Truck travel time on link $(i,j)$ is $\tilde{t}^K_{ij} = t^K_{ij} + \theta^K_{ij}\hat{t}^K_{ij}$ and drone travel time is $\tilde{t}^D_{ij} = t^D_{ij} + \theta^D_{ij}\hat{t}^D_{ij}$, where total deviations are budgeted by $\Gamma^K$ and $\Gamma^D$.

**Parameters:**
- Truck speed: 30 km/h
- Drone speed: 60 km/h
- Truck capacity ($L^K$): 100 kg
- Drone capacity ($L^D$): 10 kg
- Max drones per truck ($L^R$): 1
- Max drone flight duration ($T^D$): 30 minutes
- Penalty for unserved task ($B$): 1000
- Uncertainty budget for trucks ($\Gamma^K$): 1
- Uncertainty budget for drones ($\Gamma^D$): 1
- Max deviation coefficient ($\eta^K = \eta^D$): 0.3 (i.e., $\hat{t}^K_{ij} = 0.3 t^K_{ij}$, $\hat{t}^D_{ij} = 0.3 t^D_{ij}$)

**Locations & Coordinates (km):**

*Depot (starting and ending node):*
- Node 0 (start): (0, 0)
- Node 4 (end):   (9, 0)

*Customer Nodes:*
- Node 1: (3, 0)  [Delivery task, weight demand = 5 kg, priority $p_{1,1}$ = 2]
- Node 2: (6, 0)  [Delivery task, weight demand = 3 kg, priority $p_{2,1}$ = 1]
- Node 3: (4, 3)  [Surveillance task, time demand = 5 min, priority $p_{3,2}$ = 3]  *(truck-unreachable)*

**Network Information:**
- Truck-reachable nodes $V^k$: {0, 1, 2, 4}
- Drone-reachable nodes $V^d$: {0, 1, 2, 3, 4}
- Node 3 can only be served by drones (truck-unreachable due to post-disaster road damage)
- 1 truck ($|K|$ = 1), 1 drone ($|D|$ = 1)

**Task Types:**
- Task type $m=1$: Delivery (weight demand $d^w_{i1} > 0$, time demand $d^t_{i1} = 0$)
- Task type $m=2$: Surveillance (weight demand $d^w_{i2} = 0$, time demand $d^t_{i2} > 0$)
