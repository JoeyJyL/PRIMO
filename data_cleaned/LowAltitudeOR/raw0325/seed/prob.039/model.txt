Identifying the decision variables, objective function, and constraints for the drone delivery routing problem based on the paper "Vehicle Routing Problems for Drone Delivery" by Dorling et al. (2017).

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ x_{ij}^r $: a binary variable indicating whether route $ r $ travels from location $ i $ to location $ j $ (1) or not (0).
- $ u_r $: a binary variable indicating whether route $ r $ is used (1) or not (0).
- $ y_i^r $: a continuous variable representing the payload weight (kg) when leaving location $ i $ on route $ r $.
- $ q_r $: a continuous variable representing the battery weight (kg) for route $ r $.
- $ z_r $: a continuous variable representing the energy consumed (kJ) on route $ r $.
- $ t_i^r $: a continuous variable representing the time (seconds) when location $ i $ is visited on route $ r $.
- $ a_r $: a continuous variable representing the arrival time (seconds) at the depot after completing route $ r $.
- $ n $: an integer variable representing the number of drones to purchase.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_{ij}^r \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i, j \in N $, $ i \neq j $, $ r \in R $.
- $ u_r \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ r \in R $.
- $ y_i^r \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in N $, $ r \in R $.
- $ q_r \geq 0 $ for all $ r \in R $.
- $ z_r \geq 0 $ for all $ r \in R $.
- $ t_i^r \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in N $, $ r \in R $.
- $ a_r \geq 0 $ for all $ r \in R $.
- $ n \in \mathbb{Z}^+ $, $ 1 \leq n \leq M $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which includes the cost of purchasing drones and the cost of energy consumed.

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{r \in R} F \cdot n + \varepsilon \sum_{r \in R} z_r
$$

Where:

- $ F $ is the cost of one drone (dollars).
- $ \varepsilon $ is the energy cost per kJ (dollars/kJ).
- $ n $ is the number of drones purchased.
- $ z_r $ is the energy consumed on route $ r $ (kJ).

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Customer Visit Constraint:**

Each customer must be visited exactly once across all routes.

For each customer $ i \in N_0 $:

$$
\sum_{r \in R} \sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ij}^r = 1
$$

Where:
- $ N_0 $ is the set of customer locations (excluding depot).

2. **Flow Conservation Constraint:**

At each location on each route, the number of incoming edges equals the number of outgoing edges.

For each location $ i \in N $ and route $ r \in R $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ij}^r - \sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ji}^r = 0
$$

3. **Route Start Constraint:**

Each route must start from the depot if it is used.

For each route $ r \in R $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N_0} x_{0j}^r = u_r
$$

4. **Route End Constraint:**

Each route must return to the depot if it is used.

For each route $ r \in R $:

$$
\sum_{i \in N_0} x_{i0}^r = u_r
$$

5. **Payload Weight Tracking Constraint:**

The payload weight when leaving the depot equals the sum of demands on that route.

For each route $ r \in R $:

$$
y_0^r = \sum_{j \in N_0} D_j \cdot x_{0j}^r
$$

Where:
- $ D_j $ is the demand (package weight) at customer $ j $.

6. **Payload Decrease Constraint:**

The payload decreases by the demand amount at each customer visited.

For each route $ r \in R $, and customers $ i, j \in N_0 $, $ i \neq j $:

$$
y_i^r \geq y_j^r + D_j \cdot x_{ij}^r - K(1 - x_{ij}^r)
$$

Where:
- $ K $ is a large constant (Big M).

7. **Capacity Constraint:**

The battery weight plus payload weight cannot exceed the drone's carrying capacity.

For each location $ i \in N $ and route $ r \in R $:

$$
q_r + y_i^r \leq Q \cdot u_r
$$

Where:
- $ Q $ is the maximum carrying capacity of the drone (kg).

8. **Energy Consumption Constraint:**

The energy consumed on each route is calculated using the linear power model.

For each route $ r \in R $:

$$
z_r = \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} \left[\alpha \left(q_r + \frac{y_i^r + y_j^r}{2}\right) + \beta\right] \cdot \left(\frac{d_{ij}}{v} + \tau\right) \cdot x_{ij}^r
$$

Where:
- $ \alpha $ is the power consumption coefficient (kW/kg).
- $ \beta $ is the base power consumption (kW).
- $ d_{ij} $ is the distance between locations $ i $ and $ j $ (meters).
- $ v $ is the flight speed (m/s).
- $ \tau $ is the service time at each location (seconds).

9. **Battery Energy Constraint:**

The battery must provide enough energy to complete the route.

For each route $ r \in R $:

$$
\xi \cdot q_r \geq z_r
$$

Where:
- $ \xi $ is the battery energy density (kJ/kg).

10. **Time Progression Constraint:**

Time increases as the drone travels along the route.

For each route $ r \in R $, locations $ i \in N $ and $ j \in N_0 $, $ i \neq j $:

$$
t_j^r \geq t_i^r + \left(\frac{d_{ij}}{v} + \tau\right) - K(1 - x_{ij}^r)
$$

11. **Arrival Time Constraint:**

The arrival time at the depot is calculated based on the last customer visited.

For each route $ r \in R $ and customer $ i \in N_0 $:

$$
a_r \geq t_i^r + \left(\frac{d_{i0}}{v} + \tau\right) - K(1 - x_{i0}^r)
$$

12. **Time Limit Constraint:**

All routes must be completed within the delivery time limit.

For each route $ r \in R $:

$$
a_r \leq T + K(1 - u_r)
$$

Where:
- $ T $ is the delivery time limit (seconds).

13. **Drone Count Constraint:**

The number of drones must be sufficient to execute all routes.

$$
n \geq \sum_{r \in R} u_r
$$

14. **Drone Availability Constraint:**

The number of drones cannot exceed the maximum available.

$$
n \leq M
$$

Where:
- $ M $ is the maximum number of drones available.

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the drone delivery routing problem is as follows:

**Sets:**

- $ N = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5\} $: Set of all locations (0 = depot, 1-5 = customers).
- $ N_0 = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\} $: Set of customer locations only.
- $ R = \{0, 1, 2, 3, 4\} $: Set of potential routes.

**Parameters:**

- $ d_{ij} $: Distance between location $ i $ and $ j $ (meters).
- $ D_i $: Demand at customer $ i $ (kg).
- $ F = 500 $: Cost of one drone (dollars).
- $ Q = 3.0 $: Maximum carrying capacity (kg).
- $ v = 6 $: Flight speed (m/s).
- $ \tau = 60 $: Service time per location (seconds).
- $ \alpha = 0.217 $: Power consumption coefficient (kW/kg).
- $ \beta = 0.185 $: Base power consumption (kW).
- $ \xi = 650 $: Battery energy density (kJ/kg).
- $ \varepsilon = 0.1 $: Energy cost (dollars/kJ).
- $ T = 600 $: Delivery time limit (seconds).
- $ M = 5 $: Maximum number of drones.
- $ K = 10000 $: Big M constant.

**Decision Variables:**

- $ x_{ij}^r \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i, j \in N $, $ i \neq j $, $ r \in R $.
- $ u_r \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ r \in R $.
- $ y_i^r \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in N $, $ r \in R $.
- $ q_r \geq 0 $ for all $ r \in R $.
- $ z_r \geq 0 $ for all $ r \in R $.
- $ t_i^r \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in N $, $ r \in R $.
- $ a_r \geq 0 $ for all $ r \in R $.
- $ n \in \mathbb{Z}^+ $, $ 1 \leq n \leq M $.

**Objective Function:**

Minimize:

$$
F \cdot n + \varepsilon \sum_{r \in R} z_r
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Customer Visit Constraint:**

For each $ i \in N_0 $:

$$
\sum_{r \in R} \sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ij}^r = 1
$$

2. **Flow Conservation Constraint:**

For each $ i \in N $, $ r \in R $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ij}^r - \sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} x_{ji}^r = 0
$$

3. **Route Start Constraint:**

For each $ r \in R $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N_0} x_{0j}^r = u_r
$$

4. **Route End Constraint:**

For each $ r \in R $:

$$
\sum_{i \in N_0} x_{i0}^r = u_r
$$

5. **Payload Weight Tracking Constraint:**

For each $ r \in R $:

$$
y_0^r = \sum_{j \in N_0} D_j \cdot x_{0j}^r
$$

6. **Payload Decrease Constraint:**

For each $ r \in R $, $ i, j \in N_0 $, $ i \neq j $:

$$
y_i^r \geq y_j^r + D_j \cdot x_{ij}^r - K(1 - x_{ij}^r)
$$

7. **Capacity Constraint:**

For each $ i \in N $, $ r \in R $:

$$
q_r + y_i^r \leq Q \cdot u_r
$$

8. **Energy Consumption Constraint:**

For each $ r \in R $:

$$
z_r = \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in N, j \neq i} \left[\alpha \left(q_r + \frac{y_i^r + y_j^r}{2}\right) + \beta\right] \cdot \left(\frac{d_{ij}}{v} + \tau\right) \cdot x_{ij}^r
$$

9. **Battery Energy Constraint:**

For each $ r \in R $:

$$
\xi \cdot q_r \geq z_r
$$

10. **Time Progression Constraint:**

For each $ r \in R $, $ i \in N $, $ j \in N_0 $, $ i \neq j $:

$$
t_j^r \geq t_i^r + \left(\frac{d_{ij}}{v} + \tau\right) - K(1 - x_{ij}^r)
$$

11. **Arrival Time Constraint:**

For each $ r \in R $, $ i \in N_0 $:

$$
a_r \geq t_i^r + \left(\frac{d_{i0}}{v} + \tau\right) - K(1 - x_{i0}^r)
$$

12. **Time Limit Constraint:**

For each $ r \in R $:

$$
a_r \leq T + K(1 - u_r)
$$

13. **Drone Count Constraint:**

$$
n \geq \sum_{r \in R} u_r

$$
n \leq M
