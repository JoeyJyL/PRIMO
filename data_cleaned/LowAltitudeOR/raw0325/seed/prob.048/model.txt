Identifying the decision variables, objective functions, and constraints for the multi-objective vertiport location optimization problem based on the middle-mile package delivery framework.

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ x_{kt} $: a binary variable indicating whether a vertiport of type $ t $ is placed in zone $ k $ (1) or not (0).
- $ y_c $: a binary variable indicating whether customer zone $ c $ is covered (1) or not (0).
- $ r_{wck} $: a continuous variable representing the fraction of demand from warehouse $ w $ to customer $ c $ routed through vertiport in zone $ k $.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_{kt} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in K, t \in T $.
- $ y_c \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ c \in C $.
- $ 0 \le r_{wck} \le 1 $ for all $ w \in W, c \in C, k \in K $.

### Step 2: Objective Functions Construction

The problem has three objectives that need to be optimized simultaneously.

**Objective 1: Minimize Unserved Demand**

$$
\text{Minimize } f_1 = \sum_{c \in C} w_c (1 - y_c)
$$

Where:
- $ C $ is the set of customer zones.
- $ w_c $ is the demand weight (packages) of customer zone $ c $.
- $ y_c $ indicates if customer $ c $ is covered.

**Objective 2: Minimize Total Safety Risk**

$$
\text{Minimize } f_2 = \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{t \in T} S_k \cdot x_{kt}
$$

Where:
- $ K $ is the set of candidate vertiport zones.
- $ T $ is the set of vertiport types.
- $ S_k $ is the safety score of zone $ k $ (higher = worse).

**Objective 3: Minimize Total Noise Nuisance**

$$
\text{Minimize } f_3 = \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{t \in T} N_k \cdot x_{kt}
$$

Where:
- $ N_k $ is the noise score of zone $ k $ (higher = worse).

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Vertiport Budget Constraint:**

Exactly $ p $ vertiports must be selected:

$$
\sum_{k \in K} \sum_{t \in T} x_{kt} = p
$$

Where:
- $ p = 3 $ is the number of vertiports to locate.

2. **Single Type per Zone:**

At most one vertiport type can be placed in each zone:

$$
\sum_{t \in T} x_{kt} \le 1, \quad \forall k \in K
$$

3. **Coverage Definition:**

A customer $ c $ is covered only if at least one vertiport within range serves it:

$$
y_c \le \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{t \in T} x_{kt} \cdot v_{wck}, \quad \forall c \in C, w \in W
$$

Where:
- $ v_{wck} = 1 $ if $ dist(w, k) \le f_p $ and $ dist(k, c) \le f_d $, otherwise $ v_{wck} = 0 $.
- $ f_p = 30 $ km is the maximum drone range.
- $ f_d = 10 $ km is the maximum last-mile distance.

4. **Demand Allocation:**

Demand can only be routed through selected vertiports within range:

$$
r_{wck} \le \sum_{t \in T} x_{kt} \cdot v_{wck}, \quad \forall w \in W, c \in C, k \in K
$$

5. **Coverage Requirement:**

Customer is covered if the total allocated fraction equals 1:

$$
\sum_{k \in K} r_{wck} = y_c, \quad \forall w \in W, c \in C
$$

6. **Capacity Constraint:**

Total demand routed through a vertiport cannot exceed its capacity:

$$
\sum_{w \in W} \sum_{c \in C} w_c \cdot r_{wck} \le \sum_{t \in T} \Gamma_t \cdot x_{kt}, \quad \forall k \in K
$$

Where:
- $ \Gamma_t $ is the capacity (packages/day) for vertiport type $ t $.

7. **Minimum Safety Distance:**

Selected vertiports must be at least $ d_{min} $ apart:

$$
x_{kt} + x_{k't'} \le 1, \quad \forall k, k' \in K: k \ne k', dist(k, k') < d_{min}, \forall t, t' \in T
$$

Where:
- $ d_{min} = 2 $ km is the minimum safety distance.

8. **Maximum Safety Distance:**

Each selected vertiport must have at least one other vertiport within $ d_{max} $:

$$
\sum_{k' \in K: k' \ne k, dist(k,k') \le d_{max}} \sum_{t' \in T} x_{k't'} \ge \sum_{t \in T} x_{kt}, \quad \forall k \in K
$$

Where:
- $ d_{max} = 10 $ km is the maximum safety distance.

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the multi-objective vertiport location problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- $ x_{kt} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in K, t \in T $.
- $ y_c \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ c \in C $.
- $ 0 \le r_{wck} \le 1 $ for all $ w \in W, c \in C, k \in K $.

**Objective Functions:**

f_1 = \sum_{c \in C} w_c (1 - y_c)

**Constraints:**

\sum_{k \in K} \sum_{t \in T} x_{kt} = p

\sum_{t \in T} x_{kt} \le 1, \quad \forall k \in K
$$

3. **Coverage Definition:**

$$
y_c \le \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{t \in T} x_{kt} \cdot v_{wck}, \quad \forall c \in C, w \in W
$$

4. **Demand Allocation:**

$$
r_{wck} \le \sum_{t \in T} x_{kt} \cdot v_{wck}, \quad \forall w \in W, c \in C, k \in K
$$

\sum_{k \in K} r_{wck} = y_c, \quad \forall w \in W, c \in C
