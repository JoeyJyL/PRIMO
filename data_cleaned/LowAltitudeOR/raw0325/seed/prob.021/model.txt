### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let’s define the decision variables for the problem.

Let’s denote:

- $ y_i $: a binary variable indicating if node $ i $ is used/opened.
- $ x_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating if arc $ (i,j) $ is traversed.

Thus, we have:

- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in N $.
- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ (i,j) \in A $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total cost, which consists of the fixed costs for opening nodes and the travel costs associated with traversing arcs.

The objective function is:

$$
\min Z = \sum_{i \in N} F_i y_i + \sum_{(i,j) \in A} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ F_i $ is the fixed cost of opening node $ i $ (0 for Origin $ O $ and Destination $ D $).
- $ c_{ij} $ is the travel cost of arc $ (i, j) $ (calculated as distance $ \times $ rate).
- $ N $ is the set of nodes and $ A $ is the set of feasible arcs.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let’s identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Flow Balance Constraint (Shortest Path):**

Standard flow conservation must be maintained to ensure a path from the Origin $ O $ to the Destination $ D $.

For each node $ i \in N $:

$$
\sum_{j} x_{ij} - \sum_{j} x_{ji} = 
\begin{cases} 
1 & \text{if } i = O \\
-1 & \text{if } i = D \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

2. **Node Activation Constraint:**

A node $ i $ must be opened ($ y_i = 1 $) if there is any flow entering or leaving it.

For each node $ i \in N $:

$$
\sum_{j} x_{ij} \le y_i
$$

$$
\sum_{j} x_{ji} \le y_i
$$

3. **Binary Constraints:**

The decision variables must be binary.

For all $ i \in N $ and $ (i,j) \in A $:

$$
y_i \in \{0, 1\}, \quad x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in N $.
- $ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ (i,j) \in A $.

Minimize:

$$
\min Z = \sum_{i \in N} F_i y_i + \sum_{(i,j) \in A} c_{ij} x_{ij}
