### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ z_k $: a binary variable indicating if vertiport zone $ k $ is opened.
- $ R_k^{ij} $: a continuous variable representing the fraction of demand from warehouse $ i $ to destination $ j $ routed through vertiport $ k $.

Thus, we have:

- $ z_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in K $.
- $ R_k^{ij} \in [0, 1] $ for all $ k \in K $, $ i \in I $, $ j \in J $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the total package demand served across all OD pairs and vertiports.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Maximize } \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} W_{ij} R_k^{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ W_{ij} $ is the demand (packages/day) from warehouse $ i $ to destination $ j $.
- $ K $ is the set of candidate vertiport zones, $ I $ is the set of warehouses, and $ J $ is the set of destinations.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Vertiport Count Constraint:**

Exactly $ p $ vertiports must be selected.

$$
\sum_{k \in K} z_k = p
$$

Where:

- $ p = 3 $ is the number of vertiports to open.

2. **Coverage Feasibility (AND condition):**

Demand from OD pair $ (i, j) $ can be routed through vertiport $ k $ only if $ k $ is open and can cover the pair (drone range $ \le 30 $ km AND last-mile $ \le 10 $ km).

For each $ k \in K $, $ i \in I $, $ j \in J $:

$$
R_k^{ij} \le v_k^{ij} \cdot z_k
$$

Where:

- $ v_k^{ij} = 1 $ iff $ \text{dist}(i, k) \le 30 $ and $ \text{dist}(k, j) \le 10 $.

3. **Capacity Constraint:**

Total demand routed through vertiport $ k $ cannot exceed its capacity.

For each $ k \in K $:

$$
\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} W_{ij} R_k^{ij} \le \Gamma \cdot z_k
$$

Where:

- $ \Gamma = 288 $ packages/day (small vertiport capacity).

4. **Demand Fraction Constraint:**

Each OD pair's demand can be served at most once (total fraction across all vertiports $ \le 1 $).

For each $ i \in I $, $ j \in J $:

$$
\sum_{k \in K} R_k^{ij} \le 1
$$

5. **Safety Distance Constraint:**

Any two selected vertiports must be at least $ d_{\min} $ apart.

For each pair $ k_1, k_2 \in K $ where $ \text{dist}(k_1, k_2) < d_{\min} $:

$$
z_{k_1} + z_{k_2} \le 1
$$

Where:

- $ d_{\min} = 2.44 $ km.

6. **Variable Bounds:**

$$
z_k \in \{0, 1\}, \quad R_k^{ij} \in [0, 1]
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- $ z_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in K $.
- $ R_k^{ij} \in [0, 1] $ for all $ k \in K $, $ i \in I $, $ j \in J $.

**Objective Function:**

Maximize:

$$
\text{Maximize } \sum_{k \in K} \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} W_{ij} R_k^{ij}
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Vertiport Count:**

$$
\sum_{k \in K} z_k = p
$$

2. **Coverage Feasibility:**

For each $ k \in K $, $ i \in I $, $ j \in J $:

$$
R_k^{ij} \le v_k^{ij} \cdot z_k
$$

3. **Capacity:**

For each $ k \in K $:

$$
\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} W_{ij} R_k^{ij} \le \Gamma \cdot z_k
$$

4. **Demand Fraction:**

For each $ i \in I $, $ j \in J $:

$$
\sum_{k \in K} R_k^{ij} \le 1
$$

5. **Safety Distance:**

For each $ k_1, k_2 $ with $ \text{dist}(k_1, k_2) < d_{\min} $:

$$
z_{k_1} + z_{k_2} \le 1
$$

6. **Variable Bounds:**

$$
z_k \in \{0, 1\}, \quad R_k^{ij} \in [0, 1]
$$

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Program)
2. Problem Scale: Medium (8 candidates, 3 warehouses, 10 destinations, 240 continuous + 8 binary variables)
3. Solution Method: Exact MILP solver (Gurobi)
