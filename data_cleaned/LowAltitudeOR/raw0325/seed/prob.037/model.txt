### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ a_{ij} $: a continuous variable representing the drone delivery offer rate (number of orders accepted) for product category $ j $ in service zone $ i $.
- $ u_{ij} $: a continuous variable representing the unfulfilled demand (lost orders) for category $ j $ in zone $ i $.
- $ W $: a continuous variable representing the total workload in excess of the drone fleet capacity (triggers delay costs).

Thus, we have:

- $ a_{ij} \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in N, j \in M $.
- $ u_{ij} \geq 0 $ for all $ i \in N, j \in M $.
- $ W \geq 0 $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize total net profit over the planning period. The net profit consists of revenue from accepted orders minus opportunity costs for lost demand, delivery delay costs for excess workload, variable costs per delivery, and fixed fleet costs.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in M} (w_{j} - c_v) \cdot a_{ij} - \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in M} c_{j} \cdot u_{ij} - c_w \cdot W - K \cdot c_F
$$

Where:

- $ w_{j} $ is the unit profit for product category $ j $.
- $ c_{j} $ is the per order opportunity cost for lost demand of category $ j $.
- $ c_v = 1.20 $ is the variable operating cost per delivery.
- $ c_w = 100.00 $ is the delivery delay cost per excess workload unit.
- $ K = 8 $ is the number of drones, $ c_F = 0.50 $ is the fixed cost per drone per period.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

1. **Offer Rate Bounded by Demand:**

The drone delivery offer rate for each product-zone pair cannot exceed the demand:

For each $ i \in N, j \in M $:

$$
a_{ij} \leq D_{ij}
$$

2. **Unfulfilled Demand Definition:**

Lost demand equals the gap between total demand and accepted orders:

For each $ i \in N, j \in M $:

$$
u_{ij} \geq D_{ij} - a_{ij}
$$

3. **Excess Workload (Delay) Definition:**

Total normalized workload from accepted orders may exceed the fleet capacity of 1.0. Excess generates delay costs:

$$
W \geq \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in M} \frac{a_{ij}}{K \mu_i} - 1
$$

where $ \mu_i = \frac{v}{2 s_i} $ is the per-drone service rate for zone $ i $, $ v $ is drone speed, and $ s_i $ is distance for zone $ i $.

4. **Non-negativity:**

$$
a_{ij} \geq 0, \quad u_{ij} \geq 0, \quad W \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in N, j \in M
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Sets:**

- $ N = \{Z1, Z2, Z3\} $: set of service zones.
- $ M = \{P1, P2, P3\} $: set of product categories.

**Parameters:**

- $ D_{ij} $: demand for product $ j $ in zone $ i $ (units/period).
- $ w_{j} $: unit profit for product category $ j $ (\$/unit). $ w_{P1} = 2.00, w_{P2} = 4.50, w_{P3} = 8.00 $.
- $ c_{j} $: per order opportunity cost for category $ j $. $ c_{P1} = 0.60, c_{P2} = 1.50, c_{P3} = 3.00 $.
- $ s_i $: distance from warehouse to zone $ i $ (miles). $ s_{Z1} = 4, s_{Z2} = 7, s_{Z3} = 11 $.
- $ v = 40 $ mph: drone speed.
- $ \mu_i = v / (2 s_i) $: per-drone service rate for zone $ i $ (trips/hr).
- $ K = 8 $: number of drones.
- $ c_w = 100.00 $: delivery delay cost per excess workload unit (\$/unit).
- $ c_v = 1.20 $: variable cost per delivery (\$/order).
- $ c_F = 0.50 $: fixed cost per drone per period (\$/drone/hr).

**Decision Variables:**

- $ a_{ij} \geq 0 $: drone delivery offer rate for category $ j $ in zone $ i $.
- $ u_{ij} \geq 0 $: unfulfilled demand for category $ j $ in zone $ i $.
- $ W \geq 0 $: excess workload beyond capacity.

**Objective Function:**

Maximize:

$$
Z = \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in M} (w_{j} - c_v) \cdot a_{ij} - \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in M} c_{j} \cdot u_{ij} - c_w \cdot W - K \cdot c_F
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Offer rate bounded by demand:**
$$
a_{ij} \leq D_{ij} \quad \forall i \in N, j \in M
$$

2. **Unfulfilled demand definition:**
$$
u_{ij} \geq D_{ij} - a_{ij} \quad \forall i \in N, j \in M
$$

3. **Excess workload (delay):**
$$
W \geq \sum_{i \in N} \sum_{j \in M} \frac{a_{ij}}{K \mu_i} - 1
$$

4. **Non-negativity:**
$$
a_{ij}, u_{ij}, W \geq 0
$$

1. Optimization Problem Classification: LP
2. Problem Scale: Small
