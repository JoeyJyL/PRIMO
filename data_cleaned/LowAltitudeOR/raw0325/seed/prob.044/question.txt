# Flying Sidekick Traveling Salesman Problem with Stochastic Travel Time (FSTSP-STT) - Simplified Scenario

## Background
A delivery company operates a coordinated truck-drone delivery system. The truck follows a traveling salesman route while the drone launches from the truck to deliver packages to nearby customers. Unlike the deterministic FSTSP, the truck's travel time is stochastic due to traffic congestion, modeled as a Markov process with three traffic states: light (0), normal (1), and heavy (2).

The problem is formulated as a Markov Decision Process (MDP) and solved using reinforcement learning. However, for this simplified scenario, we solve the **deterministic version** (FSTSP) using a Mixed Integer Program (MIP) to find the optimal routing and scheduling.

## Problem Description
The company must determine:
1. The truck's delivery route (TSP-style)
2. Which customers are served by the drone (UAV sorties)
3. The launch and retrieval nodes for each drone sortie

**Objective**: Minimize the total delivery time (makespan) — the time at which both the truck and drone return to the depot.

## Simplified Scenario

We have **1 depot** (node 0 = start, node 5 = end depot) and **4 customers** (nodes 1, 2, 3, 4).

- All customers are drone-eligible (C' = {1, 2, 3, 4}).
- The drone can serve **one customer per sortie**.
- The drone must return to the truck (not the depot) after each sortie.

## Parameters

### Node Coordinates (km)
| Node | X    | Y    | Description     |
|------|------|------|-----------------|
| 0    | 0.0  | 0.0  | Starting depot  |
| 1    | 3.0  | 4.0  | Customer 1      |
| 2    | 6.0  | 1.0  | Customer 2      |
| 3    | 8.0  | 5.0  | Customer 3      |
| 4    | 2.0  | 7.0  | Customer 4      |
| 5    | 0.0  | 0.0  | Ending depot    |

### Travel Times
- **Truck speed**: 25 mph (≈ 40.23 km/h), travels on Manhattan metric roads.
- **Drone speed**: 50 mph (≈ 80.47 km/h), flies Euclidean (straight-line) paths.
- Truck travel time s_ij = Manhattan_distance(i,j) / truck_speed (hours)
- Drone travel time σ_ij = Euclidean_distance(i,j) / drone_speed (hours)

### Drone Parameters
- **Flight endurance**: e = 0.5 hours (30 minutes)
- **Launch time**: s_L = 1/60 hours (1 minute)
- **Recovery time**: s_R = 1/60 hours (1 minute)

### Stochastic Travel Time (for context)
The truck travel time changes stochastically with traffic conditions:
- Light traffic (λ=0): truck travel time = 0.5 × normal
- Normal traffic (λ=1): truck travel time = 1.0 × normal
- Heavy traffic (λ=2): truck travel time = 2.0 × normal

**For this MIP formulation, we use normal traffic (λ=1) as the deterministic baseline.**

## Decision
Find the optimal truck route and drone sortie assignments to minimize total delivery time (makespan), subject to:
- Each customer served exactly once (by truck or drone)
- Drone endurance constraint: each sortie ≤ e hours
- Time synchronization: truck and drone must meet at retrieval nodes
- Drone can only launch/retrieve at customer nodes visited by the truck
- Drone cannot return to the same node it launched from
