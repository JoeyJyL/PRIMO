Identifying the decision variables, objective function, and constraints for the UAV Traffic Management Full Optimization (FO) model based on the paper "Traffic management and resource allocation for UAV-based parcel delivery in low-altitude urban space" by Li, Hansen, and Zou (2022).

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ I_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating whether flight $ i \in \mathcal{F} $ takes path $ j \in \mathcal{P} $ (1) or not (0).
- $ t_i $: a continuous variable representing the assigned departure time (seconds) of flight $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ D_i $: a continuous variable representing the delay cost ($) of flight $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ z_c $: a binary variable equal to 1 if both flights in conflict $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $ take their respective conflicting paths (i.e., $ I_{ij} = 1 $ and $ I_{mn} = 1 $).
- $ X_c $: a binary variable equal to 1 if flight $ i $ exits the conflict region before flight $ m $ enters (flight $ i $ passes first) for conflict $ c \in \mathcal{C} $.
- $ x_c $: a binary variable equal to 1 if flight $ m $ exits the conflict region before flight $ i $ enters (flight $ m $ passes first) for conflict $ c \in \mathcal{C} $.

Thus, we have:

- $ I_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $, $ j \in \mathcal{P} $.
- $ t_i \geq 0 $ and continuous for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ D_i \geq 0 $ and continuous for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ z_c, X_c, x_c \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ c \in \mathcal{C} $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total system cost, which includes the delay cost of all UAV missions and the path cost of all UAV missions.

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} D_i + \sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} c_{ij} \cdot I_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ D_i $ is the delay cost of flight $ i $.
- $ c_{ij} $ is the path cost of flight $ i $ taking path $ j $.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Piecewise Linear Delay Cost Constraint (linearized):**

The delay cost function is piecewise linear with a threshold $ d $. The linearization replaces the original piecewise definition with two lower-bound constraints:

For each flight $ i \in \mathcal{F} $:

$$
D_i \geq r_i (t_i - s_i)
$$

$$
D_i \geq r_i \cdot d + R_i (t_i - s_i - d)
$$

Where:
- $ r_i $ is the unit delay cost ($/s) when delay $ \leq d $.
- $ R_i $ is the unit delay cost ($/s) when delay $ > d $; $ R_i = 2r_i $.
- $ s_i $ is the scheduled departure time of flight $ i $.
- $ d $ is the delay cost threshold (seconds).

2. **Conflict Resolution — Flight $ i $ passes before flight $ m $:**

If both flights take their conflicting paths ($ z_c = 1 $) and flight $ i $ is assigned to pass first ($ X_c = 1 $), then flight $ i $ must exit the conflict region at least $ t_0 $ seconds before flight $ m $ enters:

For each $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
t_i + e_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - X_c) + B_c + t_m
$$

Where:
- $ e_c $ is the travel time from origin to exit point of conflict region for flight $ i $.
- $ B_c $ is the travel time from origin to entry point of conflict region for flight $ m $.
- $ t_0 $ is the safety separation buffer (10 seconds).
- $ M $ is a sufficiently large constant (Big-M).

3. **Conflict Resolution — Flight $ m $ passes before flight $ i $:**

For each $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
t_m + E_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - x_c) + b_c + t_i
$$

Where:
- $ E_c $ is the travel time from origin to exit point of conflict region for flight $ m $.
- $ b_c $ is the travel time from origin to entry point of conflict region for flight $ i $.

4. **Linearization of $ z_c = I_{ij} \cdot I_{mn} $ (upper bounds):**

For each $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
z_c \leq I_{ij}
$$

$$
z_c \leq I_{mn}
$$

$$
z_c + 1 \geq I_{ij} + I_{mn}
$$

5. **Conflict Ordering Constraint:**

If both flights take conflicting paths, exactly one must pass first:

For each $ c \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
X_c + x_c = z_c
$$

6. **Departure Time Feasibility Constraint:**

The assigned departure time must not be earlier than the scheduled departure time:

For each $ i \in \mathcal{F} $:

$$
t_i \geq s_i
$$

7. **Path Selection Constraint:**

Each flight must choose exactly one path:

For each $ i \in \mathcal{F} $:

$$
\sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} I_{ij} = 1
$$

8. **Non-negativity and Binary Constraints:**

$$
D_i \geq 0, \quad t_i \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{F}
$$

$$
I_{ij}, z_c, X_c, x_c \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the UAV Traffic Management Full Optimization (FO) problem is as follows:

**Sets:**

- $ \mathcal{F} = \{1, 2, 3, 4\} $: Set of UAV flights.
- $ \mathcal{P} = \{0, 1\} $: Set of alternative paths (0 = optimal, 1 = alternative).
- $ \mathcal{C} = \{C1, C2, C3, C4\} $: Set of spatial conflict flight-path pairs, where each element $ c = (i, j, m, n) $ denotes that flight $ i $ on path $ j $ spatially conflicts with flight $ m $ on path $ n $.

**Parameters:**

- $ s_i $: Scheduled departure time of flight $ i $ (seconds): $ s_1=0, s_2=10, s_3=5, s_4=20 $.
- $ c_{ij} $: Path cost of flight $ i $ taking path $ j $ ($): $ c_{i,0}=0 $ for all $ i $; $ c_{1,1}=0.5, c_{2,1}=0.8, c_{3,1}=0.3, c_{4,1}=0.6 $.
- $ r_i $: Unit delay cost ($/s) when delay $ \leq d $: $ r_1=0.004, r_2=0.002, r_3=0.005, r_4=0.003 $.
- $ R_i = 2r_i $: Unit delay cost ($/s) when delay $ > d $.
- $ d = 300 $: Delay cost threshold (seconds).
- $ t_0 = 10 $: Safety separation buffer (seconds).
- $ M = 10000 $: Big-M constant.
- $ b_c, e_c, B_c, E_c $: Travel times to entry/exit points of conflict regions (seconds).

**Decision Variables:**

- $ I_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $, $ j \in \mathcal{P} $.
- $ t_i \geq 0 $ and continuous for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ D_i \geq 0 $ and continuous for all $ i \in \mathcal{F} $.
- $ z_c, X_c, x_c \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ c \in \mathcal{C} $.

**Objective Function:**

Minimize:

$$
\sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} D_i + \sum_{i \in \mathcal{F}} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} c_{ij} \cdot I_{ij}
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Piecewise Linear Delay Cost (linearized):**

For each $ i \in \mathcal{F} $:

$$
D_i \geq r_i (t_i - s_i)
$$

$$
D_i \geq r_i \cdot d + R_i (t_i - s_i - d)
$$

2. **Conflict Resolution — Flight $ i $ first:**

For each $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
t_i + e_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - X_c) + B_c + t_m
$$

3. **Conflict Resolution — Flight $ m $ first:**

For each $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
t_m + E_c + t_0 \leq M(1 - x_c) + b_c + t_i
$$

4. **Linearization of $ z_c $:**

For each $ c = (i,j,m,n) \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
z_c \leq I_{ij}
$$

$$
z_c \leq I_{mn}
$$

$$
z_c + 1 \geq I_{ij} + I_{mn}
$$

5. **Conflict Ordering:**

For each $ c \in \mathcal{C} $:

$$
X_c + x_c = z_c
$$

6. **Departure Time Feasibility:**

For each $ i \in \mathcal{F} $:

$$
t_i \geq s_i
$$

7. **Path Selection:**

For each $ i \in \mathcal{F} $:

$$
\sum_{j \in \mathcal{P}} I_{ij} = 1
$$

8. **Non-negativity and Binary Constraints:**

$$
D_i \geq 0, \quad t_i \geq 0 \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{F}
$$

$$
I_{ij}, z_c, X_c, x_c \in \{0, 1\}
$$
