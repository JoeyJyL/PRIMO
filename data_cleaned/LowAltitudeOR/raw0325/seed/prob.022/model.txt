### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the sets and decision variables for the problem.

**Sets:**
- $S$: Set of hubs (departure points), indexed by $s$
- $I$: Set of candidate charging station locations, indexed by $i, j$
- $N$: Set of delivery points (customers), indexed by $n$
- $E$: Set of feasible edges where $E = \{(s,i) | s \in S, i \in I, d(s,i) \leq 2R\} \cup \{(i,j) | i,j \in I, i \neq j, d(i,j) \leq 2R\}$

**Parameters:**
- $R$: One-way coverage radius of a drone
- $2R$: Maximum distance a drone can travel on a full charge
- $l_e$: Length of edge $e \in E$
- $c_{in}$: Binary coverage matrix, $c_{in} = 1$ if delivery point $n$ is within distance $R$ from station $i$
- $\theta$: Weight parameter for bi-objective function, $\theta \in [0,1]$
- $\beta_1, \beta_2$: Normalizing constants for objective function

**Decision Variables:**
- $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if candidate location $i$ is activated as a station; 0 otherwise.
- $y_{esi} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if edge $e$ is used in the path between hub $s$ and station $i$; 0 otherwise.
- $u_{si} \in \{0, 1\}$: Binary variable equal to 1 if location $i$ is a terminal station connected to hub $s$; 0 otherwise.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize a weighted combination of total path lengths and the number of active stations.

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \frac{\theta}{\beta_1} \cdot \sum_{s \in S} \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{e \in E} l_e \cdot y_{esi} \cdot u_{si} + \frac{(1-\theta)}{\beta_2} \cdot \sum_{i \in I} x_i
$$

Where:
- First term: Sum of lengths of paths connecting hubs to terminal stations
- Second term: Number of active charging stations
- $\theta = 0$: Minimize number of stations only
- $\theta = 1$: Minimize total path lengths only
- $\theta \in (0,1)$: Balanced optimization

**Linearization:** Since the first term involves multiplication of binary variables $y_{esi} \cdot u_{si}$, we introduce auxiliary variable $z_{esi}$ and constraints:
$$z_{esi} \leq y_{esi}, \quad z_{esi} \leq u_{si}, \quad z_{esi} \geq y_{esi} + u_{si} - 1$$

The linearized objective becomes:
$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \frac{\theta}{\beta_1} \cdot \sum_{s \in S} \sum_{i \in I} \sum_{e \in E} l_e \cdot z_{esi} + \frac{(1-\theta)}{\beta_2} \cdot \sum_{i \in I} x_i
$$

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

**1. Flow Balance Constraints:**

Terminal nodes have only entering edges; intermediate nodes have balanced in/out flow:

$$
\sum_{e \in E: dest(e)=k} y_{esi} - \sum_{e \in E: source(e)=k} y_{esi} = \begin{cases} u_{si} & \text{if } i = k \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases} \quad \forall s \in S, \forall i,k \in I
$$

**2. Single Outgoing Edge from Hub:**

At most one edge originating from hub $s$ for each path to destination $i$:

$$
\sum_{e \in E: source(e)=s} y_{esi} \leq 1 \quad \forall s \in S, i \in I
$$

**3. Edge Activation Constraint:**

Edges can only be activated on paths to terminal stations:

$$
y_{esi} \leq u_{si} \quad \forall e \in E, s \in S, i \in I
$$

**4. Single Hub Assignment:**

Each terminal station is connected to at most one hub:

$$
\sum_{s \in S} u_{si} \leq 1 \quad \forall i \in I
$$

**5. Delivery Point Coverage:**

Every delivery point must be covered by at least one active terminal station:

$$
\sum_{s \in S} \sum_{i \in I} c_{in} \cdot u_{si} \geq 1 \quad \forall n \in N
$$

**6. Station Activation Linkage:**

A station is active if any path passes through it:

$$
\sum_{s \in S} \sum_{e \in E} y_{esi} \leq x_i \cdot M \quad \forall i \in I
$$

Where $M$ is a large constant (e.g., $|I|$).

**7. Variable Domains:**

$$
x_i \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in I
$$

$$
y_{esi} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall s \in S, i \in I, e \in E
$$

$$
u_{si} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall s \in S, i \in I
$$

$$
z_{esi} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall s \in S, i \in I, e \in E
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**
- $x_i \in \{0, 1\}$: Station activation
- $y_{esi} \in \{0, 1\}$: Edge usage in path from $s$ to $i$
- $u_{si} \in \{0, 1\}$: Terminal station designation
- $z_{esi} \in \{0, 1\}$: Linearization auxiliary variable

**Objective Function:**

Minimize weighted combination of total path lengths and number of active stations:
$$
Z = \frac{\theta}{\beta_1} \cdot \sum_{s,i,e} l_e \cdot z_{esi} + \frac{(1-\theta)}{\beta_2} \cdot \sum_{i \in I} x_i
$$

**Constraints:**
1. Flow balance at each node (path connectivity)
2. Single outgoing edge from hub per path
3. Edge activation only for terminal station paths
4. Single hub assignment per terminal station
5. Complete delivery point coverage
6. Station activation linkage
7. Binary variable domains

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP
2. Problem Scale: Small
