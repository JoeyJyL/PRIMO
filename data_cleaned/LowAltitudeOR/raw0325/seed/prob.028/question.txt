
"SkyMetro" is designing a regional UAM network with a focus on infrastructure cost management.
**Network Topology:**
- **V1 (City Hub):** High land cost ($100/space). Central business district.
- **V2 (North Suburb):** Low land cost ($20/space). Residential.
- **V3 (South Suburb):** Low land cost ($20/space). Residential.

**Demand Scenarios (Trips/hr):**
- **T1 (AM Peak):** Massive inflow to City.
  - V2 $\to$ V1: 30
  - V3 $\to$ V1: 20
- **T2 (Mid-Day):** Balanced light traffic.
  - V1 $\leftrightarrow$ V2: 10 each way.
  - V1 $\leftrightarrow$ V3: 10 each way.
- **T3 (PM Peak):** Massive outflow from City.
  - V1 $\to$ V2: 30
  - V1 $\to$ V3: 20

**Operational Parameters:**
- **Flight Time:** 0.5 hrs per trip (uniform).
- **Charging Time:** 0.3 hrs per trip (Fast charging).
- **Minimum Idle:** 1 drone per vertiport at all times.
- **Costs:**
  - Fleet CapEx: $500 per drone.
  - Relocation OpEx: $5 per empty flight.
  - Vertiport Space CapEx: Varies by location (see above).

**Planning Problem:**
Determine the optimal **Fleet Size** and **Number of Parking Spaces** at each vertiport to handle the peak loads across all time windows, while minimizing Total Cost (Investment + Operations).
- *Note:* Since V1 is expensive, the solver might try to minimize V1's size, but the physics of "Steady State" flow (Arrivals = Departures) imposes strict constraints.
