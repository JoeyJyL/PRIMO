### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let's define the decision variables for the problem.

Let's denote:

- $ \psi_m(t) $: discrete direction chosen by aircraft $ m $ at time $ t $, where $ \psi_m(t) \in \{0, \frac{\pi}{4}, \frac{\pi}{2}, \ldots, \frac{7\pi}{4}\} $ (8 compass directions).
- $ d_{m,t,k} $: a binary variable, equals 1 if aircraft $ m $ at time $ t $ chooses direction $ k \in \{0,\ldots,7\} $.
- $ \beta_{m,n}(t) $: fraction of bandwidth $ B_{\max} $ allocated by BS $ n $ to aircraft $ m $ at time $ t $, drawn from $ \{0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8\} $.
- $ b_{m,t,l} $: a binary variable, equals 1 if aircraft $ m $ at time $ t $ uses bandwidth level $ l $.
- $ s_{m,t,n} $: a binary variable, equals 1 if aircraft $ m $ at time $ t $ is served by BS $ n $.
- $ r_m(t) $: a binary variable, equals 1 if the AoI of aircraft $ m $ resets at time $ t $.
- $ \text{AoI}_m(t) $: continuous variable for the Age of Information of aircraft $ m $ at time $ t $.

Thus, we have:

- $ d_{m,t,k} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ m \in \mathcal{M}, t \in \mathcal{T}, k \in \{0,\ldots,7\} $.
- $ b_{m,t,l} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ m \in \mathcal{M}, t \in \mathcal{T}, l \in \mathcal{L} $.
- $ s_{m,t,n} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ m \in \mathcal{M}, t \in \mathcal{T}, n \in \mathcal{N} $.
- $ r_m(t) \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ m \in \mathcal{M}, t \in \mathcal{T} $.
- $ \text{AoI}_m(t) \in [0, \text{AoI}_{\max}] $ for all $ m \in \mathcal{M}, t \in \{0,\ldots,T\} $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the combined remaining power and AoI freshness across all aircraft.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Maximize } \sum_{m=1}^{M} \left[ \frac{P^{\text{remain}}_m(T)}{P_{\max}} + \frac{\text{AoI}_{\max} - \overline{\text{AoI}}_m}{\text{AoI}_{\max}} \right]
$$

Where:

- $ P^{\text{remain}}_m(T) = P^{\text{init}} - \sum_{t=1}^{T} P_m(t) $ is the remaining power of aircraft $ m $ after $ T $ slots.
- $ \overline{\text{AoI}}_m = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=1}^{T} \text{AoI}_m(t) $ is the time-averaged AoI.
- $ P_{\max} $ is the total power budget and $ \text{AoI}_{\max} $ is the AoI threshold.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let's identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Direction Selection (one direction per aircraft per slot):**

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{7} d_{m,t,k} = 1, \quad \forall m \in \mathcal{M},\ \forall t \in \mathcal{T}
$$

2. **Position Dynamics:**

$$
x_m(t+1) = x_m(t) + \sum_{k=0}^{7} \Delta x_k \cdot d_{m,t,k}
$$

$$
y_m(t+1) = y_m(t) + \sum_{k=0}^{7} \Delta y_k \cdot d_{m,t,k}
$$

Where $ (\Delta x_k, \Delta y_k) = (v \cos(k\pi/4),\ v \sin(k\pi/4)) $.

3. **Bandwidth Level Selection (one level per aircraft per slot):**

$$
\sum_{l \in \mathcal{L}} b_{m,t,l} = 1, \quad \forall m \in \mathcal{M},\ \forall t \in \mathcal{T}
$$

4. **Serving BS Selection (one BS per aircraft per slot):**

$$
\sum_{n \in \mathcal{N}} s_{m,t,n} = 1, \quad \forall m \in \mathcal{M},\ \forall t \in \mathcal{T}
$$

5. **Bandwidth Capacity per BS:**

$$
\sum_{m=1}^{M} \beta_{m,n}(t) \le 1, \quad \forall n \in \mathcal{N},\ \forall t \in \mathcal{T}
$$

6. **AoI Reset Logic:**

The reset indicator $ r_m(t) = 1 $ iff the selected (BS, bandwidth level) pair achieves data rate $ \ge \alpha_{\max} $. This is precomputed as a binary parameter $ \text{canReset}(m,t,n,l) $ and linked via linearized product constraints.

7. **AoI Dynamics (linearized):**

$$
\text{AoI}_m(t+1) \le \text{AoI}_{\max} \cdot (1 - r_m(t))
$$

$$
\text{AoI}_m(t+1) \ge \text{AoI}_m(t) + 1 - (\text{AoI}_{\max}+1) \cdot r_m(t)
$$

8. **AoI Threshold:**

$$
\sum_{m=1}^{M} \text{AoI}_m(t) \le \text{AoI}_{\max}, \quad \forall t \in \{0,\ldots,T\}
$$

9. **Collision Avoidance (linearized via big-M):**

For each time $ t $ and each aircraft pair $ m \neq \mu $, at least one of:

$$
x_m(t) - x_\mu(t) \ge D_{\text{col}}, \quad x_\mu(t) - x_m(t) \ge D_{\text{col}}
$$
$$
y_m(t) - y_\mu(t) \ge D_{\text{col}}, \quad y_\mu(t) - y_m(t) \ge D_{\text{col}}
$$

enforced by binary indicator variables.

10. **Boundary Constraints:**

$$
0 \le x_m(t) \le 500, \quad 0 \le y_m(t) \le 500, \quad \forall m, t
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**

- $ d_{m,t,k} \in \{0,1\} $, $ b_{m,t,l} \in \{0,1\} $, $ s_{m,t,n} \in \{0,1\} $, $ r_m(t) \in \{0,1\} $.
- $ x_m(t), y_m(t) \in [0, 500] $, $ \text{AoI}_m(t) \in [0, \text{AoI}_{\max}] $.

**Objective Function:**

Maximize:

$$
\sum_{m=1}^{M} \left[ \frac{P^{\text{remain}}_m(T)}{P_{\max}} + \frac{\text{AoI}_{\max} - \overline{\text{AoI}}_m}{\text{AoI}_{\max}} \right]
$$

**Constraints:**

1. One direction per aircraft per slot.
2. Position dynamics.
3. One bandwidth level per aircraft per slot.
4. One serving BS per aircraft per slot.
5. Bandwidth capacity per BS.
6. AoI reset logic (linearized).
7. AoI dynamics (linearized).
8. AoI threshold.
9. Collision avoidance (big-M linearized).
10. Boundary constraints.

1. Optimization Problem Classification: MILP (Mixed-Integer Linear Program, with linearized AoI dynamics and collision avoidance)
2. Problem Scale: Medium (M=2 aircraft, N=4 BSs, T=10 slots, ~600 binary + 22 continuous variables)
3. Solution Method: Exact MILP solver (Gurobi) with precomputed rate feasibility and big-M linearization
