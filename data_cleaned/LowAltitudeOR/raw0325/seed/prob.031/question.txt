{
type: paper-based optimization problem
source: "A branch-price-and-cut algorithm for the time-dependent multiple truck-drone routing problem", Computers and Operations Research, Vol. 182, 2025
fullContent:

A logistics company operates a fleet of trucks, each equipped with multiple drones, to deliver parcels to customers in an urban area. Trucks travel on roads with time-dependent travel times (due to traffic congestion), while drones fly directly over the urban area with constant (time-independent) travel times. The goal is to minimize the total duration of all truck routes.

**Problem Type:** Time-Dependent Multiple Truck-Drone Routing Problem (TD-MTDRP) — a variant of the Vehicle Routing Problem with Time Windows (VRPTW), formulated as a Set-Partitioning Model (SPM), solved via Branch-Price-and-Cut (BPC).

**Problem Description:**
- A fleet of K homogeneous trucks departs from a depot (node 0) and returns to the depot (node n+1)
- Each truck carries at most m drones
- Trucks visit customer nodes and serve them directly (truck delivery)
- When a truck stops at a customer node, it simultaneously dispatches all its drones to serve nearby customers (drone delivery)
- The truck WAITS at the customer node until ALL drones return before moving to the next customer
- Each customer is served exactly once (by either truck or drone)
- Objective: minimize total duration (travel time + waiting time) of all truck routes

**Sets:**
- V = {0} ∪ N ∪ {n+1}: all nodes (depot 0, customers N, ending depot n+1)
- E = {(i,j) | i,j ∈ V}: edges for truck routes
- A = {<i,j> | i,j ∈ N}: arcs for drone routes
- K: set of homogeneous trucks
- Ω: set of all feasible truck-drone combination routes

**Parameters:**
- b_i: demand of customer i ∈ V
- s_i^t: truck service time at node i
- s_i^d: drone service time at node i
- g_i: parcel weight at node i
- [e_i, l_i]: time window (earliest/latest service start time) at node i
- Q: truck capacity
- m: maximum number of drones per truck
- B: maximum drone battery (expressed in flight time)
- D: maximum drone payload weight
- τ_{ij}(δ_i): time-dependent truck travel time on edge (i,j) when departing at time δ_i
  (modeled as piecewise linear function based on stepwise speed profile)
- t_{ij}: constant drone travel time on arc <i,j>
- t_r: truck travel time in route r ∈ Ω
- w_r: truck waiting time (including service time and waiting for drones) in route r ∈ Ω
- α_{ir}: number of times customer i is visited by truck in route r
- β_{ir}: number of times customer i is visited by drone in route r

**Decision Variables:**
- θ_r ∈ {0,1}: 1 if truck-drone route r ∈ Ω is selected in the optimal solution

**Objective Function (Set-Partitioning Model):**
Minimize total duration of all routes:

  min  Σ_{r ∈ Ω} (t_r + w_r) θ_r

**Constraints:**
1. Each customer visited exactly once (by truck or drone):
   Σ_{r ∈ Ω} (α_{ir} + β_{ir}) θ_r = 1,  ∀i ∈ N

2. Number of trucks used does not exceed fleet size:
   Σ_{r ∈ Ω} θ_r ≤ |K|

3. Binary variable definition:
   θ_r ∈ {0,1},  r ∈ Ω

**Ready Time Functions (state dynamics):**
For a truck-drone route r = (v_0, v_1(H_1), v_2(H_2), ..., v_i(H_i)):
- Truck arrival time: ρ_i^t = δ_{i-1}^t + τ_{(i-1)i}(δ_{i-1}^t)
- Drone arrival time: ρ_{ij}^d = ρ_i^t + t_{ij},  ∀h_{ij} ∈ H_i
- Drone ready time: δ_{ij}^d = ρ_j^d + s_j^d,  ∀h_{ij} ∈ H_i
- Truck ready time: δ_i^t = max(ρ_i^t + s_i^t, max_{h_{ij} ∈ H_i}(δ_{ij}^d + t_{ji}))

**Speed Profile (time-dependent truck travel):**
The planning horizon is divided into 5 time zones with different speed profiles:
- Slow:   [P0=1.0, P1=0.33, P2=0.67, P3=0.5, P4=0.83]
- Normal: [P0=1.17, P1=0.67, P2=1.33, P3=0.83, P4=1.0]
- Fast:   [P0=1.5, P1=1.67, P2=1.17, P3=1.33, P4=1.0] (relative speeds)

**Small-Scale Instance for Exact Solving:**
Depot at (50, 50). 6 customers in a 100×100 area.
- 1 truck (K=1), max 2 drones per truck (m=2)
- Truck capacity Q = 30, Drone payload limit D = 10
- Drone battery B = 20 (flight time units)
- Drone speed = 2 × truck speed (truck speed = 1 unit/time)
- Drone service time = 0.5 × truck service time

Customers:
- C1: (20, 80), demand=5,  weight=4,  s^t=2, s^d=1, window=[0, 100]
- C2: (80, 80), demand=8,  weight=12, s^t=3, s^d=1.5, window=[0, 100]  [too heavy for drone]
- C3: (20, 20), demand=6,  weight=5,  s^t=2, s^d=1, window=[0, 100]
- C4: (80, 20), demand=7,  weight=9,  s^t=3, s^d=1.5, window=[0, 100]
- C5: (50, 80), demand=4,  weight=3,  s^t=2, s^d=1, window=[0, 100]
- C6: (50, 20), demand=5,  weight=6,  s^t=2, s^d=1, window=[0, 100]

Truck travel time (Euclidean distance / speed=1, time-independent for simplicity):
  τ_{ij} = Euclidean_distance(i, j) / 1.0

Drone travel time:
  t_{ij} = Euclidean_distance(i, j) / 2.0

**Question:** Find the optimal assignment of customers to truck/drone delivery and the optimal truck route to minimize total duration, subject to capacity, battery, payload, and time window constraints.
