### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let’s define the decision variables for the problem.

Let’s denote:

- $ x_j $: a binary variable indicating if vertiport $ j $ is opened.
- $ y_i $: a binary variable indicating if demand zone $ i $ is covered.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in J $.
- $ y_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in I $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the weighted score, which balances covering demand against construction costs and noise impact.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = W_{cov} \sum_{i \in I} D_i y_i - W_{cost} \sum_{j \in J} C_j x_j - W_{noise} \sum_{j \in J} N_j x_j
$$

Where:

- $ W_{cov}, W_{cost}, W_{noise} $ are the weights for coverage, cost, and noise objectives respectively.
- $ D_i $ is the demand of zone $ i $.
- $ C_j $ is the construction cost of vertiport $ j $.
- $ N_j $ is the noise impact factor of vertiport $ j $.
- $ I $ is the set of demand zones and $ J $ is the set of candidate vertiports.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let’s identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Budget (Count) Constraint:**

The number of opened vertiports must not exceed the maximum limit $ P $.

$$
\sum_{j \in J} x_j \le P
$$

Where:

- $ P $ is the maximum number of vertiports to open.

2. **Coverage Logic Constraint:**

A zone $ i $ is considered covered ($ y_i = 1 $) only if at least one vertiport $ j $ capable of covering it (where $ a_{ij} = 1 $) is opened.

For each zone $ i \in I $:

$$
y_i \le \sum_{j \in J} a_{ij} x_j
$$

Where:

- $ a_{ij} $ is a binary parameter equal to 1 if vertiport $ j $ covers zone $ i $, and 0 otherwise.

3. **Binary Constraints:**

The decision variables must be binary.

For all $ j \in J $ and $ i \in I $:

$$
x_j \in \{0, 1\}, \quad y_i \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

**Constraints:**

1. **Budget (Count) Constraint:**

$$

For each $ i \in I $:

$$
