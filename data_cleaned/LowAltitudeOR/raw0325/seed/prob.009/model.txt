### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let’s define the decision variables for the problem.

Let’s denote:

- $ x_j $: a binary variable indicating if charging station $ j $ is opened.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in J $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the total number of charging stations opened to cover all target zones.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Minimize } Z = \sum_{j \in J} x_j
$$

Where:

- $ J $ is the set of candidate charging station sites.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let’s identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Coverage Constraint:**

Every target zone $ i $ must be covered by at least one open charging station. A station $ j $ covers target $ i $ if the distance $ d_{ij} $ is within the maximum service radius $ R_{max} $ (i.e., $ a_{ij} = 1 $).

For each target zone $ i \in I $:

$$
\sum_{j \in J} a_{ij} x_j \ge 1
$$

Where:

- $ a_{ij} $ is a binary parameter, $ a_{ij} = 1 $ if $ d_{ij} \le R_{max} $, else 0.
- $ I $ is the set of target zones.

2. **Binary Constraints:**

The decision variables must be binary.

For all $ j \in J $:

$$
x_j \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- $ x_j \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ j \in J $.

**Objective Function:**

Minimize:

$$
Z = \sum_{j \in J} x_j
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Coverage Constraint:**

For each $ i \in I $:

$$
\sum_{j \in J} a_{ij} x_j \ge 1
$$

2. **Binary Constraints:**

For all $ j \in J $:

$$
x_j \in \{0, 1\}
$$

1. Optimization Problem Classification: ILP
2. Problem Scale: Medium
