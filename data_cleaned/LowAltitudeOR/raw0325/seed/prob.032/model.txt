### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

This model is based on the Revenue Maximization (REV) variant of the Air Taxi Skyport Location Problem, formulated as a single-allocation p-hub median problem with choice-constrained elastic demand.

**Sets:**

- $ \mathcal{L} = \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\} $: set of origin zones (also candidate skyport locations).
- $ \mathcal{J} = \{A, B\} $: set of destination airports.
- $ p = 2 $: number of skyports to open (budget constraint).

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in \mathcal{L} $: equals 1 if a skyport is opened at candidate location $ k $, 0 otherwise.
- $ x_{ikj} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{L}, k \in \mathcal{L}, j \in \mathcal{J} $: equals 1 if demand at origin $ i $ for airport $ j $ is routed via skyport $ k $, 0 otherwise.

### Step 2: Parameters (Pre-computed)

**Pre-computed Parameters:**

- $ D_{ij} $: monthly demand from origin $ i $ to airport $ j $.
- $ \theta_{ikj} $: population choice probability of using air taxi from origin $ i $ to airport $ j $ via skyport $ k $, computed using the binary logit model:

$$
\theta_{ikj} = \frac{e^{V_{air}^{ikj}}}{e^{V_{air}^{ikj}} + e^{V_{ground}^{ij}}}
$$

where:

$$
V_{ground}^{ij} = 0.0313 \times c_{ij} - 0.0125 \times f_{ij}
$$

$$
V_{air}^{ikj} = 0.018 \times d_{kj} - 0.0213 \times f_{ikj}
$$

- $ f_{ij} $: direct ground taxi fare from origin $ i $ to airport $ j $.
- $ f_{ikj} = f_{ik} + t_k + f_{kj} $: total air taxi fare from $ i $ to $ j $ via skyport $ k $ (access fare + transfer cost + flight fare).
- $ f_{ik} + f_{kj} $: operator revenue per air taxi passenger (access fare + flight fare, excluding transfer cost from revenue).

### Step 3: Objective Function Construction

The objective is to maximize the total estimated air taxi revenue across all origin-skyport-airport combinations:

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{L}} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{J}} \sum_{k \in \mathcal{L}} (f_{ik} + f_{kj}) \cdot \theta_{ikj} \cdot D_{ij} \cdot x_{ikj}
$$

The term $ (f_{ik} + f_{kj}) \cdot \theta_{ikj} \cdot D_{ij} $ represents the expected revenue from passengers at origin $ i $ going to airport $ j $ via skyport $ k $. Since $ \theta_{ikj} $ and fares are pre-computed constants, the objective is linear in $ x_{ikj} $.

### Step 4: Constraint Construction

1. **Single Allocation Constraint:** Each origin-airport pair must be assigned to exactly one skyport:

For all $ i \in \mathcal{L}, j \in \mathcal{J} $:

$$
\sum_{k \in \mathcal{L}} x_{ikj} = 1
$$

2. **Route Activation (Hub Linking) Constraint:** Demand can be routed via skyport $ k $ only if $ k $ is open:

For all $ i \in \mathcal{L}, k \in \mathcal{L}, j \in \mathcal{J} $:

$$
x_{ikj} \leq y_k
$$

3. **Budget Constraint:** Exactly $ p $ skyports must be opened:

$$
\sum_{k \in \mathcal{L}} y_k = p
$$

4. **Binary Constraints:**

$$
y_k \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall k \in \mathcal{L}
$$

$$
x_{ikj} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \forall i \in \mathcal{L}, k \in \mathcal{L}, j \in \mathcal{J}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in \mathcal{L} $ (5 variables)
- $ x_{ikj} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in \mathcal{L}, k \in \mathcal{L}, j \in \mathcal{J} $ (5 × 5 × 2 = 50 variables)

Total: 55 binary variables.

**Objective Function:**

$$
\text{Maximize } Z = \sum_{i \in \mathcal{L}} \sum_{j \in \mathcal{J}} \sum_{k \in \mathcal{L}} (f_{ik} + f_{kj}) \cdot \theta_{ikj} \cdot D_{ij} \cdot x_{ikj}
$$

**Constraints:**

1. Single Allocation: $ \sum_{k} x_{ikj} = 1 $ for all $ i, j $ (10 constraints)
2. Hub Linking: $ x_{ikj} \leq y_k $ for all $ i, k, j $ (50 constraints)
3. Budget: $ \sum_{k} y_k = p $ (1 constraint)
4. Binary: $ y_k, x_{ikj} \in \{0, 1\} $

Total constraints: 61 (excluding binary domain constraints).

Classification: ILP
Scale: Medium
