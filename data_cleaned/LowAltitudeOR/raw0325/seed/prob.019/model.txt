### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let’s define the decision variables for the problem.

Let’s denote:

- $ y_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating if edge $ (i,j) $ is opened.
- $ x_{ijk} $: a continuous variable representing the flow on edge $ (i,j) $ destined for node $ k $.

Thus, we have:

- $ y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ (i,j) \in E $.
- $ x_{ijk} \ge 0 $ for all $ (i,j) \in E $ and $ k \in D $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to minimize the weighted cost, which consists of the fixed costs for opening edges (based on distance) and the variable operational costs related to the flow distance.

The objective function is:

$$
\min Z = \sum_{(i,j) \in E} d_{ij} y_{ij} + \alpha \sum_{k \in D} \sum_{(i,j) \in E} d_{ij} x_{ijk}
$$

Where:

- $ d_{ij} $ is the distance (cost) of edge $ (i,j) $.
- $ \alpha $ is the weight for operational cost.
- $ E $ is the set of candidate edges and $ D $ is the set of demand nodes.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let’s identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Topology Constraint:**

Flow can only exist on an edge if that edge is opened.

For each edge $ (i,j) \in E $:

$$
\sum_{k \in D} x_{ijk} \le M y_{ij}
$$

Where:

- $ M $ is a large constant (Big-M parameter).

2. **Flow Conservation Constraint:**

Standard network flow balance must be maintained for each demand $ k $ at every node $ i $.

For each node $ i \in N $ and demand node $ k \in D $:

$$
\sum_{j:(i,j) \in E} x_{ijk} - \sum_{j:(j,i) \in E} x_{jik} = 
\begin{cases} 
Dem_k & \text{if } i = S \\
-Dem_k & \text{if } i = k \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

Where:

- $ Dem_k $ is the demand of node $ k $.
- $ S $ is the source node.

3. **Binary and Non-negativity Constraints:**

The decision variables must adhere to their respective domains.

For all $ (i,j) \in E $ and $ k \in D $:

$$
y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad x_{ijk} \ge 0
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- $ y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ (i,j) \in E $.
- $ x_{ijk} \ge 0 $ for all $ (i,j) \in E, k \in D $.

**Objective Function:**

Minimize:

$$
\min Z = \sum_{(i,j) \in E} d_{ij} y_{ij} + \alpha \sum_{k \in D} \sum_{(i,j) \in E} d_{ij} x_{ijk}
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Topology Constraint:**

For each $ (i,j) \in E $:

$$
\sum_{k \in D} x_{ijk} \le M y_{ij}
$$

2. **Flow Conservation Constraint:**

For each $ i \in N, k \in D $:

$$
\sum_{j} x_{ijk} - \sum_{j} x_{jik} = 
\begin{cases} 
Dem_k & \text{if } i = S \\
-Dem_k & \text{if } i = k \\
0 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

3. **Binary and Non-negativity Constraints:**

$$
y_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad x_{ijk} \ge 0
$$
