Identifying the decision variables, objective function, and constraints for the Location-Routing Problem (LRP).

### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let’s define the decision variables for the problem.

Let’s denote:

- [cite_start]$ y_k $: a binary variable indicating whether depot $ k $ is open (1) or not (0)[cite: 4].
- [cite_start]$ x_{ij} $: a binary variable indicating if a UAV traverses the arc from node $ i $ to node $ j $ (1) or not (0)[cite: 5].
- [cite_start]$ u_i $: an auxiliary continuous variable for subtour elimination (MTZ)[cite: 6].

Thus, we have:

- [cite_start]$ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all candidate depots $ k \in D $[cite: 4].
- [cite_start]$ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all arcs $(i, j)$ in the set of nodes $ N $[cite: 5].
- $ u_i \ge 0 $ (implied for cumulative distance/load tracking).

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

[cite_start]The goal is to minimize the total cost, which includes the fixed opening costs for depots and the variable routing costs (distance)[cite: 7].

The objective function is:

$$
\min \sum_{k \in D} F_k y_k + \sum_{(i,j) \in A} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

Where:

- [cite_start]$ F_k $ is the fixed opening cost for depot $ k $[cite: 2].
- [cite_start]$ c_{ij} $ is the distance/cost between node $ i $ and node $ j $[cite: 3].
- $ D $ is the set of candidate depots and $ A $ is the set of arcs.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let’s identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Target Coverage Constraint:**

[cite_start]Each target point must be entered and left exactly once[cite: 7].

For each Target $ j \in T $:

$$
\sum_{i \in N} x_{ij} = 1
$$

For each Target $ i \in T $:

$$
\sum_{j \in N} x_{ji} = 1
$$

[cite_start][cite: 8]

2. **Depot Logic & Flow Conservation Constraint:**

Routes must start and end at the same open depot. [cite_start]If a route leaves depot $ k $, then depot $ k $ must be open[cite: 9].

For each Depot $ k \in D $:

$$
\sum_{j \in T} x_{kj} = \sum_{j \in T} x_{jk} \le M y_k
$$

Where:
- [cite_start]$ M $ is a Big-M parameter[cite: 3].
- $ T $ is the set of Target points.

3. **Subtour Elimination & Connectivity (MTZ) Constraint:**

[cite_start]Ensure valid loops connected to depots and eliminate subtours[cite: 9].

For arcs connecting targets:

$$
u_j - u_i \ge c_{ij} - M(1 - x_{ij})
$$

[cite_start][cite: 10]

4. **Range Constraint:**

The total distance of any specific route must be less than or equal to the maximum UAV range $ L $. [cite_start]Note: In the simplified logic provided, this is checked by tracking cumulative distance via $ u_i $[cite: 3, 11].

5. **Binary Constraints:**

The decision variables must satisfy their binary nature.

$$
x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\}, \quad y_k \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- [cite_start]$ y_k \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ k \in D $[cite: 4].
- [cite_start]$ x_{ij} \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ (i,j) \in A $[cite: 5].
- [cite_start]$ u_i $ auxiliary variables[cite: 6].

**Objective Function:**

[cite_start]Minimize Total Cost[cite: 7]:

$$
\min \sum_{k \in D} F_k y_k + \sum_{(i,j) \in A} c_{ij} x_{ij}
$$

**Constraints:**

1. **Target Coverage Constraint:**

[cite_start]For each Target $ j \in T $ (entry) and $ i \in T $ (exit)[cite: 8]:

$$
\sum_{i \in N} x_{ij} = 1, \quad \sum_{j \in N} x_{ji} = 1
$$

2. **Depot Logic Constraint:**

[cite_start]For each Depot $ k \in D $[cite: 9]:

$$
\sum_{j \in T} x_{kj} = \sum_{j \in T} x_{jk} \le M y_k
$$

3. **Subtour Elimination Constraint:**

$$
u_j - u_i \ge c_{ij} - M(1 - x_{ij})
$$
