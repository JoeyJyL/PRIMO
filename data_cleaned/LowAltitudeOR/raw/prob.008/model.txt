### Step 1: Decision Variables Definition

Let’s define the decision variables for the problem.

Let’s denote:

- $ x_i $: a binary variable indicating if vertiport $ i $ is opened.
- $ y_r $: a binary variable indicating if RS route $ r $ is active (served).
- $ w_z $: a binary variable indicating if ODM zone $ z $ is served.

Thus, we have:

- $ x_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in V $.
- $ y_r \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ r \in R $.
- $ w_z \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ z \in Z $.

### Step 2: Objective Function Construction

The goal is to maximize the total served demand, considering both the RS routes and the ODM zones.

The objective function is:

$$
\text{Maximize } \sum_{r \in R} D_r^{RS} y_r + \sum_{z \in Z} D_z^{ODM} w_z
$$

Where:

- $ D_r^{RS} $ is the demand of RS route $ r $.
- $ D_z^{ODM} $ is the demand of ODM zone $ z $.
- $ R $ is the set of RS routes and $ Z $ is the set of ODM zones.

### Step 3: Constraint Construction

Now, let’s identify and translate the constraints into mathematical expressions.

1. **Budget Constraint:**

The total construction cost of all opened vertiports must not exceed the total budget.

$$
\sum_{i \in V} C_i x_i \le B
$$

Where:

- $ C_i $ is the construction cost of vertiport $ i $.
- $ B $ is the total budget.
- $ V $ is the set of candidate vertiports.

2. **RS Route Logic (AND condition):**

A route $ r $ can be active only if both its origin vertiport $ o_r $ and destination vertiport $ d_r $ are opened.

For each route $ r \in R $:

$$
y_r \le x_{o_r}
$$

$$
y_r \le x_{d_r}
$$

3. **ODM Zone Logic (Coverage):**

An ODM zone $ z $ is served only if its associated vertiport $ v_z $ is opened.

For each zone $ z \in Z $:

$$
w_z \le x_{v_z}
$$

4. **Binary Constraints:**

The decision variables must be binary, indicating a yes/no decision.

For all $ i \in V, r \in R, z \in Z $:

$$
x_i, y_r, w_z \in \{0, 1\}
$$

### Summary of the Mathematical Model

The mathematical model for the problem is as follows:

**Decision Variables:**

- $ x_i \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ i \in V $.

- $ w_z \in \{0, 1\} $ for all $ z \in Z $.

Maximize:
