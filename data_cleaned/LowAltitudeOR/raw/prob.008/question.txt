
The Beijing Municipal Transport Commission is planning the first phase of the Urban Air Mobility (UAM) network using eVTOL aircraft.
**Goal:** Select locations to build Vertiports to maximize the total served passenger demand, considering two distinct types of service:
1. **Regular Shuttle (RS):** Fixed routes between key hubs. A route is active only if **BOTH** its origin and destination have an open Vertiport.
2. **On-Demand Mobility (ODM):** Flexible taxi-like service within specific zones. A zone is served if **AT LEAST ONE** Vertiport is opened within or adjacent to it (simplification for coverage).

**Constraints:**
- **Budget:** Total construction cost cannot exceed **25M**.
- **Candidate Sites:** 3 potential locations.

**Candidate Vertiports (Cost & Location):**
- **V1 (CBD - Guomao):** Cost 10M | High ODM potential.
- **V2 (PEK - Capital Airport):** Cost 15M | Key endpoint for Shuttle.
- **V3 (PKX - Daxing Airport):** Cost 12M | Key endpoint for Shuttle.

**Demand Data:**
*RS Routes (Requires both endpoints Open):*
- **Route A (CBD <-> PEK):** V1 AND V2 must be open. Demand: **100** passengers/day.
- **Route B (CBD <-> PKX):** V1 AND V3 must be open. Demand: **110** passengers/day.

*ODM Zones (Requires specific site Open):*
- **Zone 1 (CBD Area):** Covered if V1 open. Demand: **20**.
- **Zone 2 (PEK Area):** Covered if V2 open. Demand: **30**.
- **Zone 3 (PKX Area):** Covered if V3 open. Demand: **30**.

**Analysis:**
- Combination V1+V2: Cost 25M (Feasible). Covers Route A (100) + Zone 1(20) + Zone 2(30). Total = 150.
- Combination V1+V3: Cost 22M (Feasible). Covers Route B (110) + Zone 1(20) + Zone 3(30). Total = 160.
- Combination V2+V3: Cost 27M (Infeasible, exceeds budget).
