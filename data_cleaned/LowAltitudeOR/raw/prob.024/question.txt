"SeoulAir" operates a UAM network connecting the City Center, a Residential District, and the Airport.
**Problem:** Determine the optimal **Fleet Size**, **Vertiport Capacity** (Parking Spaces), and **Relocation Operations** to serve daily demand.
**Methodology:**
- Decompose the day into **2 Steady-State Time Windows**:
  - **T1 (AM Peak):** Commuters fly Residential → City.
  - **T2 (PM Peak):** Commuters fly City → Residential.
- For each window, determine the optimal flows (Service + Relocation) to balance the network.
- **Global Planning:** The required infrastructure is the **Maximum** needed across all time windows.

**Data - Vertiports:**
- **V1 (Residential):** High Outflow in AM.
- **V2 (City Center):** High Inflow in AM.
- **V3 (Airport):** Constant low demand.

**Demand Patterns (Trips/hr):**
*T1 (AM Peak):*
- V1 → V2: 20 trips/hr.
- V3 → V2: 5 trips/hr.
*T2 (PM Peak):*
- V2 → V1: 20 trips/hr.
- V2 → V3: 5 trips/hr.

**Operational Parameters:**
- **Flight Time:** 0.5 hours (all links).
- **Charging Time:** 0.5 hours (required after every flight).
- **Safety Buffer:** At least **1 idle drone** required at each vertiport at all times.

**Cost Parameters:**
- **Fleet Cost:** $100 per drone.
- **Parking Space Cost:** $20 per space.
- **Relocation Flight Cost:** $5 per flight (energy/wear).

**Objective:**
Minimize Total Cost = (Fleet Cost) + (Parking Space Cost) + (Relocation Cost).

**Expected Analysis:**
- In AM Peak: V2 receives 25 drones/hr but sends 0 → needs relocation V2→V1 (20) and V2→V3 (5).
- In PM Peak: V2 sends 25 drones/hr but receives 0 → needs relocation V1→V2 (20) and V3→V2 (5).
- Total relocation per period: 25 flights.
- Fleet sizing: (25 passenger + 25 relocation) × (0.5 + 0.5) + 3 idle = 53 drones.
